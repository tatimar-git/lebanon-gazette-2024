The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 4 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 5 – Building a baby crib.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
The candidate should be able to read and understand woodworking

Cut the wood panels drawings and cut list and read measurements. The candidate should be able mark lines to measure on the wood board to cut pieces to size and know how to operate a table saw. ☐ ☐
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: dowel joints The candidate should know how to mark the location of the mortise and tenon joint on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to drill dowels using a spindle drill bit and a dowel jig, then dry fit the joint and adjust it if needed. ☐ ☐
Apply finishing technique: Wood filler and sanding The candidate should know how to choose the color of the wood filler, how to apply it and then sand the access of the filler then sand the whole surface to prepare it to be upholstered. ☐ ☐
Assemble the pieces The candidate should know how assemble the bed by gluing and joining the doweled pieces then measure with dowels and mark drilling holes on the wood pieces using a pencil, a measure tape, a square and a spirit level before fixing legs and platform. The candidate should know how to glue and drive screws in place to assemble all pieces to the crib and sand finish if needed. ☐ ☐
