١٧٦٣
Mount the side rails to the main posts using rail brackets (beds hangers):
- Place the brackets on the rails and posts and trace the edges
- Use a centred punch to indicate the holes of these brackets
- Remove brackets
- Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood
- Drill pilot holes into all the marked points using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
- Glue the back of the bracket with roc glue
- Position the bracket back in place between the previously traced edges
- Let sit for few minutes
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into these holes using your drill until the screw is flush with the brackets
