Cut the wood panels
Interpret and carefully follow the cut list:
The cabinet maker should be aware of the outer size of the cabinet he is making to get an accurate size at the end of this building process. Therefore, the cabinet maker should keep a side note of the outer dimensions:
- Single bed height: 104 cm head, 65 cm foot
- Single bed width: 100 cm
- Single bed length: 202cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using a 30mm thickness board
- Mark 1 headboard: length = 104 cm, width= 100 cm, thickness 3 cm
- Mark 1 footboard: length = 65 cm, width= 100 cm, thickness 3 cm
- Mark 2 bed rails: length= 196 cm, width= 15 cm thickness 3 cm
- Mark 1 side support battens: length= 94 cm, width=6 cm, thickness 3 cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using 18 mm MDF board
Mark 1 mattress board: length= 198 cm, width= 104 cm
Lay out carefully how you will cut every piece from the sheets of MDF: where to start and which pieces to cut first
- Mark an "x" on the waste side of the board. This will help position the blade
Prepare your table saw to start cutting:
- Make sure the blade is set to a true 90-degree angle to the bed/plate of the tool
- Lower the blade (with the power off) and position it so there's the thickness of a hair between the teeth and the line
- Adjust the width of the fence that you need to cut
- Adjust the blade so it's higher than the thickness of the wood
- Make sure the edge of the blade is on the outside edge of the cut line, so the full amount
