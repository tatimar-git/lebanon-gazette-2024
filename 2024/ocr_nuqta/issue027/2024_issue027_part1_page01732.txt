COMPETENCE STANDARD

ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, bed-makers, and related trades workers ISCO-08-7522 Bed-makers and related workers | Competence Standard Beds Making
EQF level - 3
A competent person should be able to fabricate cabinets: cut, fit, join, assemble, and finish prepared wooden parts to make cabinets, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.

Competence Ability / Learning Outcome Knowledge


- Different types of hazards related to a workshop site and the measures to minimize the effect or the occurrence
- Type and use of Personal Protection Equipment and first aid kit
- Carpentry terminology for operations, tools, and materials
- Blueprints interpretation
- Different types of wood methods for storing
- Drawings and installation manual to build and assemble a base cabinet
- Use of machines to cut particle pine wood
- Use of lacquer to finish the wood pieces
- Use of simple hardware connectors and screws
- Use of tools, materials to assemble all components together
- Drawings and installation manual to fix accessories
- Use of machines to cut MDF
- Use of finishing sander to finish the wood pieces
- Use of mortise and tenon connectors
- Use of tools, materials to assemble all components together

CU1 Setting up the working place | • Use PPE to prevent risks of personal injuries • Organize working space and equipment, tools, consumables, and materials • Identify hazards and risks and take preventive measures • Provide first aid in case of personal injury • Interpret technical drawings and blueprints
CU2 Building a bunk bed | • Cut the wood panels • Apply finishing technique: edge Sanding • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the ladder: centered lap joint and screws • Assemble the pieces
CU3 Building a single bed | • Cut the wood panels • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the base support frame: screws • Apply finishing technique: sanding • Assemble the pieces
