Identify hazards and risks and take preventive measures
- Take safety measures to prevent hazards of injuries:
OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY STANDARDS
- Take a walk along the intended exit pathway and remove any obstacles that might get in your way
- Keep space clutter free and tripping free by removing (disposing and/or storing) any disorganized clutter that might jeopardize moving safely
- Abide by housekeeping procedures at the end of every working day
- Install valid fire extinguishers, fire alarm and first aid kits
- Use the deadlift technique to avoid injury when carrying anything
- Take breaks at regular intervals and when needed
- Read carefully and abide by operating instructions on tools and power machine labels
- Turn off machines while not in use and before moving, cleaning, adjusting, oiling or unjamming
- Report workplace accidents or safety incidents to supervisor
Provide first aid in case of personal injury
- Provide a first aid kit that should always be available at the workshop:
- Plasters and band aids
- Sterile gauze and eye dressings
- Bandages
- Safety pins
Disposable sterile gloves
Tweezers
Take the following general steps in case of injury:
- Place your hand in front of the injured person mouth to check the breathing. Place two fingers on the injured person neck below the ear to check heartbeats (normal is 60-100 beats per minute). If necessary, begin rescue breathing and Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR)
- If the person's breathing and heart rate are normal but the person is unconscious, treat the person as if there is a spinal injury. Stabilize the head and neck by placing your hands on both sides of the person's head. Keep the head in line with the spine and prevent movement. Call for medical help
