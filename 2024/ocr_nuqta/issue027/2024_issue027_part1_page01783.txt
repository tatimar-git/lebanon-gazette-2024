١٧٨٣
Drill pilot holes on the legs:
- Use a punch tool to scribe the marks made previously
- Drill a pilot hole through the leg: each side of a corner and each single leg should have 2 holes, so the total drilled holes are 20 holes
Glue legs corners and single center legs to the rails:
- Apply PVA glue to the legs on the side to be glued
- Position the legs on the previously drawn line across the rails: the top edge of the legs should be flush with the line
- Make sure the legs are flush to the line using a spirit level
- Clamp tightly so it does not move and let dry
- Fix legs to the rails with screws
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into all the drilled pilot holes using your drill until the screw is flush with the legs surface
Fix the crib platform to the legs:
- Apply a small amount of glue on the top edges of the legs
- Position the cribs platform on top of the legs
- Drive screws into the comers moving 1 cm form the corner edge to fix the platform to the legs
