Drill pilot holes on the support battens and rails:
- Place the slats support battens on the inside side of the rails let the bottom edge of the batten be flush with the bottom edge of the rails
- Use a level and pencil to trace the edges of the batten on the rail and make sure its level
- Remove the battens
Mark drilling holes with a pencil at the horizontal centre of the traced batten: mark a hole every 30.8 cm in order to mark 4 holes and mark 2 holes at both ends moving 5 cm from the ends
Take the same measurements on the rail and transfer the same points to the batten board: marks should be levelled and compatible with the marks on the rails
Use a punch tool to scribe the marked holes
Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood
Drill pilot holes on the rails using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
Remove masking tape off the drill bit
Drill a pilot hole through the battens where the marks are placed
Glue the slats support battens to the side rails:
Repeat the same cleaning process for glue mentioned above
Spray vertically contact adhesive spray on the rails where they will meet the supports
Spray horizontally contact adhesive spray on support boards
Position the battens on the previously traced marks on the rails
Make sure the board is levelled and square using a spirit level
Clamp tightly so it does not move and let dry
Fix battens to the rails with screws:
Drive coarse thread wood screws into all the pilot holes using your drill until the screw is flush on the side of the battens
Fix slats to ballens:
- Glue slats separators blocks to the battens repeating the same cleaning and gluing process mentioned previously: using a measure tape and a level place a separator block every 8 cm on the batten
- Place slats between the block separators on the lower and upper beds
Fix guardrails:
- Glue the 2 guardrails to the main post fitting them between the 3 block separators: use the gluing technique mentioned previously
Assemble ladder:
- Glue the ladder sideboard by applying glue inside the laps and fixing to the rails: use the gluing technique mentioned previously
- Clamp the ladder sideboard and the guardrails together to get a tight fix
- Let dry
- Make sure the board is levelled and square using a spirit level
Fix ladder to bed:
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into the predrilled holes on the ladders sideboard and the guardrails to fix them together using your drill until the screw is flush with the drilled surface
- Position ladder steps to the previously traced edges on the outer post and side ladder board
- Fix the steps with clamps tightly
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into the predrilled holes on the ladder's sideboard,
outer post and the ladders steps to fix them together using your drill until the screw is flush with the drilled surface
