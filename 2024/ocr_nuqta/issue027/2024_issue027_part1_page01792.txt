ASSESSMENT STANDARDS - UNIT 6
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee: | Date:
Assessor:


Ability / learning outcome Criteria for competence unit 6 – Housekeeping and reporting YES / NO


Clean the tools and put back in place Are tools vacuumed from dust before cleaning?
Is the excess of grease removed from tools?
Is rust removed from the tools?
Are tools and machines lubricated?
Are saws and tools sharpened?
Are saws aligned?
Are tools protected from rusting?
Are tools placed in the planned place?
Are working tables cleaned before leaving the working place?
Are combustible materials stored in approved containers?
Are any worn, ripped, or damaged flooring that poses a tripping hazard replaced?


Clean surfaces Is wall paint kept clean to highlight railings, guards, and other safety equipment?
Are walkways kept free from debris and any trip hazards?
Are aisles, stairways, emergency exits, electrical panels and doors cleared of clutter, and untidy areas purged?
Are spills cleaned such as oil on floors immediately?
Are floor areas sweep on a regular basis to remove debris and dust using detergents and cleaning tools?
Dispose of the waste Are scrap containers placed near where the waste is produced?
Are all waste receptacles labeled?
Are all waste containers emptied regularly?
