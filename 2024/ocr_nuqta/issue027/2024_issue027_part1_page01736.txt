EDUCATION STANDARD

ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, bed-makers, and related trades workers | Competence Standard
ISCO-08-7522 Bed-makers and related workers | Beds Making
EQF level - 3
Overview | At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to fabricate wooden boxes and frames, and fit, join, assemble and finish prepared wooden parts to make a bed, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.
Competences | CU1. Setting up the working place

CU2. Building a bunk bed
CU3. Building a single bed
CU4. Building a lift bed with storage
CU5. Building a baby crib
CU6. Housekeeping and reporting
Abilities / Learning Outcomes • Use different engineered wood types
• Use several joining techniques
• Use and chose several finishing techniques
Knowledge • Engineered wood types
• Wood finishing techniques
• Joining techniques
Learning program During the classroom lessons the following topics will be covered:
1. Best uses of different engineered wood types, advantages, disadvantages
2. Best wood finishing techniques according to wood type
3. Best joining techniques according to wood and furniture type


During the workshop lessons the following practical activities will be execute:
1. Cutting wood using various machines and hand tools
2. Using several joining techniques according to wood and furniture type
3. Using fishing techniques according to wood and furniture type
During on the job training the following job activities will be carried out
1. Cutting wood pieces
2. Joining wood pieces
3. Finishing wood pieces
4. Assembling wood pieces to obtain piece of furniture
