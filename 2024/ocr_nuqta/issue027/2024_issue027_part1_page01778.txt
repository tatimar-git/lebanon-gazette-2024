The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 4 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 4 – Building a lift bed with storage.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Cut the wood panels | The candidate should be able to read and understand woodworking drawings and cut list and read measurements. The candidate should be

able mark lines to measure on the wood board to cut pieces to size and know how to operate a table saw.
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: faux tenon The candidate should know how to mark the location of the mortise on both jointing sides on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to cut the mortise joint using a mortising machine, then dry fit the joint and adjust it if needed. ☐ ☐
Assemble the pieces The candidate should know how to fix the rails to the headboard and footboard to form the bed frame by fixing the domino joints with glue and them mark the location of pilot holes on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to fix the cleats to the rails with screws and PVA glue. ☐ ☐
Prepare and install lifting mechanism to bed platform The candidate should know how assemble dry fit a bed lift mechanism first temporarily to check fit and operation then fix it to the bed rails and bed platform and operate carefully. ☐ ☐
