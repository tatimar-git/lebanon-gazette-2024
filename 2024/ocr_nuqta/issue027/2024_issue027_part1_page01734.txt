ASSESSMENT STANDARD

ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, bed-makers, and related trades workers ISCO-08-7522 Bed-makers and related workers | Competence Standard Beds Making
EQF level - 3
A competent person should be able to fabricate wooden boxes and frames: cut, fit, join, assemble, and finish prepared wooden parts to make a bed, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.
Competence unit 1 | Setting up the working place
Assessment criteria | The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the

abilities / learning outcomes related to CU1 in less than 2 hours.
Assessment procedure and indicators Demonstrate ability to:
YES/NO Use PPE to prevent risks of personal injuries
YES/NO Organize working space and equipment, tools, consumables, and materials
YES/NO Identify hazards and risks and take preventive measures
YES/NO Provide first aid in case of personal injury

YES/NO Interpret technical drawings and blueprints

Competence unit 2 Building a bunk bed
Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to CU2 in less than 8 hours.
Assessment procedure and indicators Demonstrate ability to:
YES/NO Cut the wood panels
YES/NO Apply finishing technique: sanding
YES/NO Prepare and apply the joining technique for bed frame: connectors hardwares & screws


YES/NO Prepare and apply the joining technique for the ladder, centered lap joint and screws
YES/NO Assemble the pieces

Competence unit 3 Building a single bed
Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to CU3 in less than 8 hours.
