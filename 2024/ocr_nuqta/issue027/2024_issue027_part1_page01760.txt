The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 5 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 – Building a bunk bed.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Cut the wood panels | The candidate should be able to read and understand woodworking drawings and cut list and read measurements. The candidate should be able mark lines to measure on the wood board to cut pieces to size and

know how to operate a table saw.
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws The candidate should know how to mark the location of pilot holes on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil The candidate should know how to fix bed rail brackets to join the pieces. ☐ ☐
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the ladder and guardrails: centered lap joint and screws The candidate should know how to mark the location of lap joint on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to use a band saw, to cut lap joints, how to dry fit the joints and how to fix them with glue, in addition to preparing wood pieces to be screwed by drilling pilot holes. ☐ ☐
Apply finishing technique: sanding The candidate should know how sand the surface of the stool and clean all the surface to remove dust before sealing. The candidate should know how to seal and prime the stool to prepare it to the spray-painting technique. ☐ ☐
Assemble the pieces The candidate should know how to measure and mark drilling holes on the wood pieces using a pencil, a measure tape, a square and a spirit level before fixing the ladder, the guardrails, the battens, and the slats to the bed. The candidate should know how to glue and drive screws in place to assemble all pieces to the bed. ☐ ☐
