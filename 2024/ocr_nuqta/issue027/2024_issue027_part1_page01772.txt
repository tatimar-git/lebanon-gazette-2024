Interpret and carefully follow the cut list:
The cabinet maker should be aware of the outer size of the cabinet he is making in order to get an accurate size at the end of this building process. Therefore, the cabinet maker should keep a side note of the outer dimensions:
- Lift bed height: 100 cm head, 30cm foot
- Lift bed width: 100 cm
- Lift bed length: 200 cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using an 18 mm thickness board:
- Mark 2 head and foot boards: width=30cm, length = 96.4 cm
- Mark 2 bed rails: width= 30cm, length= 196 cm
- Mark 1 bed platform: length= 200 cm, width= 100 cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using a 2.5 mm thickness board:
Mark 2 length =96.4 cm thickness 2.5 cm, width=10cm
Lay out carefully how you will cut every piece from the sheets of MDF: where to start and which pieces to cut first
- Mark an "x" on the waste side of the board. This will help position the blade
Prepare your table saw to start cutting:
put a compatible blade when you need to cut the pine wood
- Make sure the blade is set to a true 90-degree angle to the bed/plate of the tool
- Lower the blade (with the power off) and position it so there's the thickness of a hair between the teeth and the line
- Adjust the width of the fence that you need to cut
