Mark the hole where you want to drill your screws on the outer side of the main posts
Place the top head and foot board in a way that the lower point of the chamfered edge is 3.8 cm below the top of the post
Centre the top headboard to the post and trace the edges
Draw a vertical line at the centre of the traced edges
Mark two points on that line for the pilot holes moving 2.5 cm from the centre
Take the same measurements on the post and transfer the same points to the top board moving 2.5 cm from the centre: marks should be levelled and compatible with the marks on the post
Use a punch tool to scribe the marked holes
Repeat the same process for all the head and foot bottom and centre boards and the posts
Drill a pilot hole at the edge of the boards where the marks are placed:
Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood
Drill pilot holes using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
Remove masking tape of the drill bit
Drill a pilot hole through the edge of the posts where the marks are placed
Position the board on the previously traced marks on the post clamp tightly so it does not move
Make sure the board is levelled and square using a spirit level

- Drive coarse thread wood screws into these holes using your drill until the screw is flush on the side of the post

Glue outer posts to main posts:
Clean MDF before gluing:
- Pour 1/4 cup dishwashing liquid into a quart-size spray bottle and fill the rest of the bottle with warm water and shake the bottle to mix the contents
- Spray the cleaning mixture directly onto the MDF surface be careful not to get the cleaning solution on exposed edges of particle board
- Wash the section to be glued with a sponge and rinse with a sponge and cool water
- Dry the MDF with a towel
- Apply rubbing alcohol to the end of a soft rag and rub the area to be glued with the rubbing alcohol
- Allow the rubbing alcohol to dry completely
Attach the main posts to the outer posts:
- Spray vertically contact adhesive spray on the upper outer post on the side to be glued
