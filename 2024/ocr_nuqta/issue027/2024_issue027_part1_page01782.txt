Sweep the hand sander back and forth along the MDF, working with the grain: do not apply any pressure; the weight of the hand sander is enough
Wipe down the MDF with a tack cloth to remove all the sanding grit
Go over the MDF again with a 120-grit sandpaper disk and wipe it down thoroughly: always work in the direction of the grain, never against it because this can cause it to chip
Finish the MDF by going over it with the hand sander fitted with 220-grit or finer sandpaper
Wipe the MDF down with a tack cloth and it should be polished enough to take a finish coat

Assemble all pieces
Assemble back and front boards to the side boards:
Glue dowels:
- Apply a layer of glue before inserting the dowels inside the drilled holes on the surface of the back and front rails
- Place the dowels in the drilled-out holes
- Try to join the pieces together when the glue has set, without glue on the other doveled side to make sure the fit is right again
- Apply glue into the holes of the end grained edges of the side's rails
- Join two doveled sides to form a corner: you should get 4 joined corners at the end
- Use a mallet to lightly drive the pieces in
- Secure them with a clamp
- Let dry over night
Fix legs to the bottom rails of the crib:
Mark pilot holes on the rails:
- Mark 4 point on each rail moving 2 cm from the top edge of the rail: this is a guideline for the top edge of the legs to be placed on
- Mark 4 point on each rail moving 3 cm down from the previously drawn line: mark 2 points at both ends moving 3.25 cm from the edges and 2 other points moving 63.2 cm from both ends
- Mark 4 point on each rail moving 3 cm down from the previously drawn line: mark 2 points at both ends moving 3.25 cm from the top edge and 2 other points moving 63.2 cm from both ends
Connect the 4 marked point across the bottom bed rails by drawing a line using a square and a pencil
Mark pilot holes on the legs:
- Mark 2 holes on each corner side and each single leg, moving 2.55 cm form the side ends and 3.25 cm from the top edge
Drill pilot holes on the bottom rails:
- Use a punch tool to scribe the marks made previously
- Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood: set the depth for 1.2 cm
- Drill pilot holes on the rails using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws: the total drilled holes are 20 holes
- Remove masking tape off the drill bit
