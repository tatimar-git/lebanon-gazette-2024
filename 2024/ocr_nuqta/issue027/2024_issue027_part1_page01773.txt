- Adjust the blade so it's higher than the thickness of the wood
- Make sure the edge of the blade is on the outside edge of the cut line, so the full amount measured is left behind
- Switch the power on
Make the cut:
- Use a push shoe, push stick, or splitter when cutting thin boards
- Let the blade come to a full stop before raising the blade. To prevent removing a small amount of wood which can change the length of our cut
- Measure the work piece after cutting
- Make sure pieces are square and cut at perfect 90-degree angles
- Adjust the cut if necessary
- Pull back the board and turn off the saw
- Label the cut pieces as you go to make it easier to assemble later
- Check all cut pieces for square by measuring from the top right corner across the piece to the lower left corner
- Note the measurement, then measure the opposite two comers. If the measurements are the same, your piece is square
- Sand lightly by hand pieces' edges if needed using a 320 to 400 grit sandpaper
- Put all the cut boards aside
- Store disposable scrap pieces in designated buckets
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: butterfly connectors
- Choose a drill bit for wood with a size compatible with screws
- The wood screws would lock together the two-wood board
- At first use low speed to make sure the drill will not slip from position
- Use 4 clamps and a square wooden post
- Drill the holes in the wood screws using an electric screwdriver
- Assemble the bed frame with butterfly connectors to more stability
Prepare and install lifting mechanism to bed platform
- Chose a lift mechanism that is compatible with the weight of the platform board and mattress
- Follow the instructions of the lift up kit to install it at the right spot according to sizes
- Position lower bracket on the inside face of the cleat
- Mark with a pencil the screw holes of the lower bracket on the inside face of the cleat
Make sure the bracket is square and well levelled
Drill the marked holes on the cleats with pilot drill
Position the lower bracket in place
Attach the bracket temporarly with screws
Attach rod end of spring to lower bracket
Repeat the same process to the opposite side
Position the platform board on the bed frame and lift it up with the help of some lumber: make sure the edges of the platform and the frame are flush and square
Lift it up the rod spring and mark where it touches the platform board to determine where the upper bracket will be positioned
Install the upper bracket temporarly at the marked location with screws
Repeat the same process to the opposite side using the same previous dimensions as a reference
Connect the spring to the upper bracket assembly on both sides of the bed
Install mattress and all bedding
Carefully operate the bed lift: if the Bed lift operates correctly, finish installing the brackets, if not, adjust your fittings
Remove the brackets: while the spring rod is attached
Apply rod glue to brackets and position in place
Fix brackets through bolts and washers while the spring rod is attached
