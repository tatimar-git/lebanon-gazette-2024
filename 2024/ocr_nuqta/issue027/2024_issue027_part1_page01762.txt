Switch the power on
Make the cut:
Use a push shoe, push stick, or splitter when cutting thin boards
Let the blade come to a full stop before raising the blade. To prevent removing a small amount of wood which can change the length of our cut
image here
- Measure the work piece after cutting
- Make sure pieces are square and cut at perfect 90-degree angles
- Adjust the cut if necessary
- Pull back the board and turn off the saw
- Label the cut pieces as you go to make it easier to assemble later
- Check all cut pieces for square by measuring from the top right corner across the piece to the lower left corner
- Note the measurement, then measure the opposite two corners. If the measurements are the same, your piece is square
- Sand lightly by hand pieces' edges if needed using a 120 to 320 grit sandpaper
- Put all the cut boards aside
- Store disposable scrap pieces in designated buckets

Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws
Screw head and foot top, bottom, and centre boards to the main posts:
- Mark the hole where you want to drill your screws on the outer side of the main posts
- Place the top head and foot board in a way that the lower point of the edge is 3.8 cm below the top of the post
- Centre the top headboard to the post and trace the edges
- Draw a vertical line at the centre of the traced edges
- Mark two points on that line for the pilot holes moving 2.5 cm from the centre
- Take the same measurements on the post and transfer the same points to the top board
moving 2.5 cm from the centre: marks should be levelled and compatible with the marks on the post
- Use a punch tool to scribe the marked holes
- Repeat the same process for all the head and foot bottom
- Drill a pilot hole at the edge of the boards where the marks are placed:
- Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood
- Drill pilot holes using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
- Remove masking tape of the drill bit
- Drill a pilot hole through the edge of the posts where the marks are placed
- Position the board on the previously traced marks on the post clamp tightly so it does not move
- Make sure the board is levelled and square using a spirit level
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into these holes using your drill until the screw is flush on the side of the post
- Repeat the same process to all the head and foot
