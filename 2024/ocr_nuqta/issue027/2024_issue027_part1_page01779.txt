Interpret and carefully follow the cut list:
The cabinet maker should be aware of the outer size of the cabinet he is making in order to get an accurate size at the end of this building process. Therefore, the cabinet maker should keep a side note of the coter dimensions:
- Baby crib back height: 85cm, front height: 85 cm
- Baby crib width: 76 cm
- Baby crib length: 134.5 cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using an 18 mm thickness board:
- Mark 4 front and back rails: length= 131.5 cm, width= 8 cm
- Mark 4 side rails: length= 76 cm, width= 8 cm
Mark 4 legs width =8 cm length =85
Mark 20 legs width =8 cm length=64 cm
Mark with a pencil on the side you need for your project the cutting lines of the pieces to size accordingly using a 6 mm thickness board:
Mark 2 front and back board width =62 cm length = 66 cm
Lay out carefully how you will cut every piece from the sheets of MDF: where to start and which pieces to cut first:
Mark an “x” on the waste side of the board. This will help position the blade
Prepare your table saw to start cutting:
Make sure the blade is set to a true 90-degree angle to the bed/plate of the tool
Lower the blade (with the power off) and position it so there's the thickness of a hair between the teeth and the line
Adjust the width of the fence that you need to cut
Adjust the blade so it's higher than the thickness of the wood
