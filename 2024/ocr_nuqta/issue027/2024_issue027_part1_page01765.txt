Attach rails to the headboard:
- Find the small holes or grooves near the base of your headboard
- There should be an equal amount on the left and right side
- Attach one of your bed frame's side rails to each side of the headboard, making sure the finished side of each rail points out
Some side rails will simply fall into place, though others may need to be secured with included screws and nuts
Attach rails to the footboard:
Connect the baseboard to the side rails.
For most wooden bed frames, you can attach the baseboard using the exact same method as the headboard
However, since your baseboard is shorter than the headboard, the grooves or holes may look slightly different and any screws or nuts necessary to secure the baseboard may be smaller
Make sure the unfinished side of your frame points in
Fix the mattress board to the support battens:
Drill pilot holes on the upper surface of support battens:
Mark drilling holes with a pencil: move 10 cm from the end sides of the battens at the 4 corners of the bed in order to get 4 holes
Take the same measurements on the battens and transfer the same points to the mattress board: marks should be levelled and compatible with the marks on the rails
Use a punch tool to scribe the marked holes
Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going to deep into the wood
Drill pilot holes on the battens using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
Remove masking tape off the drill bit

- Fix the mattress board to the battens with screws:
- Drive coarse thread wood screws into all the pilot holes using your drill until the screw is flush on the side of mattress board
- Sand the MDF board to avoid splinters with 320 grit sandpaper
