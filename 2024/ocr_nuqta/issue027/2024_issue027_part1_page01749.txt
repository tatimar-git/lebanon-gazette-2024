Mark the block separators you want to cut:
- Use a square and a pencil to draw a straight line where you want to cut
- Mark lightly 3 blocks separators support for the guardrails of 5 cm length on the 17 cm pre-cut board: take the width of the miter saw blade (0.3) into account when measuring super-precise cuts, therefore let there be 0.4 cm between each block to get precise cuts
- Mark lightly 11 blocks separators for the slats of 8 cm length on the 93 cm pre-cut board: take the width of the miter saw blade (0.3) into account when measuring super-precise cuts, therefore let there be 0.4 cm between each block to get precise cuts
Prepare the miter saw to crosscut:
- Place the safety miter saw guard and tighten until secure
- Set the miter and bevel to 0 degrees to make straight 90°crosscuts lifting the knob up and down to rotate the miter gauge to the desired degree
- Plug your saw into a receptacle
- Push the work piece against the fence and hold it with one hand away from the blade
Lower the blade without pushing the trigger to make sure that the curve is on the other side of the piece you want to keep
- Adjust if needed
- Hold the handle and press the trigger to turn the miter saw on
- Bring the blade up to full speed before pulling it down towards the wood
- Lower the blade and make your cut, moving from front to back
- Release the trigger once the board is cut and let the blade come to a complete stop before raising it
- Cut all the marked pieces repeating the same process
- Measure all your cuts to make sure of the dimensions
- Adjust cuts to dimensions if needed
Chamfer the head and foot top boards
- Move 25,5 cm from the left and right-side edges of the board and draw a straight line
- Move 3.8 cm down from the top edge of the board and draw a straight line across the board
- Connect the ends of the 2 drawn lines to draw an angled line of an angle approximately 10°
Prepare the miter saw to crosscut:
- Set the bevel to 0 degrees and miter to 10 degrees to make a 10°miter cut lifting the knob up and down to rotate the miter gauge to the desired degree
- Plug your saw into a receptacle
- Push the work piece against the fence and hold it with one hand away from the blade
