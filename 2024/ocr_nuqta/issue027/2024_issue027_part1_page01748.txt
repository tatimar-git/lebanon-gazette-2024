- Lay out carefully how you will cut every piece from the sheets of PB: where to start and which pieces to cut first
- Mark an "x" on the waste side of the board. This will help position the blade
Prepare your table saw to start cutting:
- Make sure the blade is set to a true 90-degree angle to the bed/plate of the tool
- Lower the blade (with the power off) and position it so there's the thickness of a hair
between the teeth and the line
- Adjust the width of the fence that you need to cut
- Adjust the blade so it's higher than the thickness of the wood
- Make sure the edge of the blade is on the outside edge of the cut line, so the full amount measured is left behind
- Switch the power on
Make the cut:
- Use a push stick or splitter when cutting thin boards
- Let the blade come to a full stop before raising it, to prevent removing a small amount of wood which can change the length of our cut
- Measure the work piece after cutting
- Make sure pieces are square and cut at perfect 90-degree angles
- Adjust the cut if necessary
- Pull back the board and turn off the saw
- Label the cut pieces as you go to make it easier to assemble later
Check measurements:
- Check all cut pieces for square by measuring from the top right corner across the piece to the lower left corner
- Note the measurement. Then measure the opposite two corners. If the measurements are the same, your piece is square
- Sand lightly by hand pieces' edges if needed using a 120 to 320 grit sandpaper
- Put all the cut boards aside
- Store disposable scrap pieces in designated buckets
