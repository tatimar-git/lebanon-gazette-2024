ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, bed-makers, and related trades workers | Occupational Standard Beds Making
ISCO-08-7522 Bed-makers and related workers
EQF level - 3

The occupation covers the process of fabricating wooden boxes and frames: cutting, fitting, joining, assembling, and finishing prepared wooden parts to make a bed within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.

Process Element Description


P1
Process to make beds Output(s) • Beds produced according to design and type of use.
Process
Sub-Process
Activity • S1: Setting up the working place
• S2: Building a bunk bed
• S3: Building a single bed
• S4: Building a lift bed with storage
• S5: Building a baby crib
• S6: Housekeeping and reporting
Input(s) • Requirements and instructions for building a box and a frame to form a bed.
Resources • Technical documentation (execution drawings, material selection, and data sheets) of design.
• Tools and machinery for wood cutting, assembling, joining, and finishing.
• Materials and consumables used for building the cabinet.
• Personal Protective Equipment (safety harness and heavy-duty working shoes) and first aid kit.
• Cleaning kit (garbage bags, broom, basket, etc.).
