The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 5 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 3 – Building a single bed.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Cut the wood panels | The candidate should be able to read and understand woodworking drawings and cut list and read measurements. The candidate should be able mark lines to measure on the wood board to cut pieces to size and | ☐ | ☐

know how to operate a table saw.
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws The candidate should know how to mark the location of pilot holes on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil The candidate should know how to fix bed rail brackets to join the pieces. ☐ ☐
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the base support frame: screws The candidate should know how to mark the location of pilot holes on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to fix the base support frame with screws and PVA glue. ☐ ☐
Apply finishing technique: lacquer finishing The candidate should know how to prepare MDF wood pre finishing from cleaning to sanding and know what joints and areas to cover with masking tape before sending the workpieces to the painter's shop. ☐ ☐
Assemble the pieces The candidate should know how assemble the bed by gluing and joining the connectors hardware and screws pieces then measure and mark drilling holes on the wood pieces using a pencil, a measure tape, a square and a spirit level before fixing the support battens and mattress to the bed frame. The candidate should know how to glue and drive screws in place to assemble all pieces to the bed and sand finish if needed. ☐ ☐
