Preserve food with salt
- Salting is a process of preserving food by adding Sodium Chloride to food.
- Salting is also known as curing
- Two methods of salting are common
a- The dry method in which salt is added directly to the food to draw out moisture and inhibits the growth of bacteria that might spoil the food. It is a common method used with fish and meat.
b- The wet method, known as brining when salt and water are used, or pickling when vinegar, water and salt are being used. It is a common method used in preserving vegetables and cured beef.
N.B: Refer to Competence units 2 and 4.
