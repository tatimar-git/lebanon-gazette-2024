* Pickling is the process of preserving food in an acid solution, usually vinegar, or in salt solution (brine). The process of pickling is also known as brining and the resulting foods as pickles.
* Most major vegetables can be preserved by pickling. Cucumbers, cabbage, green olives, carrots, cauliflower, capers, onion, pepper and turnip are all famous pickled foods.
1- Pickling cucumbers
* Sterilize jars and lids in boiling water
* Wash and dry the cucumbers
* Add 4 cups of water, 1 cup of apple cider vinegar, 4 tablespoons of kosher salt into a pot
* Boil the mixture
* Put a grape leaf or some dill in the bottom of the jar. It helps in maintaining the cucumber crispness
* Add 1 clove of garlic in each jar
* Place cucumbers in the jar leaving a little space below the neck of the jar
* Ladle the boiling brine into the pickling jar leaving around 1.5 cm of space in the jar
* Wipe clean the top of the jar with a wet paper towel
* Close tight the jars with the lid
* Let the cucumbers pickles rest for 30 days before using them
N.B: You can substitute the apple cider vinegar with white vinegar
2- Pickling turnips
* Prepare the brine as discussed above in the pickling cucumber section
* Trim the sides of the turnip
* Peel off the skin of 1 turnip, peel in strips another one and leave 1 turnip unpeeled
* Peel off the skin of the beetroot
* Slice the turnips and beetroot as desired
* Place the sliced turnips and beetroot in a bowl and toss them together
* Cut the garlic in slices and add it to the turnips and beetroot
- Put 2 to 3 grapes leaves in the pickling jar along with 2 garlic cloves and 2 dry bay leaves
- Place the turnips and beetroot in the pickling jar
- Fill the jars with the boiling brine leaving a little space below the neck of the jar
- Drizzle top with some olive oil
- Seal the jars tightly
- Place the jars in a cool dark place for 7 to 10 days before using them
3- Pickling green olives
- Select the olives
- Crack the olives because they are bitter and put them in water
- Soak the cracked olives in water for 3 days to remove the bitterness
- Change the water daily
- Combine half cup of kosher salt with 4 cups of water
- Fill the jars with olives
- Slice some lemons (the acidity of lemon preserves the green colour of olives)
- Place one slice of lemon in each jar
- Slice some garlic cloves and jalapeno peppers and add them to the pickling jars (for flavour)
- Fill the jar with the water and salt brine leaving some space on the top of the jar
- Drizzle some olive oil on top (to keep moisture and seal the air)
- Seal the jars well tight with the lids
- Leave the jars undisturbed for 1 month before using them
