Education Standard
Preparing food and meals ingredients using chemical techniques
NQF level - 2
Overview
At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of chemical techniques such as brining, curing, marinating, drying, fermenting, pickling, salting, sugaring, and seasoning to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.

Competences | CU1. Setting up the work area CU2. Brining, marinating and curing food CU3. Drying food CU4. Fermentation and pickling CU5. Salting, sugaring and seasoning CU6. Housekeeping the work area
Abilities / Learning Outcomes | • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures ▪ Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the work area ▪ Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures ▪ Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients • Brine food ▪ Marinate food • Cure food • Dry food in the sun ▪ Dry food indoor using air ▪ Dry food with solar energy • Dry food with electric energy ▪ Dry food in the oven ▪ Dry food in the microwave • Ferment food using the lactic acid fermentation method ▪ Ferment food using the alcohol fermentation method

- Ferment food using the acetic fermentation method
- Pickle food
- Preserve food with salt
- Preserve food with sugar
- Season food correctly
- Identify hazards and risks associated with cleaning and take preventive measures
- Use appropriate detergents in cleaning
- Use cleaning detergents in correct quantity
- Wear protective gear to prevent injuries
- Dispose food waste appropriately
- Clean and sanitize tools and food contact surfaces
- Clean and sanitize machines used in preparing food
- Clean and sanitize floors, sinks, and walk-in coolers
- Collect dirty kitchen laundry
