3) Drying fruits
- Prepare the lemon juice mixture: 2 table spoons of lemon juice mixed with 4 cups of water
- Wash, dry and cut fruits (apricots, apples, peaches, cherries, or any type of fruit you wish to dry)
- Cut fruits in half or slice it (peaches should be peeled), remove seeds
- Soak fruits in the lemon water mixture for 10 minutes. It will help in preventing oxidation and browning the fruits
- Lay the fruits on perforated trays and cover it with cheese cloth to protect it from bugs.
- Put the trays in direct sunlight and bring it inside during the night
- Turn it over once in a while
- Keep it in the sun for around 3 to 4 days depending on weather conditions
- Store dried fruits in airtight containers
N.B: Most dried fruits are peaches, apples, apricots, figs, raisins, dates and prunes.
