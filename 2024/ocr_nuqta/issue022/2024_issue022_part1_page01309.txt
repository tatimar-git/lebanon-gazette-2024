* Alcohol fermentation is a complex process during which yeasts convert sugars to ethanol, carbon dioxide and other metabolic by-products that contribute to the chemical composition and sensorial properties of the fermented foodstuffs.
* Alcoholic fermentation is the basis for the manufacturing of alcoholic beverages such as wine and beer. Even breads rise due the carbon dioxide produced during the alcoholic fermentation of flour starch.
1) Ginger beer
Phase 1: Preparing ginger bug
* Combine 500 ml of water with 2 tablespoons of sugar (28 grams) and 2 tablespoons of finely chopped ginger (22 grams) in a glass or food grade plastic container
* Mix ingredients well together until the sugar is dissolved
* Cover with cheesecloth and let it sit for 24 hours
* Add the same amount of sugar and ginger to the container every 24 hours until it becomes fizzy. It takes arund 2 to 3 days
Phase 2: Preparing ginger beer
* Pour 1.8 litre of water into a large pot
* Add 273 grams of granulated sugar and 54 grams of grated ginger
* Bring it to boil then simmer between 5 to 8 minutes
* Let it cool naturally until it reaches room temperature allowing the ingredients to steep
* Strain the mixture into a mixing bowl
* Press out the juice
* Add 110 grams of ginger bug with the juice of 3 lemons
* Stir well to combine all ingredients
* Transfer the mixture to flip-top bottles leaving enough headroom
* Lock the bottles and let it sit at room temperature for 3 to 6 days until the mixture beccmes fizzy
* Label the bottles and store them in the refrigerator
