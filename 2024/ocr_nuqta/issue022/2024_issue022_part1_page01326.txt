ferment food using the lactic acid fermentation method | the candidate should prepare the sourdough starter, feed the starter regularly until it is frothy and bubbly, refrigerate or dehydrate the starter, bring the milk to boil then cool it down, culture it with yogurt, place it in a warm environment covered to become yogurt.

ferment food using the acetic fermentation method | the candidate should prepare the apples based on the recipe instructions, place apples in a container then add sugar and water, cover the container with cheesecloth, push the pieces up and down with a stick once per day for the first 22 days, then every 2-3 days from day 23 to day 30, strain the liquid into a jar and let it ferment for another 30 days. the candidate should prepare vegetables to be pickled according to the recipe, prepare the boiled brine, place vegetables in the jars with aromatics, add the boiling brine, seal the jars and let it rest enough time, crack the green olives, prepare a cold brine, place the olives along with aromatics in the jars, add the cold brine, seal the jars and let rest enough time.
