The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 3 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 - Brining, Marinating and Curing food

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES
Brine food | The candidate should identify the time needed to brine each type of food, mix salt and sugar with water and heat the solution to dissolve it, cool it down with ice, add the whole chicken and keep it submerged at all times, and refrigerate it enough time to gain extra moisture. | ☐
Marinate food | The candidate should identify the time needed to marinate each type of food, mix the marinade ingredients well with chicken wings, cover the bowl well and refrigerate enough time to tenderize and gain flavour. | ☐
Cure food | The candidate should pat dry the beef eye round cut, cover it with salt at all sides, refrigerate it for 24 hours, wipe off the excess of salt, wrap well the meat and truss it, refrigerate it again for 3 to 6 weeks to lose enough of its moisture. | ☐
