ASSESSMENT STANDARDS - UNIT 6
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 6- Housekeeping the work area | Yes / No
Clean equipment and utensils | Are cutting boards soaped and scrubbed with a brush to remove dirt?
Are cutting boards rinsed with hot water, sanitized and air dried?
Are the 3 basins filled 3/4 full with hot water?
Is food scraped from dishes to be washed?
Are dishes soaped in the first basin, rinsed in the centre one, then sanitized in the third?
Are dishes placed on a drain rack to dry?
Are basins drained one at a time and cleaned?
Is waste disposed from trash bins?
Are detergents and sanitizers dissolved in correct amounts?
Are racks, walls, counter tops, scale and any other appliance used during food preparation cleaned?
Clean working area | Is the exterior of fridge cleaned?
Is dirt on the floor removed with a broom?
Are floor and feet of tables washed and brushed?

Is floor dried with a squeagee?
Is warning sign wet floor placed?
