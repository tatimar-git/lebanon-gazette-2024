Criteria for competence unit 4- Fermentation and Pickling
Yes / No
Are flour and water mixed together in equal amounts in a bowl?
Is the bowl covered and left undisturbed for 2 days at a temperature of 21°C to 28°C away from direct sunlight?
Is the dough starter fed after 2 days by removing half of its quantity and adding equal amounts of water and flour?
Ferment food using the lactic acid fermentation method
Is the feeding process carried on every 12 hours for 7 days or every 8 hours if bubbling is slow?
Is feeding stopped when the starter is enough frothy and bubbly?
Is the starter refrigerated or dehydrated for later use?
Is milk brought to boil at 82°C and simmered?
Is milk stirred often to prevent burning the bottom and temperature checked with a thermometer?
Is milk cooled down to reach a temperature of 43°C, transferred to a container and cultured with ready yogurt?
Is bowl covered and placed in warm temperature of 32°C for 5 to 8 hours to ferment?
Is yogurt refrigerated?
Are water, sugar and chopped ginger combined together in a container?
Is container covered with cheesecloth and left undisturbed for 24 hours?
Is the ginger bug fed every 24 hours by adding equal amounts of sugar and ginger for 2-3 days?
Ferment food using the alcohol fermentation method
Are water, grated ginger and sugar boiled together and simmered for 5-8 minutes?
Is mixture cooled to room temperature and strained?
Are ginger bug and lemon juice added?
Is mixture transferred to flip-top bottles?
Are bottles sealed and let to sit at room temperature for 3-6 days to become fizzy?
Are bottles labelled and refrigerated?
Are apples washed, dried, cored and cut in small chunks then placed in a glass jar?
Are sugar and water added to the jar?
Are apples consolidated with a stick?

Ferment food using the acetic fermentation method | Is the jar covered with cheesecloth and left undisturbed for 7 days?
Are apples pieces pushed up and down once per day from day 2 till day 22?
Pickle food | Is the same process repeated every 2 or 3 days from day 23 till day 30?
Are ingredients strained and liquid transferred to a jar?
Is the jar sealed lightly and liquid left to ferment undisturbed for another month?
Does the liquid smells like vinegar after one month?
Is vinegar strained using a cheesecloth into bottles?
Are pickling jars sterilized?
Are water, kosher salt and apple cider vinegar boiled to constitute a brine?
Are cucumbers, turnips and beetroot washed and dried?
Are grape leaves and garlic gloves placed in the jars?
Are turnips and beetroot peeled and cut?
Are cucumbers, turnips and beetroot placed in the jars?
Is the boiling brine added to the pickling jars?
Is olive oil drizzled on top of the turnips?
Are jars sealed and left undisturbed enough time before using them?
Are olives selected, crushed and soaked in water for 3 days to remove the bitterness?
Is the water and salt mixture prepared?
Are olives placed in jars and topped up with the cold brine?
Are slices of lemon, jalapeno peppers and garlic added to the jars?
Is olive oil drizzled on top of the jars?
Are jars well sealed and left undisturbed for 1 month?
