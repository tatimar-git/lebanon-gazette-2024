It is an indirect way of drying food using solar energy. Air heated by the sun is harnessed and used to run the solar dryer.
Pros
- Easy to make from plastic wrap or cardboard boxes
- Very low cost
- Drying process is fast since the inside is warmer than the outside environment
- Less risk of spoilage because of the high speed of drying
- Food is protected from pests, flies, rain and dust
- Labour saving, it doesn't need to be moved inside at night
Cons
- Challenge of getting the right temperature and humidity throughout the drying process
- Weather unpredictability
Drying Process
- Prepare fruits and vegetables as discussed above
Place food on screen trays inside the solar dryer and close the door
Keep the outside plastic covers clean, otherwise it will reduce the dryer performance
Open the door slightly (20 mm) to improve air circulation, especially in hot weather, because fruits produce moisture when drying during the first few hours
Check food after 2 days
Unload the dryer as soon as possible in the afternoon
Let the dried food cool down before storing it in airtight container
Food dehydrators have an electric heating element to dry out food. A fan and vents are in place to circulate heat for uniform drying. Good quality dehydrators allow more of the flavour and colour to be retained in the dried food.
Pros
Food dehydrators have adjustable airflow and temperature controls, which allows the user to fine-tune the level of drying conditions for higher quality results.
Food dehydrators are ideal for drying delicate leafy herbs, fruits, starchy vegetables and even meats.
More foods can be dried at the same time
Faster drying compared with traditional drying methods
Consistent good results
Cons

- Requires counter space

- Fans are noisy during dehydration

Drying process
- Prepare the vegetables or fruits you wish to dry as mentioned above
- Place a tray in the bottom of the dehydrator for better air circulation
- Place food on the next tray over a mesh liner
- Close the dehydrator with the lid
- Set temperature at 35°C, not higher
- Wait for 12 hours (time varies depending on the food type)
- Place dried food on parchment paper to cool down
- Place dehydrated food in airtight container
N.B: There are many types of dehydrators, we suggest a countertop dehydrator with temperature control key.
