Season food correctly
Are the jars boiled covered in water for 20 minutes to remove air?
Are strawberries scooped off and chopped roughly?
Are strawberries macerated with sugar and lemon juice?
Is mixture brought to boil in a pot?
Is cooking heat kept at medium heat with constant stirring?
Is the mixture thicker and foam disappeared?
Is jam stored in jars while it is still hot?
Are seasoning ingredients identified?
Are different types of sugar identified?
Are the seasoning technique steps identified?
