COMPETENCE UNIT 6: HOUSEKEEPING THE WORK AREA
TRAINING STANDARD - UNIT 6
TRAINING GUIDELINES
Ability / Learning outcome:
- Clean equipment and utensils
- Clean working area
Training site:
- Commercial kitchen or food production area
Required Tools:
- Buckets
- Dustpan
- Food pedal waste tins
- Squeegees
- Microfiber cloths
- Brushes
- Scraper
- Sponges
- Dish drainer
- Wet floor sign
Required Materials and Consumables:
- Chemical resistant gloves
- Sanitizer (Chlorine, Iodine, or quat based)
- Soap detergent alkaline based
- Disposable waste bags
Clean equipment and utensils
Cleaning cutting boards
- Acid detergent on top of cutting board
- Brush the cutting board with a bristle brush
- Rinse the cutting board in a hot rinse water diluted with sanitizer
- Allow to dry
Three compartment sink cleaning tools
- Plug all three basins
- Fill the left basin 3/4 full of hot water
- Dispense soap
- Fill the centre basin 3/4 full with hot water at least 45°C
- Fill the right basin 3/4 full of sanitizer at a temperature of at least 28°C
- Scrap solid waste into the trash
- Dispose of the liquids in the dish room
- Soap and scrub each dish and tool
- Rinse in the centre basin
- Submerge in the sanitizer for at least 50 seconds
- Remove the dishes
- Allow to air-dry
- Put the dishes away when dry
- Drain one basin at a time
- Clean as you go
- Hose the counter with sanitizer
