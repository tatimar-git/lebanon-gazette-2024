Learning program
During the classroom lessons the following topics will be covered:
- Types of hazards and food contaminants, use of PPE, personal hygiene and work clothing
- Brining, marinating and curing food techniques
- Drying food methods
- Fermenting food methods
- Pickling food method
- Preserving food in salt methods
- Preserving food in sugar methods
- Seasoning food ingredients
- Seasoning food technique
- Cleaning and housekeeping the workplace
During the workshop lessons the following practical activities will be executed:
- Brine food before cooking
- Solar drying food steps
- Electric dehydrating steps
- Oven drying food steps
- Microwave drying food steps
- Preserving food in salt methods
- Food to preserve in sugar
- Preserving food in sugar methods
- Seasoning ingredients
- Seasoning food technique
- Different types of hazards associated with cleaning
- Types of cleaning
- Marinate food before cooking
- Cure food to preserve it
- Dry food in different methods
- Ferment food in different methods
- Sugar food in different methods
- Season food correctly
- Clean and housekeep the working area
During on the jcb training the following job activities will be carried out:
- Identify hazards and food contaminants, use of PPE and work clothing, apply personal hygiene habits (2 hours)
- Brine, marinate and cure food (16 hours)
- Dry a variety of food in different methods (16 hours)
- Ferment and pickle a variety of food in different methods (16 hours)
