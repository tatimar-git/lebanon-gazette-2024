STANDARDS FOR THE TESTING OF FOOD AND DRUGS
C/CIP/8/2014/11 STANDARD

الوثيقة رقم 11 من لجنة معايير الأدوية | Constitutional Standards
المادة 11 من المادة 11 | Testing food and drug ingredients using chemical techniques

المادة 1
The constitution covers the general of testing food and drug ingredients using a variety of chemical techniques such as testing, mixing, sampling, drying, fermenting, grinding, milling, sieving, separating and packaging to preserve the food material and to provide suitable and adequate labels. It is also used to determine whether the food meets the quality requirements.
