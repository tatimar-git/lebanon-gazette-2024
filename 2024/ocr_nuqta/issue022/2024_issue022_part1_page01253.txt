COMPETENCE STANDARD

ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Competence Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using chemical techniques
NQF level - 2

A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of chemical techniques such as brining, curing, marinating, drying, fermenting, pickling, salting, sugaring, and seasoning to preserve the food mainly and to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.

Competence | Ability / Learning Outcome
CU1 Selling up the work area | • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the work area • Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures • Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients
CU2 Brining, Marinating and Curing food | • Brine food in a saline solution • Brine food in a flavoured brine • Dry Brine food instantly • Marinate food in an acidic solution • Cure food by brining, marinating, smoking or picking Difference between brining, marinating and curing • Brining is for moisture. Brining makes lean meat juicier • Marinating is for moisture and flavour • Curing is for meat preservation
CU3 Drying food | • Dry food in the sun • Dry food indoor using air • Dry food with solar energy dryer • Dry food in an electric dehydrator • Dry food in the oven • Dry food in the microwave
CU4 Fermenting and Pickling | • Ferment food using the lactic acid fermentation method • Ferment food using the alcohol fermentation method • Ferment food using the acetic fermentation method • Pickle food

CU5
Salting, Sugaring and Seasoning food
- Preserve food with salt
- Preserve food with sugar
- Season food correctly
CU6
Housekeeping the work area
- Clean equipment and utensils
- Clean and sanitize the working area
