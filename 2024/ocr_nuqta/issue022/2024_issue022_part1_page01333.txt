الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٢ - ٢٠٢٤/٥/٣٠
Foods can be seasoned with salt, herbs, acids or spices to intensify the natural flavour of the food without changing it.
1- Herbs
Common herbs include sage, oregano, thyme, mint, rosemary, basil, chives, parsley, and marjoram.
- Use the herbs fresh to get more flavour or dry them to preserve it for later use
- Rinse the fresh herbs before adding to a recipe
- Store fresh herbs in the refrigerator
2- Spices
- Spices come from woody and non-woody plants. Instead of the leaves, spices come from a plant's fruit, seeds, root, flowers or bark. Most spices come from warm, tropical areas.
- Common spices are cumin, nutmeg (seeds), cinnamon (bark), vanilla (orchid's undeveloped fruit), ginger (root), cloves, saffron (parts of a flower), and pepper.
3- Salt
Salt is a universal seasoning ingredient added to almost every dish
Salt is also used to preserve food in curing and drying
Most common types of used salt are kosher salt, sea salt and table salt
a) Table salt has small grains and is usually iodized and can be easily sprinkled. It has additives other than Iodine and cannot retain moisture, thus making it unsuitable for food preservation. It is twice sallier than kosher and sea salt.
b) Sea salt is more flaky and coarser. You can put it in a salt grinder and grind it into small pieces.
c) Himalayan pink salt is also a sea salt containing all kinds of minerals in addition of magnesium, copper and calcium. It can be used the same as other types of salt, in cooking, on table, etc.
d) Kosher salt is not as coarse as sea salt or Himalayan salt. It has no additives. It is ideal for seasoning and food preservation because it penetrates food very quickly
4- Season food
- Follow the recipe to know what seasoning ingredients to use and the correct quantity to add
- Take a pinch of salt between your fingers, raise your hand high and sprinkle the salt while moving over the food. The higher you raise your hand, the more evenly seasoned the food will be
- Taste your food to know if you have to add more seasoning ingredients
- Add hard herbs to the food at the beginning of cooking (like when roasting chicken)
- Place fresh herbs in the dish for garnishing purposes and fresh flavour
- Toast spices to enhance the natural flavour
N.B: During cooking, if you want to dry out moisture salt food first (example onions), If you want food crispy salt it later (potatoes)
