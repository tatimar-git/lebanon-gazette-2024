2- Canning fruits in syrup
- Cut apricots in half along the seam and remove the pit
- Boil water and sugar in a pot (light syrup: 1 litre of water with 250g sugar; medium fruit syrup: 1 litre water with 400g sugar; heavy syrup: 1 litre water with 750g sugar)
- Add cut apricots to the syrup and boil it for 4 minutes
- Skim off any foam that might rise
image here
- Remove the apricots from the boiling syrup and place them face down in the jar
- Fill the jar with syrup
- Seal the jar with the lid
- Place the jars in boiling water and let it boil for 20 minutes
3- Orange peel candles
- Peel off the oranges
- Bring to boil 4 cups of water in a pot
- Add the oranges peel and boil for 5 minutes
- Remove the orange peels with a spider strainer
- Remove the white part from the inside of the peel
- Cut the peels in strips
- Bring to boil 1 cup of water with 1/2 cup of sugar
- Add the orange peel and cook for 15 minutes
- Remove the peels and place them in a plate and let them cool down
- Let the peels dry on a rack
- Coat with sugar
- Store peels in pickling jars
4- Fruits Jam
- Wash the strawberries (1 kg)
- Scoop the top leafy part out with a melon baller (scooper)
- Chop the strawberries roughly and place them in a bowl
- Add lemon juice of 1 lemon
- Add 2 cups of sugar
- Toss well sugar with strawberries
- Macerate the ingredients for 1 hour on the counter or overnight covered in the refrigerator
- Transfer the juicy mixture to a large pot
- Bring it to boil over medium heat while stirring constantly
- Continue cooking until the foam has disappeared and the mixture has thickened
- Test the thickness by pouring a spoon in a frozen plate. Pass your finger and flip the plate to both sides. If the jam stays put then it is done
- Transfer the jam to the glass jars and store them for later use
