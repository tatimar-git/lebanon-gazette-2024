الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٢ - ٢٠٢٤/٥/٣٠
١٢٦٧
Do not come to work if you are sick
Take a daily shower to keep hair, body, and face clean
Brush teeth daily and after smoking or eating food with strong odours
Wash hands before, during, and after preparing food
Wash hands after using the toilet, after eating or smoking, after coughing or blowing nose, and after touching garbage or any other dirty surface and object
Wet hands with clean warm running water
Apply soap
Rub both hands together palms facing
Rub the back of one hand with the palm of the other, with fingers interlaced
Rub hands together, palms facing with fingers interlaced
Rub the back of the fingers (hand closed) on the opposing palm
Rub each thumb with the other hand using a rotational motion
Rub tips of fingers on the opposite palm
Rub wrists with the other hand using a rotational motion
Rinse hands with water and thoroughly dry your hands immediately using disposable paper towels or air hands dryers
Dry hands using a clean towel, paper towels, or the air-dryer
Wear clean kitchen clothing and PPE
Pull hair back and cover it with a toque or helmet
Cover facial hair with beard net
Keep finger nails short and clean
Avoid nail polish, perfume and aftershave
Remove jewellery and accessories
- Do not smoke in food preparation areas
- Wear gloves before handling raw meats and ready to eat foods
- Remove kitchen clothing before smoking or leaving the kitchen
- Direct your sneeze into your upper arm
- Discard chewing gum
- Keep hands away from your hair, nose, face, ears, and eyes
- Cover cuts, open sores, and rashes on your hands or forearm with bandages and gloves
- Do not sit on food preparation surfaces
- Do not put your shoes on kitchen walls
