This is the quickest method for drying food. Only few short cycles can dry out food with flavour and colour all intact.
It works better with leafy vegetables and herbs. Other types of food cannot be dried in a microwave since they will be cooked before drying out.
Pros
- Quick food drying
- Maintains food flavour and colour
Cons
- Works only for small amounts of food
- Food may taste burnt, because the colour is retained, making it difficult to know if it's starting to cook rather than drying
- Challenge of knowing what cycle to use to effectively dry food
Drying herbs
- Wash and dry herbs in a salad spinner as discussed earlier
- Separate the leaves and arrange in a single layer on a paper towel
- Cover with another paper towel
- Place in the microwave
- Microwave in 20 seconds intervals for 1 minute
- Check the leaf after 1 minute. If they are berdy or feel moist, they need more time
- Decrease to 10 seconds intervals
- Check the leaves. If they are crispy then they are perfectly dried
- Let it cool before storing them in airtight containers
