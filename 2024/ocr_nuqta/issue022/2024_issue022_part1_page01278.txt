Curing is a method of food preservation especially for meats and fishes, by the addition of salt.
The aim is to draw moisture cut of the food by the process of osmosis.
Salt curing is also known as dry curing.
Since curing decreases water content, the food becomes inhospitable for the microbe growth that causes food spoilage.
Parma ham, Spanish Chorizo and Italian Salami are famous salt cured foods.
Curing beef meat with salt
- Place a layer of non-iodized salt in the bottom of a mixing bowl
- Pat the beef meat dry
- Put meat over and cover it all completely with salt
- Put the meat in the refrigerator for 24 hours
- Wipe off salt after 24 hours with a clean damped paper towel
- Wrap meat with a clean cheese cloth
- Truss the meal
- Hang the meat in the fridge. After 3 to 6 weeks, the meal should lose 30 to 40% of its weight
Difference between brining, marinating and curing
- Brining is for moisture. Brining makes meat juicier
- Marinating is for flavour
- Curing is for meat preservation
