It is the process of soaking foods, particularly meats, in a seasoned, often acidic, called marinade before cooking.
The objective of marinating is to enhance flavour and change surface texture.
Basic ingredients are salt, fat (olive oil), acid (apple cider vinegar, balsamic vinegar, or citrus juice), sugar (brown sugar or honey) seasonings (garlic, ginger, mustards, etc.), herbs (thyme, basil, oregano, parsley or mint)
Always keep a marinade in the refrigerator
Do not reuse a marinade
Use non-reactive food container to marinate food
Marinade time

Meat | Time needed
Beef and pork sirloin, flank and skirt | 24 hours
Chicken breasts or thighs | 2 hours
Whole chicken | 24 hours to 2 days
Seafood | 15 to 30 minutes
Whole game birds and ducks | 4 to 6 hours
Simple marinade recipe
- 140 ml citrus juice or apple cider vinegar
- 120 ml olive oil
- 2 Tb herbs and spices
- 2 Tb salt
Chicken wings marinade
- Pour the olive oil in a mixing bowl
- Add the chicken wings
- Add vinegar
- Add dry basil
- Add onion powder
- Mix all with a spoon
- Cover bowl with cling film
- Refrigerate for 2 hours
