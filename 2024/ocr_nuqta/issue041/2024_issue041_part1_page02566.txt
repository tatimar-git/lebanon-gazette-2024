USAID | LEBANON
FROM THE AMERICAN PEOPLE
AMENDMENT THREE TO THE
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
AND
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF LEBANON
FOR COLLABORATION UNDER THE QUALITY INSTRUCTION TOWARDS
ACCESS AND BASIC EDUCATION IMPROVEMENT PROJECT
Whereas, the Government of the United States of America, acting through the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID), and the Government of Lebanon, acting through the Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE), signed a Memorandum of Understanding, dated March 31, 2016 for collaboration under the Quality Instruction Towards Access and Basic Education Improvement
(QITABI) project (the “MOU”);
Whereas, the QITABI project will continue through an extension of the duration of implementation of QITABI 2 by World Learning, as well as through a follow on award carried out by Research Triangle International, until July 31, 2028;
Now, therefore, the Parties wish to amend the MOU as follows pursuant to Section 3.7 (Amendment and Modification) of the MOU:
1. The expiration date set out in Section 3.10 (Effective Date) of the MOU is hereby modified from “March 31, 2024” to July 31, 2028”, to include the time extension of QITABI 2 and the new QITABI 3 project.
2. References to the “Project” in the MOU are deemed to include QITABI 2 and QITABI 3 and for the avoidance of doubt, the terms of the MOU are to apply to assistance implemented under both QITABI Projects.
3. The MOU now includes Annex II that outlines the objectives of QITABI 3; it is attached to this Amendment.
Effective date: This Amendment is effective as of the date of signature by both Parties.
Language: This Amcndment is prepared in two originals, in the English and Arabic languages. In the
event of ambiguity or conflict among versions, the English language version is to control.
Except as specifically amended or modified by this Amendment, the MOU is to remain in effect in accordance with its terms.
In witness whereof, the Parties each acting through its duly authorized representative, have caused this Amendment to be signed in their names on the dates set out below.
