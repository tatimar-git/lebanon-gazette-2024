FROM THE AMERICAN PEOPLE
ANNEX II
The Parties agree that the following objectives constitute a program designed to achieve the purpose of this MOU to the extent they are supported by USAID's in-kind assistance. Provision of in-kind assistance is subject to the terms and conditions of this MOU.
USAID's assistance, subject to the terms and conditions of U.S. law and the agreement between
USAID and the Implementing Partner, will be implemented by the Implementing Partner and will aim to achieve the following outcomes through the Project:
1. Improved national-level service delivery of inclusive education: This outcome is expected to be achieved through a) Technical and administrative support provided to MEHE and CERD, and b) Improved inclusive curriculum, research and assessment capacities.
2. Improved instruction for literacy, numeracy, social and emotional learning and completion: This outcome is expected to be achieved through a) Improved cadre of educators who deliver evidence-based instruction, b) Students have access to social and emotional learning, life skills and youth programs to prepare them for the future, c) Schools adopt mechanisms that promote the engagement of caregivers in their children's learning and leveraging the community as part of improvement efforts.
