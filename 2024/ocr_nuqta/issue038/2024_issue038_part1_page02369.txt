Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Lebanon and the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the exemption from visa requirements for holders of diplomatic, service and special passports
The Government of the Republic of Lebanon and the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan, hereinafter referred to as “the Parties”,
Desiring to further strengthen their friendly relations by facilitating the entry, exit and movement of holders of diplomatic and service/special passports of the Republic of Lebanon and the Republic of Kazakhstan;
Acting pursuant to the national legislation of the States;
have agreed as follows:
Article 1
Types of passports
The provisions of the present Agreement apply to the following types of passports:
for citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan:
- diplomatic passport;
- service passport;
for citizens of the Republic of Lebanon:
- diplomatic passport;
- service passport;
- special passport.
Article 2
Visa exemption
Citizens of the State of each Party holding valid diplomatic and service/special passports may repeatedly enter to, transit through, exit and stay in the territory of the State of the other Party for a period not exceeding thirty (30) days in each period of one hundred and eighty (180) days from the date of their first entry.
Article 3
Members of diplomatic missions or consular offices
Citizens of the State of each Party holding valid diplomatic and service/special passports assigned as members of diplomatic missions or consular posts in the territory of the State of the other Party and the members of their families may enter to, transit through, exit and stay in the territory of the State of the other Party without obtaining appropriate visas for the period of their accreditation.
