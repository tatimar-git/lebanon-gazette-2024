Article 4
Duration of passports validity
The duration of validity of diplomatic and service/special passports of citizens of the State of each Party shall be at least six (6) months prior to the date of entry into the territory of the State of the other Party.
Article 5
Entry and exit conditions
Entry/exit and transit of citizens of the States of the Parties, holders of valid diplomatic and service/special passports on the territory of the State of the other Party are carried out through checkpoints of the state border of the two States open for international communication.
Article 6
Powers of the authorities
1. Citizens of the States of the Parties, holders of valid diplomatic and service/special passports, during their stay in the territory of the State of the other Party are obliged to comply with the applicable laws and regulations of that Party without prejudice to the provisions of the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations of April 18, 1961 and the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations on April 24, 1963.
2. Each of the Parties reserves the right to refuse entry or shorten the period of stay in the territory of its State of citizens of the State of the other Party whose presence is considered undesirable.
Article 7
Suspension
The Parties may temporarily, partially or completely suspend the operation of this Agreement in order to ensure national security, public order and public health. The Party making the decision to suspend the validity of this Agreement shall notify the other Party through diplomatic channels no later than thirty (30) calendar days before the date of entry into force of such decision.
Article 8
Samples of passports and travel documents
1. The Parties shall exchange samples of valid diplomatic and service/special passports through diplomatic channels within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of signing of this Agreement.
