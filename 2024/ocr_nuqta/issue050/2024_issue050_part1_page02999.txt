Prospectivity Studies and Dataset Rejuvenation
Over the years working with LPA, TGS have undertaken a number of prospectivity initiatives on
the Lebanon datasets to help promote and showcase the prospectivity of the Lebanon Offshore. The addition of new data to an area or new understanding brings new insights. The recent drilling in Block 9 has given renewed interest to the area along with encouraging responses after the well results indicating a commitment to additional drilling in the area. The well results are key to updating understanding of the offshore.
Quantitative Interpretation [QI] and Rock Physics Study:
QI and rock physics studies over a selected area of the Lebanon offshore can be undertaken to better understand the prospectivity potential.
