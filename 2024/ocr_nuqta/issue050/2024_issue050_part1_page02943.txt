Decision No. 25 D/M dated 2024/12/4
Granting PGS Geophysical AS a non-exclusive reconnaissance license in Block no.8 Offshore Lebanon
The Minister of Energy and Water,
- Pursuant to the Law no. 132 dated 2010/8/24 (Offshore Petroleum Resources Law), particularly Article 11,
- Pursuant to the Decree no. 7968/2012 (Petroleum Administration), particularly Article 9,
- Pursuant to the Decree no. 10289/2013
(Petroleum Activities Regulations), particularly Articles 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, and 22,
- Pursuant to the reconnaissance license application submitted by PGS Geophysical AS through its parent company, wholly
owned by TGS ASA, and the contents, annexes, and documents of the application,
- Pursuant to the recommendation of the Lebanese Petroleum Administration no. 15/2024 issued in its letter No. 374/ب dated 2024/12/03.
Whereas PGS Geophysical AS, through its parent company, wholly owned by TGS ASA, submitted a request for a Reconnaissance License in Block no. 8 in offshore Lebanon to the Minister of Energy
