Continued License Round Support for Lebanon
The LPA announced the Third Offshore Licensing Round on 27th December 2023.TGS are proud
to support this offshore block offering in collaboration with LPA. Utilizing the knowledge gained in
other countries around Africa and the Eastern Mediterranean, TGS can see the benefits of flexibility
in the licensing models that countries utilize and believe that LPA and prospective Energy
Companies can benefit from this knowledge.
TGS propose to continue supporting the LPA in the Third Offshore License Round, utilizing a
marketing budget for the following activities:
• Sales & Marketing Campaign, including social media exposure
• Potential Bidder data rooms and data shows, both in-person and online
• TGS commits to supporting the LPA at 3 industry conferences or Industry events per calendar year, by way of sponsoring travel and accommodation, exhibition stand space, design and logistics, attendance for 2 LPA board members per conference. The selection of these conferences to be agreed upon minimum 6 months prior (where possible) to minimize travel and accommodation costs and provide the best opportunities for speaking and panel slots.
o For example, in 2024 we supported the Minister and 3 board members of LPA, plus conference booth and travel/accommodation for all 4 delegates. EGYPES, Cairo, 19-21 Feb 2024 – TGS had also prepared travel and accommodation for the World Energies Summit in October for one LPA member and the Minister.
NOTE: The flights, accommodation and other such use of the Marketing budget for members of the LPA in support of marketing and training etc., including the use of external consultants, up to the value stated above in any given calendar year will be subject to due diligence and compliance standards of TGS, and shall be administered by TGS, there will be no direct cash
payment to any individual.
