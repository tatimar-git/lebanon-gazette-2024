FINANCING AGREEMENT
The European Commission, hereinafter referred to as the "Commission", acting on behalf of the European Union, hereinafter referred to as the "EU",
of the one part, and
The Republic of Lebanon, hereinafter referred to as the "Partner", represented by the Ministry of Finance, signatory of the agreement, and the Ministry of Energy and Water,
of the other part, hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Parties”,
have agreed as follows:
Article 1 - Financing of the Action
1.1. The Partner agrees that the EU finances the implementation of the following action described in Annex I: System strengthening in the field of water in Lebanon ACT-61948 (the “Action”). Annex I summarises the objectives, expected results, implementation modalities and indicative duration of this Action.
1.2. The total estimated cost of this Action is EUR 17 000 000.00 and the maximum EU contribution to this Action is set at EUR 17 000 000.00.
Article 2 – Execution period
2.1 The execution period of this Financing Agreement shall commence on the date of the entry into force of this Financing Agreement and covers the period needed to implement the Action and its closure.
2.2 The effective end date of the execution period of this Financing Agreement shall be
communicated by the Commission, in writing, to the Partner.
Article 3 – General obligations
3.1 The Partner and the EU commit to engage in a constructive political dialogue at the appropriate level on the implementation of this Financing Agreement.
