Experienced Technical Teams to Support Exploration Story
TGS has a dedicated technical team overseeing our Africa and Eastern Mediterranean interests and partnerships. Our personal approach to our clients is at the forefront of what we do.
Unlike other seismic contractors, access to the large library of broadband and conventional 2D and 3D seismic data across the whole of the Eastern Mediterranean, has allowed TGS technical
teams to acquire a comprehensive understanding of the offshore oil and gas basins in the region, and that we can draw upon in our services to the LPA. Building on this regional geological knowledge, and honed from our comprehensive data library, TGS workflows provide a unique understanding of prospectivity and a reduction in exploration risk.
TGS reservoir characterization workflows are applicable in several settings from frontier basin reconnaissance, through exploration, appraisal, and field development, and further to production optimization. They can be tailored to the needs of LPA to ensure confidence in data quality when licensing to oil companies.
