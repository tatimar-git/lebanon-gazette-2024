Article 13 – Entry into force
This Financing Agreement shall enter into force on the date on which the Commission receives a notification from the Partner confirming the completion of the internal procedures of the Partner necessary for its entry into force. The Commission shall inform the Partner of the date of the receipt of this notification. This Financing Agreement shall not enter into force if such a notification is not received by the Commission by 31 December 2024.
Done in 3 original copies, 2 being handed to the Commission and 1 to the Partner.
