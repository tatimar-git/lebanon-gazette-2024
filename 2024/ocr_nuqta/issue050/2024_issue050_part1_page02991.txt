Part 2 -Data Integration and Continuing Support
The full scope of this application includes the following items and is detailed further in this application and in the supporting documentation:
1. Application for Reconnaissance License for the acquisition of approximately 1,300sqkm of new BroadBand 3D seismic data over Block 8 offshore Lebanon.
2. Prior to the new acquisition, and subject to the Ministry approval we will include all existing seismic data offshore Lebanon into a new 10 year exclusive agreement for the marketing and licensing of data offshore Lebanon.
3. A new a post-migration merge of all existing 3D seismic data offshore Lebanon into one contiguous volume
4. Eventual integration of the new survey with all existing 3D seismic data offshore Lebanon
5. Rejuvenation/re-processing of existing 3D data
6. Direct and indirect contribution to the promotion, marketing, training, institutional support and knowledge transfer
