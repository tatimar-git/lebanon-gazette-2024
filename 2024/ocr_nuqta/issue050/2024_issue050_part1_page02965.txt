5.5. Onshore Interaction
All vessels utilize the Telenor NORSat-B satellite communications system, which operates between 512 Kb/s and 2 Mb/s depending on requirements of data transfer, but additional bandwidth can be provided (at additional cost). Further details will be supplied on award of the contract.
Along with the transfer of certain data products to a client FTP site, this also allows transfer of test datasets between the vessel and a TGS Imaging centre, enabling ongoing parameter testing at the processing center while data continues to be acquired and processed in the on-board environment.
