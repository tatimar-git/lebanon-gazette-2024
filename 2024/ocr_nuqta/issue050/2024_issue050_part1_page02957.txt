3.2. Overlapping Shot Records
The continuous records passed downstream from the gAS recording system are assembled near-real time and regular online QC currently remains largely the same as for conventional acquisition, based on shot event-based records.

Depending on the survey design, each individual shot record may be longer than the time interval between shots. This means that the part of each record that is overlapped with another will be stored twice, once as the end of the preceding record and a second time as the beginning of the following record, as illustrated in Figure 4.
