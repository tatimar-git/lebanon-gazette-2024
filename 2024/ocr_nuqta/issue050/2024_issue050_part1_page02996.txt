Enhanced Value for Lebanon with Additional Data
The value of one combined dataset across all of the offshore cannot be understated, as the Ministry is aware the blocks offshore Lebanon contain a mix survey data, which, if promoted separately will make the promotion of each block more challenging.
From our experience promoting future license rounds globally and in Lebanon we understand the energy companies appreciate the ability to view the data in each of the offered blocks as one consistent volume, without geographical limitations of the underlying survey.
A unified volume provides additional confidence to the energy companies.
In this regard TGS propose to include all of the 3D data available offshore Lebanon, including the data currently covered by the existing PGS, (now TGS), as well as all other 3D data available to the Ministry.
This would bring significant value to Lebanon by allowing the creation of one dataset for future new licensing ahead of the license round and allowing for enhanced geological/geophysical studies across the entire basin by the oil and gas companies.
Using our expertise, we would post-stack merge each data volume with amplitude and phase matching, (de-noising where necessary), thus providing one contiguous volume across the
offshore area in Lebanon, and if feasible merged to our Cyprus MC3D datasets.
If appropriate we can include other datasets available such as existing 2D that may not be covered by existing MultiClient agreements.
Oil companies recognise the significant value in this type of dataset ahead of license round applications, as they are able to interpret and analyse the data with consistency across the basin.
Including the data under one agreement will significantly simplify the licensing process for the energy companies and for the LPA.
