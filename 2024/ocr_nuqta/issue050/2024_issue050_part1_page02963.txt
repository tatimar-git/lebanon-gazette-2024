5.3. Standard Real Time QC
The recording and QC system gAS is used to provide real time QC displays for the observers and the geophysicists to monitor on-line covering all required aspects of QC. All on-line and near real time QC in gAS is carried out after matching the vertical velocity geosensor response to the hydrophone and the application of a low-cut filter to restrict the data to the bandwidth of interest. A 3-6-90-120 Hz band-pass filter is applied to the hydrophone data.
The quality control products will include, but not necessarily be limited to, the following:
■ Shot gather window display for each shot point for every streamer for the hydrophone and geosensor channels (Figure 8).
■ Noise anomaly display, used to highlight major differences from shot to shot, ideal for observing source problems such as auto-fires, or external noise such as seismic interference or ship noise, from a chosen streamer (Figure 9).
■ Single trace display of a near offset channel from a chosen streamer (Figure 9).
■ Single trace display of time-break – with skew correction applied to show accurate system timing from shot to shot (Figure 9).
■ Ambient RMS noise windows, showing data from all streamers to highlight sources of noise. The shallow (water column) window is normally shown first due to contamination of deep noise window by multiple/reverberant energy (hydrophone channels only) (Figure 10).
