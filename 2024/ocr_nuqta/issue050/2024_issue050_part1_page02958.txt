3.4. Obtaining the Optimal Acquisition Parameters
The following must be considered when calculating the optimal acquisition parameters:
- Water depth in the survey area.
- Target depth(s).
- Velocity model.
- Intended shot point interval.
- Intended record length.
- Expected average acquisition bottom speed.
- Current strength relative to acquisition direction.
The results of this assessment will typically provide the optimal record length and the maximum amount of shot overlap that can be achieved. Sometimes a different shot point interval may also be considered.
Nucleus+ is used to design and model the survey (Figure 6) and a Vessel Speed Calculator will be used to generate the vessel parameters for the survey.
