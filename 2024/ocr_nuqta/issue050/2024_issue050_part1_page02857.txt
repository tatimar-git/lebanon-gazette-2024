4. Pre-conditions
The Border Control Committee is requested to comply with the following pre-conditions:
- Integrated Border Management Action plan is updated and approved at technical level;
- First Annual report on monitoring of the implementation of the IBM Strategy and Action : elaborated¹.
The security forces are requested to comply with the following pre-conditions:
- Risk Analysis Unit of the Lebanese Armed Forces and General Security are operational²;
- the entry/exit registration system (sBMS) replacement concept is approved by General Security;
- Internal Security Forces coastal brigade concept is updated based on the sectoral strategy ⁴.
Pre-conditions need to be fulfilled within 1 year from the signature of the Financing Agreement before start of activities.
IBM's Action plan under revision and annual report in drafting at the technical level of the Border Control Committee
Both units are established. It is also required that they be operational.
A new SMBS system is foreseen but not yet approved by the General Security. Support to the current system is thus not under the action, as it is not sustainable.
Stronger involvement of ISF in IBM is foreseen in the ISF strategic plan and IBM Action plan, but a more detailed required support is necessary and expected from ISF.
