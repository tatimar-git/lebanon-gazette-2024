Integration:
• After the new survey planned for in this application is acquired and processed we will integrate it into the newly merged contiguous dataset above enhancing the value and appeal of the database to the energy companies.
Rejuvenation:
• With industry support we will re-process all of the 3D data to provide a modern processed dataset for use in further exploration and license rounds but also the eventual field developments by the energy companies.
Marketing & Knowledge Share:
TGS will continue to market and promote the offshore Lebanon License rounds in a variety of ways,
• Promotion during our client meetings,
• Our own Technical G&G studies highlighting the exploration story offshore Lebanon
• Promotion via our website and other opportunities that arise.
• In addition, we propose a budget of $100,000 per year over the 10 years of the new agreement for the exclusive rights for marketing and licensing of data offshore Lebanon.
The budget will provide continued contribution for the promotion of this and future Offshore Licensing Round covering the costs of flights, accommodation and other such use of the budget for members of the LPA in support of marketing and training etc., including the use of external consultants.
The budget of $100,000 in any given calendar year will be subject to due diligence and compliance standards of TGS, and shall be administered by TGS, there will be no direct cash payment to any individual – more details are included in the supporting information below.
Institutional Support:
• We propose to provide institutional support of $375,000 per year for the first four years of the new agreement for the exclusive rights for marketing and licensing of the data offshore Lebanon.
Revenue Shares
For the proceeds received for the licensing of each individual survey data considered in the proposal, TGS propose the following Ministry revenue share subject to the agreement with the Ministry for the exclusive rights to market, promote and license all the Seismic data offshore Lebanon.

Item Subject to Revenue Share | Ministry Revenue Share %
Existing surveys, (merge of entire PGS MegaSurvey+ & Spectrum data) | 60%
Rejuvenated & re-processed data | before cost recovery 15% after 1x cost recovery 25% after 2x cost recovery 50%
New Seismic Acquisition | before cost recovery 10% after 1x cost recovery 20% after 2x cost recovery 50%

TGS commits to working in partnership with the Ministry and LPA to jointly promote oil & gas exploration and production activities, acreage opportunities and Licensing Rounds in the territory.
