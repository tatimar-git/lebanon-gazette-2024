TGS is committed to preventing harm to the environment by minimizing the risk of its operations.
Overall environmental goals:
- Plan and conduct our work to minimize the impact on marine life, habitats and local communities
- Ensure the responsible use of energy and minimize emission of CO2 and other
pollutants
• Minimize waste and ensure safe handling and responsible disposal
• Encourage the development and diffusion of technologies that minimize our environmental impact
The TGS Environment Management System provides the basic building blocks to enable TGS to implement a coherent and strategic approach to environment management across business product lines and primary activities.
The Environment Management System provides:
• Flexibility for TGS Product Lines to identify environmental aspects of primary activities and then define achievable environmental objectives and targets
• Engagement all levels/activities of TGS organization
• An interface with global environment issues such as climate change (fuel/energy use and air emissions) and flora/fauna conservation (underwater sound and marine life) whilst maintaining focus on traditional environment subjects such as Nitrogen and Sulphur Oxide (NOx and SOx) air emissions, waste management and oil spill contingency
• A mechanism for periodic review, continuous monitoring and measurement against ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 standards.
TGS adopts environmental preventive measures in accordance with International, regional and national regulations and applicable industry guidelines such as the IAGC Environmental Guidelines for Worldwide Geophysical Operations, IAGC Marine Geophysical Safety Manual or the JNCC Guidelines for Minimizing Acoustic disturbance to Marine Animals from Seismic Surveys.
