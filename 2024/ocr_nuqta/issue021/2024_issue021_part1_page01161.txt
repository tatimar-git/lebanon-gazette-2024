Cleaning and housekeeping the workplace
During the workshop lessons the following practical activities will be executed:
- Prepare food for cutting
- Prepare food for kneading
- Prepare food for mixing
- Cut food using different cutting techniques
- Knead food hand method
- Mix food using different mixing techniques
- Clean and housekeep the workplace
During on the job training the following job activities will be carried out:
- Identify hazards and food contaminants, use of PPE and work clothing, apply personal hygiene habits (5 hours)
- Cut food in different techniques (16 hours)
- Knead food using hand method (16 hours)
- Mix food using different mixing techniques (16 hours)
- Clean and housekeep the workplace (5 hours)
