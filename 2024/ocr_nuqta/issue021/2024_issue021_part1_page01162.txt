Electrical hazards
Damaged and worn electrical cords, dripping water near electricity sources and faulty equipment can cause create a shock hazard.
- Keep power leads of the floor to avoid damage
- Use only cords Ground Fault Circuit Interrupters (GFCI's)
- Avoid handling a cord or plug that has exposed wiring
- Pull on the plug not the cord to disconnect it from the outlet
- Dry hands when handling cords or plugs
- Don't run cords across walkways to avoid damaging the cord
- Inspect all electrical equipment regularly (every 2 weeks)
- Do not place electrical appliances near the sink
- Do not overload a power outlet
