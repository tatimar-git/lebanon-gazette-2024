Cut food in different techniques
Brunoise cubes cut (fine dice)
- Wash and peel carrot
- Cut the carrot in third
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle
- Slice rectangle into strips of 2 mm thickness for regular brunoise
- Lay flat the strips and cut into equally shaped dice
Julienne cut
- Wash and peel the carrot
- Cut the ends of carrot
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle
- Slice rectangle 4 cm to 5 cm lengthwise
- Lay flat and cut into strips of 1 mm thickness
