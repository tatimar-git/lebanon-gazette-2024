ASSESSMENT STANDARD

ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks NQF level - 2 | Assessment Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using mechanical techniques
A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of mechanical techniques such as cutting, kneading, and mixing to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competence unit 1 | Setting up the workplace
Assessment criteria | The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to a) Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures, b) Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures, c) Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen, d) Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff, e) Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the workplace, f) Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures, g) Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients in less than 9 hours.

Assessment procedure and indicators Setting for the assessment commercial kitchen or food production area.

Competence unit 2 Cutting food
Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to a) Check if food is in good quality, b) Clean and prepare food before cutting, c) Organize appropriate cutting tools, d) Cut food in different techniques, e) Store cut food for later use.
