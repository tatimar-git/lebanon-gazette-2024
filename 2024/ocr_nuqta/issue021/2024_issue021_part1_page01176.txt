Look for bright green colour and crispness when buying greens like celery, parsley, kale, lettuce and broccoli
Look for the head of a cabbage and avoid buying any cabbage with a hole and an off smell
Look for a bright even colour with no dark spots or mould, firm and good weight tomatoes
Choose heavy, smooth and wrinkle-free root vegetables such as turnips, carrots, beets, sweet potatoes
Look for dirty potatoes as it means they are fresh out of soil
Never buy a green potato
Do not buy potato starting to sprout
Look for potatoes without wrinkles
Look for heavy and hard onion and garlic free of sprouting
Do not buy lemons with brown spots
Discard bruised or mouldy vegetables
