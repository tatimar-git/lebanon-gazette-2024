Place dough on a clean surface to knead it
Add flour if dough is still sticky
Press the dough
Turn the dough
Fold edge to edge straight down
Press to the centre with the palms of hands
Turn the dough to the same as earlier
Fold edge to edge straight down again
Press to the centre with the palms of hands
Turn the dough again same side
Repeat again
Keep on kneading for 10 minutes until the dough becomes smooth, elastic and a bit sticky
Press the dough lightly it should spring back if fully kneaded. Otherwise knead it further.
Pull the dough out to make sure it doesn't tear
Shape the ball into a ball with a belly button called the key of the dough
Put the dough in a bowl
Cover the bowl with cling film and let it rise in a warm environment for 30 to 45 minutes
Store kneaded food for later use
- Dust preparation surface with flour
- Place the fermented dough on the flour and cover all sides
- Fold the dough into a roll
- Divide the dough roll in cuts of 250 grams to 260 gr using a scraper
- Weigh cuts on a kitchen scale
- Shape cuts into balls
- Keep key dough side down
- Place in a plastic rack with lid
- Use refrigerated dough within 2 days
- Use frozen dough within 4 weeks
N.B: Dough can be as well prepared in a stand mixer with a dough hook. But for teaching purposes, trainees should learn how to knead dough with hands. Later on after they have learned the kneading technique correctly, they can use a stand mixer in their work.
