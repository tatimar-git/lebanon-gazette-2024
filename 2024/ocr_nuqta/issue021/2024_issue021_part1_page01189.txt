COMPETENCE UNIT 3: KNEADING FOOD
TRAINING STANDARD - UNIT 3
TRAINING GUIDELINES

Ability / Learning outcome: | Required Tools: | Required Materials and Consumables:
• Organize appropriate kneading tools and materials • Knead food properly • Store kneaded food for later use | • Mixing Bowls • Wooden spoon • Dough Scraper • Spatula • Kitchen scale • Balloon wire whisk • Measuring spoons • Measuring cups • Digital instant read thermometer • Food grade silicone brush | • All-purpose flour • Instant yeast • Clean lukewarm water • Fine salt • Sugar • Cling film wrap • Vegetable oil • Food grade zip plastic bags
Training site:
• Commercial kitchen or food production area

Organize appropriate kneading tools and materials
- Food grade airtight containers
- Prepare clean mixing bowl
- Prepare Dough Scraper, wire whisk, and spatula
- Weigh required amount of flour 00 following required recipe
- Add required amount of instant yeast to water
- Add half of the clean drinkable water required
- Mix ingredients with your hands for 7 minutes
- Add the salt and the rest of the water and knead
- Check dough temperature with a thermometer it should be between 23°C - 25°C
N.B: Dough can be very sticky. Wearing gloves will make kneading a difficult task. But high standards of personal hygiene regarding hands washing, short nails, removal of accessories and wearing work cloth must highly respected.
- Clean hands with a scraper
- Mix again with a spatula
- Place the dough on a clean surface to knead it
