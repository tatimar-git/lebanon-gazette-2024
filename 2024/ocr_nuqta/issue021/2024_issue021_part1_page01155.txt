Occupational Standard
Preparing food and meals ingredients using mechanical techniques
NQF level - 2
The occupation covers the process of preparing food and meal ingredients using a variety of mechanical techniques such as cutting, kneading, and mixing to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
