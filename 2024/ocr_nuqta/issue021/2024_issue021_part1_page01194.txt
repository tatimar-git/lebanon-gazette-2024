Kneading food | The candidate should place the dough on a clean floured surface, press it, turn it, fold it edge to edge and repeat the same process for 10 minutes to have a firm and elastic dough, then shape it into a ball, place it in an oiled bowl and cover it with cling film and let it rest enough time at warm temperature. The candidate should place fermented dough on a floured surface, cover it all sides with flour, fold it in a roll, divide roll in 250 grams cuts, reshape cuts in balls and place it in plastic rack in the freezer or refrigerator, and consumed in | ☐ | ☐
