The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 5 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 - Cutting Food

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Check if food is in good quality | The candidate should identify the acceptable quality indicators of leafy vegetables, roots, cabbage, onions and garlic, lemon and tomatoes. | ☐ | ☐
Clean and prepare food before cutting | The candidate should discard spoiled vegetables, soak, wash and dry the good ones. | ☐ | ☐

Organize appropriate cutting tools The candidate should secure the cutting board with a damp cloth, sharpen the knife, place cutting tools on a tray, and prepare the kitchen bowls. ☐ ☐
Cut food in different techniques The candidate should cut vegetables in bruncise, macedoine, Julienne, Bâtonnet, rondelle, Paysanne, Carré, Parmentier, chiffonade, as well as tourné in different sizes, mincing and grating. ☐ ☐
Store cut food for later use The candidate should store cut food in airtight containers, labelled it and rotate the inventory on the FIFO system. ☐ ☐
Criteria (*) Evidence of non-compliance if any ()
