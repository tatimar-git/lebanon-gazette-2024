Straight (muffin) method
image here
Line up a muffin pan with muffin papers
Fill each muffin cup with an equal amount of batter. Each muffin cup should be 3/4 full
Place the muffin pan in the freezer overnight
Remove the muffin pan from the freezer
Pry each frozen liner of the batter carefully from the pan
Label a food grade zip lock plastic bag with the date made and name of the flavour
Stack the frozen unbaked muffins in the bag
Place the bag on the freezer and keep it for 3 to 4 weeks
image here
Creamed batter
- Transfer creamed batter into a date labelled food grade zip lock bag
- Store in the freezer for 10 to 12 weeks and for 24 hours in the refrigerator
Biscuit batter
Split the biscuits into portions sizes
Lay the portions on a baking paper over a baking tray
Transfer the baking to the freezer for 2 hours
Take the tray out of the freezer and put the frozen biscuit in a date labelled food grade zip lock bag
Freeze biscuits for up to 2 months
