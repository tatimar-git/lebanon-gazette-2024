ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Competence Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using mechanical techniques
NQF level - 2

A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of mechanical techniques such as cutting, kneading, and mixing to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality

Competence | Ability / Learning Outcome
CU1 Setting up the workplace | • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the workplace • Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures • Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients
CU2 Cutting food | • Check if food ingredients are in good quality • Clean and prepare food before cutting • Organize appropriate cutting tools • Cut food in different techniques • Store cut food ingredients for later use
CU3 Kneading food | • Organize appropriate kneading tools and materials • Knead food properly • Store kneaded food for later use
CU4 Mixing food | • Organize appropriate mixing tools and materials • Mix food properly • Store mixed food for later use
CU5 Housekeeping the workplace | • Clean equipment and utensils • Clean and sanitize the workplace
