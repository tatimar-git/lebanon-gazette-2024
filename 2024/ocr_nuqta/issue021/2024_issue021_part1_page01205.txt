ASSESSMENT STANDARDS - UNIT 4
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 4- Mixing food | Yes / No
Are mixing bowl, scraper, whisk, pots, sauce pan, pastry cutter, and stand mixer clean and prepared ?
Organize appropriate mixing tools and materials | Are flour, sugar, corn starch, salt, baking powder, butter, vegetable oil, milk liquid and vanilla scaled in correct amount?

Mix food property Are eggs counted and ready?
Is butter soften and ready for the creaming method?
Are eggs brought to room temperature for the creaming, foaming and straight mixing methods?
Are butter and milk cold enough for the rubbing method?
Are dry ingredients required for the straight mixing method (flour, salt, sugar, vanilla and baking powder) sifted together?
Are wet ingredients required for the straight mixing method (milk, eggs and oil) combined and mixed?
Are dry ingredients combined with wet ingredients and mixed well?
Is butter beaten enough in the stand mixer for the creaming method?
Is sugar added to the beaten butter and mixed for 8 to 10 minutes?
Are eggs added one at a time and mixed for 5 minutes?
Are dry ingredients required for the creaming method (flour, salt, baking powder and vanilla) sifted?
Are dry ingredients and milk added in the required sequence (4 times dry ingredients and 3 times wet ingredients, always beginning and ending with dry ones)?
Are dry ingredients required for the rubbing method (flour, sugar and salt) sifted?
Is cold butter cut well with a pastry cutter?
Is cold milk added and incorporated well with butter?
Is the dough well kneaded?
Is dough wrapped and cooled in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes?
Is butter required for the foaming method melted in a sauce pan?
Are the eggs and sugar whisked in a pot placed over another one filled with water and placed on a source of heat?
Is the temperature mixture 43°C?


Is the egg mixture beaten in a stand mixer enough time to develop foam?
Are dry ingredients (flour and corn starch) sifted and sprinkled over the foamed mixture?
Is combined mixture folded well?
Is melted butter combined with a small amount of the foamed mixture and added to the rest of it?
Store mixed food for later use Is egg foamed mixture sealed in food grade airtight container and refrigerate for 1 week?
Is muffin tray lined up with muffin papers and filled 3/4 with the muffin mixture?
Is muffin tray place in the freezer overnight?
Are frozen muffin cups removed carefully from the tray and placed in food grade zip lock plastic bags, date and flavour labelled and frozen for 3-4 weeks?
Is creamed batter placed in a date labelled food grade zip lock plastic bag and frozen for 10-12 weeks, or refrigerated for 24 hours?
Is biscuit batter portioned into smaller sizes biscuits?
Are portions placed on a baking tray over a baking paper, then placed in the freezer for 2 hours?
Are frozen biscuits placed in a date labelled food grade zip lock plastic bag and frozen for 2 months?
