Macédoine cubes cut (small dice)
- Wash and peel the carrot
- Cut the ends of carrot
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle
- Slice rectangle into 5 mm strips
- Lay flat strips and cut into 5 mm cubes
Parmentier cubes cut (Medium dice)
- Wash and peel the carrot
- Cut the ends of carrot
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle 5 cm lengthwise
- Slice rectangle into 1 cm strips
- Lay flat strips and cut into 1 cm cubes
