ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Education Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using mechanical techniques
NQF level - 2
Overview | At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of mechanical techniques such as cutting, kneading, and mixing to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competences | CU1. Setting up the workplace CU2. Cutting food CU3. Kneading food CU4. Mixing food CU5. Housekeeping the workplace
Abilities / Learning Outcomes | • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff
