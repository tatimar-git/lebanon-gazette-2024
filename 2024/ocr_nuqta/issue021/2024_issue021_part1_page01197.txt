Rubbing (Biscuit) method
- Sift scaled dry ingredients (flour, sugar and salt) using a dough scraper
- Cut cold butter into the flour using a pastry cutter
- Add cold liquid milk
- Use the dough scraper to incorporate milk with flour
- Place dough on a floured clean surface
- Knead the dough for 20 minutes
- Wrap the dough with plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes
