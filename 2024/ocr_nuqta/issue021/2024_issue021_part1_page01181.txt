Tourné cut
Wash and peel potatoes as discussed earlier
Separate the potato in half
Square the potato into a rectangle
Split the rectangle into 4 pieces
Store them in a container with water to prevent oxidation
Turn the potato into uniform elliptical shape 7 times using a tourné knife
Decide the size depending on the recipe. Fondante is 7 to 8 cm tall and 6 to 7 cm wide, Anglaise 6 to 7 cm tall and 4 to 4.5 cm wide, Château 5 to 6 cm tall and 3 to 3.5 cm wide, Cocotte 4 to 5 cm tall and 2 cm wide

Mincing
Break the skin of garlic using the flat side of chef's knife
Pull away the skin
Chop the cloves into progressively smaller dice
Push diced garlic into a small pile
Chop again until minced
Use a garlic press to fasten up the process

Grating
- Wash zucchini
- Cut off the ends
- Remove the seeds if zucchini is greater than 5 cm in diameter
- Place the grater on a bowl
- Hold the grater with one hand and the zucchini in the other hand
- Run the zucchini up and down the coarse side of the grater while pressing down firmly
- Use a food processor for a faster result
