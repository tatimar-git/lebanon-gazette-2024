Carré Cube (large dice) not a known cut and not necessary
- Wash and peel potatoes
- Cut the ends
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle
- Cut rectangle longwise into strips 2 cm thickness
- Cut strips onto cubes at 2 cm equal sides
Pont-Neuf bâtonnets cut
- Wash and peel the carrot
image here
- Cut the ends of carrot
- Square off the sides to form a rectangle 7 cm lengthwise
- Cut rectangle longwise into strip called baton 7 cm long, 1 cm wide and 1 cm thick
Paysanne cut
- Wash and peel potatces
- Cut the ends
- Square off a 7 cm section
- Cut the section into large sticks of 1 cm
- Cut sticks into thin square slices 1mm to 2mm
Chiffonade (Shredding)
- Wash and prepare lettuce as discussed above
- Roll lettuce leaves half firmly into a cylinder
- Hold the roll of lettuce between your thumb and little finger
- Place your fingers on top
- Start slicing perpendicularly to the roll into 1 mm wide ribbons
- Separate gently the shreds using your fingertips
Rondelle
- Wash and peel carrot as discussed above
- Cut the ends
- Hold the knife perpendicular to the carrot and cut even-sized round slices
- Decide the thickness of the cut depending on the recipe
- Use a mandolin if available
