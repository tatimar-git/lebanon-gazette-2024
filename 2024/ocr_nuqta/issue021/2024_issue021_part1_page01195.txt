Straight (Muffin) method
Used to prepare muffins, waffles, and pancakes.
- Bring eggs to room temperature by take it out from the fridge 30 min ahead or covering it with warm water for 5 minutes
- Break the eggs in a separate bowl
- Scale flour, sugar, salt, vanilla and baking powder
- Scale liquid milk (room temperature)
- Scale vegetable oil
- Prepare spatula, dough scraper and balloon wire whisk
- Prepare mixing bowls
Creaming method
Used to prepare cheesecake, cookies, cakes, quick bread varieties.
- Bring eggs to room temperature by take it out from the fridge 30 min ahead or covering it with warm water for 5 minutes
- Break the eggs in a separate bowl
- Scale and soften the butter by placing it 35 minutes in room temperature before mixing
- Scale baking powder, sugar, flour, vanilla and salt
- Scale liquid milk
- Prepare mixing bowls
- Prepare spatula and dough scraper
- Prepare stand mixer
Rubbing (Biscuit) method
Used to prepare pie dough, biscuits, and puff pastry.
- Scale cold butter and cut it into small pieces
- Scale flour, salt and sugar
- Scale cold liquid milk
- Prepare mixing bowls
- Prepare pastry cutter and scraper
-
Foaming (egg foaming) method
Used to prepare meringue, sponge cake, and macaroon.
- Prepare stand mixer
- Prepare pot and sauce pan
- Prepare mixing bowls, spatula and balloon wire whisk
- Scale butter
- Bring eggs to room temperature as discussed above
* Scale flour, corn starch and sugar
Mix food properly
