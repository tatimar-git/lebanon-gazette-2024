١١٩٦ الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢١ - ٢٠٢٤/٥/٢٣
Straight (Muffin) method
- Sift scaled dry ingredients together using a dough scraper (flour, salt, sugar, vanilla and baking powder)
- Combine wet ingredients in a separate bowl (milk, eggs and oil)
- Mix wet ingredients with a wire whisk
- Combine wet ingredients with dry ingredients
- Mix until dry ingredients is moistened
- Do not over mix even if the mixture is lumpy. Over mixing will yield tough end product
Creaming method
Beat the room temperature soften butter in the stand mixer until creamy using the paddle attachment
Add sugar
Mix (cream) for 8 to 10 minutes at medium speed
Check if mixture is creamed
Add eggs one at a time to prevent curling
Mix for 5 minutes
Sift scaled dry ingredients using a dough scraper (flour, salt, baking powder and vanilla)
Add quarter of the dry ingredients
Mix on a low speed to prevent dry ingredients from blooming cut of the mixing bowl
Add third of the milk and mix
Add another quarter of the dry ingredients and mix
Add the second third of the milk and mix
Add another quarter of the dry ingredients and mix
Add the last third of the milk and mix
Add the last quarter of dry ingredients, mix until the texture becomes creamy and lumps free
