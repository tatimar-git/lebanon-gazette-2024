The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 3 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 4 - Mixing food

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Organize appropriate mixing tools and materials | The candidate should prepare clean mixing tools, weigh the correct amount of flour, warm water to the right temperature and check it with a thermometer, combine yeast with water and sugar for 10 minutes, mix dry and wet ingredients with a spatula, then hand mix it. | ☐ | ☐

Mixing food The candidate should place the dough on a clean floured surface, press it, turn it, fold it edge to edge and repeat the same process for 10 minutes to have a firm and elastic dough, then shape it into a ball, place it in a bowl , oil it and cover it with cling film and let it rest enough time at warm temperature.
Store mixed food for later use The candidate should place fermented dough on a floured surface, cover it all sides with flour, fold it in a roll, divide roll in 220 grams cuts, reshape cuts in balls, oil cuts, and place it in plastic sealed bags or airtight container in the freezer or refrigerator, and consumed in the safe time range.
