Apply food safety procedures before preparing foodstuff
Promote hand washing
- Wash hands regularly and thoroughly before touching any food at least 20 seconds under running water after scraping up well
Don't let sick employees prepare food
- Keep employees suffering from vomiting, diarrhoea or any other type of transmissible disease away from the food preparation area
Food handling safety
- Wear clean kitchen uniform and hair restraints at all times
- Avoid wearing artificial nails and jewellery
- Implement HACCP standards
Wash all produce
Wash vegetables and fruits properly to remove any bacteria and dirt that may be on the surface
Store food correctly at the right temperature
- Keep raw meat and poultry separated from vegetables, prepared sauces, and anything that requires little preparation
- Store food cooled at 4°C in a shallow pan to permit ventilation
- Freeze foods at -18°C or below
Avoid cross-contamination
- Use separate cutting boards to cut different types of food ingredients: green for vegetables and fruits, yellow for raw poultry, blue for cooked food, white for dairy products, tan for fish and seafood, and red for raw meats.
- Separate raw foods from ready-to-eat food during preparation
- Sanitize kitchen utensils and equipment
Food warming
- Reheat food to 74°C and keep it above 60°C to prevent contamination
- Place food in shallow container before refrigeration
- Never leave food at room temperature
Food quality assurance
- Inspect deliveries for quality and temperature control
- Discard spoiled or damaged food
- Move accepted deliveries to proper storage areas

Track shelf life
- Use a colour coded labelling system for proper food rotation
- Use green stickers for fresh and new produces
- Use yellow stickers for foods that should be consumed within 24 hours
- Use red stickers for food products that need to be consumed immediately
- Discard food products that past their expiration date
Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the workplace
