Foaming (egg foaming) method
- Place scaled butter in a saucepan and melt
- Pour sugar into a mixing bowl
- Add the eggs
- Put some water in a pot and place on a bumer
- Place eggs and sugar bowl over the water pot
- Whisk eggs and sugar together
- Mix constantly so the eggs proteins do not cook
- Check mixture temperature with a thermometer to ensure it is 43°C
- Place the egg mixture in the mixing bowl of a stand mixer
- Mix on high speed until the foam develops then lower to medium speed
- Sift scaled dry ingredients (flour and corn starch)
- Stop mixing when the foam has doubled 3 times in volume and the mixing bowl is cold (the foam is ready also when you are able to draw a figure 8)
- Sprinkle sifted dry ingredients over the foam while folding
- Add a small amount of the batter to the melted butter and mix all together
- Add the mixture to the rest of the batter and fold the mix until ingredients are combined
