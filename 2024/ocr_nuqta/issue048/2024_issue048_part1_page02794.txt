Article-7
PERIOD OF EXECUTION
7.1 The Requested Party shall take all necessary measures to ensure as much as possible, fast and complete execution of the Request. As a rule, the Request will be expected to be executed within 30 days from the date of receipt.
7.2 The Requested Party may allow the authorized representatives of the Requesting Party to be present during the execution of such request in its country, if it does not contravene the national laws of the Requested Party.
Article-8
DENIAL OF REQUEST
8.1 The Request may be denied, fully or partially, in case the Requested Party believes that its execution could prejudice sovereignty, security or other essential interest of its State or contravenes its national laws and regulations.
