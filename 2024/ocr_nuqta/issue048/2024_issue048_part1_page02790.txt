AGREEMENT
BETWEEN
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF LEBANON
AND
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE ISLAMIC REPUBLIC OF
PAKISTAN
ON
COOPERATION IN COMBATING ILICIT TRAFFICKING AND ABUSE OF NARCOTIC DRUGS, PSYCHOTROPIC SUBSTANCES, NEW PSYCHOACTIVE SUBSTANCES AND THEIR PRECURSORS
The Government of the Republic of Lebanon and the Government of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (hereinafter, referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as "the Parties");
Desiring to strengthen the existing friendly relation between the two countries;
Expressing concern in the scaled expansion in abuse of illicit trafficking as well as narcotics drugs, psychotropic substances, new psychoactive substances (NPS) and their precursor chemicals (hereinafter, referred to as "drugs and their precursors");
Conscious that illicit trafficking of drugs, their precursors and drug
money laundering is seriously jeopardizing public health, safety and wealth of the citizens of the Parties:
Acting in the spirit of partnership, cooperation and taking into consideration provisions stipulated in the Single Convention on Narcotics Drugs of 1961 as amended by the Protocol of 1972, the Convention on Psychotropic Substances of 1971, the United Nations Conventions against Illicit Traffic in Narcotics Drugs and Psychotropic Substances of 1988, for cooperation in combating illicit trafficking and abuse of Drugs and their precursor chemicals;
Proceeding from mutual interest to take effective measures to combat illicit trafficking of drugs and their precursor chemicals:
Being guided by international obligations and respective national laws of the Parties:
