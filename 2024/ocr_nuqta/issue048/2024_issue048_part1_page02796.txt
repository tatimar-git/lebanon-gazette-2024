Article-11
EXCHANGE OF VISITS
11. In order to ensure proper implementation of this Agreement and review cooperation process, covered under this Agreement, and to develop guidelines for its further improvement, the Parties, with mutual consent, may organize the visits of delegations, dialogue, meetings, consultations and workshops. Such meetings will be conducted alternatively in the two countries at the venue and on the dates as mutually agreed between the Parties.
Article-12
SETTLEMENT OF DISPUTES
12. In case of any difference or dispute arising out of or concerning with the application or the interpretation of this Agreement shall be settled amicably through consultations or negotiations between the Parties.
Article-13
EXPENSES RELATING TO JOINT SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH ETC.
13.1 The expenditure in respect of technical, material and advisory assistance as well as expert assistance will be borne by the Requesting Party.
13.2 The expenses in connection with carrying out joint scientific research of mutual interest on problems of combating illicit trafficking of drugs and their precursors will be shared equally unless the Parties agree otherwise.
Article-14
AMENDMENT OR MODIFICATION
14.1 The Parties may amend or modify this Agreement at any time, through mutual written consent, with prior written notice of three months. Such amendment or modification shall be executed through separate Protocol and form an integral part of this Agreement.
14.2 The amendment or modification shall enter into force in the like manner as provided in Article 15.1 of this Agreement.
