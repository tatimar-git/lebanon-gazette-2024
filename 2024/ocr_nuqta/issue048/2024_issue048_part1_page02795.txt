8.3 If the Requested Party believes that immediate execution of the Request could prevent criminal prosecution or any other procedure being carried out on the territory in its country, that Party may postpone execution of the Request or execute the Request subject to certain conditions, after having consultation with the Requesting Party. Article-9
CONFIDENTIALITY
9.1 The Parties will maintain the confidentiality of information, documents and material which will be mutually shared between them.
9.2 The Requested Party will use the information provided by the Requesting Party only for the purposes stated in the Request.
9.3 The information provided under this Agreement shall not be shared with any third party without prior written consent of the Party which has provided such information.
Article-10
COSTS
10.1 Each Party shall bear expenses incurred in its territory on the execution of requests under this Agreement.
10.2 Each Party will bear all costs connected with visits and accommodation of its representatives in the territory of the other Party unless otherwise mutually agreed between the Parties.
10.3 All visits of the Requesting Party representative shall take place in accordance with the prior approval of the Requested Party.
10.4 For the visits related to training, the cost connected with travel and boarding will be borne by the Party seeking training whereas the cost of training will be borne by the host Party.
