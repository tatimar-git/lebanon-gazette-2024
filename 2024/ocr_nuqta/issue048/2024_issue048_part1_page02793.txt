Article-4
FOCAL POINTS
4.1 The Parties shall share detailed contacts of focal points for sharing any information related to this Agreement and operational and routine information, as hereunder: -
(a) In case of Islamic Republic of Pakistan: Ministry of Narcotics Control, Pakistan
(b) In case of the Republic of Lebanon: Internal Security Forces, Ministry of Interior and Municipalities
4.2 In case of any change in the focal points, the concerned Party shall inform the other of such change through diplomatic channels in writing.
Article-5
INTERACTION
5. The Parties, as per their respective laws and the UN Convention against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances of 1988, will interact and mutually assist each other to take measures in countering illicit drugs and their precursors' trafficking.
Article-6
PROCEDURE IN ASSISTANCE
6.1 The Parties shall assist each other and shall provide upon request or by initiative of one of the Parties, such assistance which is in the interest of the other Party.
