Article-3
EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION
3 The Parties agree that timely exchange of information shall be carried out
on the following through designated focal points and when necessary under
the supervision of the competent judicial authority:
(a) any illicit drug trafficking offence already committed or in the process
of being committed on the territory of the other Party;
(b) individuals suspected in illicit drugs trafficking.
(c) exchange of intelligence related to transportation of illicit drugs from the territory of one Party to the territory of the other Party;
(d) exchange of information on drug trafficking organizations (DTOs) that are active between both countries, including these organizations' structure, their connections, activities and any other significant information;
(e) exchange of information on latest trends, methods of concealment and modes of transportation of drugs and their precursors;
(f) money laundering channels and details about assets/amounts derived from illicit trade of drugs and precursors related activities;
