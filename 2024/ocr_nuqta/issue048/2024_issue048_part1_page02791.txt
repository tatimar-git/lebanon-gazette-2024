Article-1
SCOPE
1.1 The Parties shall cooperate with each other in combating trafficking of drugs and their precursors while remaining within the parameters of International obligations and their territorial jurisdiction under respective national laws.
1.2 The provision of this Agreement shall apply to legal assistance in cases pertaining to drugs and their precursors and drug related money laundering.
Article-2
FORMS OF COOPERATION
2.1 Cooperation of the Parties under this Agreement shall be realized in the following forms: -
(a) exchange of information in respect of countering illicit trafficking of drugs and their precursors through designated focal points;
(b) carrying out counter narcotics intelligence activities against trafficking and illicit trade of drugs and their precursors by one Party upon the request of other Party;
(c) exchange of experiences and information on drug abuse, prevention and drug trafficking activities:
(d) interacting and mutually assisting each other while carrying out coordinated operations including controlled delivery operations;
(c) assisting each other by sharing results of backtracking of cases bearing linkages with the other Party;
(f) assisting each other in carrying out drug related anti-money laundering investigations and tracing/confiscation of assets derived from illicit trade of drugs and precursors related activities;
(g) exchange of experiences and best practices including holding of bilateral meetings, conferences, workshops and seminars;
