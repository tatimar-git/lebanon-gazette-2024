Mutual Agreement N° 15/1
Date: 2024/3/26
WHEREAS the Litigation Department at the Ministry of Justice, in its capacity as the legal representative of the Republic of Lebanon, has become aware of a criminal complaint filed in Germany against the former Governor of the Central Bank of Lebanon, Mr. Riad Salameh, and anyone else the investigation reveals as an accomplice on charges of money laundering. As a result, his movable and immovable assets have been seized. Following this claim, the foreign judicial authorities have frozen assets belonging to the Governor in Germany.
WHEREAS in the event that the crime is proven, these frozen assets are the right of the Republic of Lebanon, which necessitates the Republic joining the aforementioned claim in order to protect its interests.
WHEREAS the Litigation Department, in accordance with the foregoing, has begun the process of searching for a German lawyer to represent the Republic of Lebanon before the German courts in the investigation file being conducted by German investigating Judge Ambika Zeeb against the former Governor of the Central Bank of Lebanon Riad Salameh, his brother Raja Salameh, and anyone else the investigation reveals as an accomplice (case number 323 Js 163576/21), without any fees in order to prevent the Republic of Lebanon from bearing this cost in these difficult economic circumstances that Lebanon is going through. To this end, on December 19, 2023, we sent a letter of invitation to tender to both Asas Law Firm and Mr. Killian Wegner, who specializes in money laundering crimes.
WHEREAS Mr. Wegner has expressed his willingness to represent Lebanon in this case, free of charge, with the assistance of Mr. Mohamad El Ghazi.
Based on the above,
And pursuant to the provisions of Article 46 of Law No. 244 dated 2021/07/19, which includes the Public Purchase Law,
And to the provisions of Article 19 of the Law organizing the Ministry of Justice,
And based on the proposal of the Director General of the Ministry of Justice,
And after consulting the President of the Litigation Department in the Ministry of Justice
