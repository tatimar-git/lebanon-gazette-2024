The technique of blind baking involves baking the dough fully by itself (weighed to keep its shape) so that it can be served with unbaked fillings, like pastry cream and fruits. It is a common technique used in making tart and pie.
- Place a parchment paper in the oiled bottom of a tart pan (no need for that step at all)
- Remove the pie dough from the refrigerator 30 minutes before using it
- Flatten the pie dough with a rolling pin on a floured surface
- Place the dough in the tart pan and drop the excess outside the pan to cover the edges
- Roll the rolling pin over the top of the tin to remove excess pastry
- Pierce the tart using a fork to prevent air bubbles that form during baking
- Let it rest for about 1 hour in the fridge
- Cover with a parchment paper
- Add dry pasta or dry pulses on top of the parchment paper to weigh it down
- Bake for 15 minutes in a preheated oven at a temperature of 180°C on the centre rack
- Remove from the oven and take off the pasta and parchment paper
- Bake for another 5 minutes
- Let it cool completely before using it in the desired recipe
