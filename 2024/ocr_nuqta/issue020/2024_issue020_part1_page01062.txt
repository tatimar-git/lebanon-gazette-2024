1- Braising meat
- Season the meat with salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Add vegetable oil into a pot and turn the heat on
- Sear the meat until it turns deep brown
- Remove the meat from the pot and set aside
- Add the aromatic garnish to the pot and sear until brown and soft
- Deglaze with red wine
- Scrape the bottom of the pan to release the brown bits
- Place the meat back in the pot and the bouquet garri
- Add stock or water halfway up the sides of the meat
- Bring to a boil then immediately lower to a simmer
- Cover the pot and keep it at low simmer cook on stovetop or in the oven at 180°C
- Check it occasionally. The liquid level should be the same. Add more if needed
- Let it braise for 1.5 to 3 hours to become fork tender
- Remove from the pot and serve
Stew meal
Stewing is a combination cooking method that uses small, uniform pieces of meat that are totally immersed in liquid and slowly simmered. The food and the liquid are served together as one dish.
1- Difference between braising and stewing
- Stews require food to be cut into uniform small pieces, while braising uses larger cuts of meat
- Food is completely submerged in liquid during stewing, while in braising the least amount of liquid is required
2- Stewing meat
- Cut meat into small uniform pieces
- Season with salt and pepper
- Heat vegetable oil in a large pot
- Add meat cuts to brown it
- Continue turning the meat for even browning
- Add the aromatic garnish to the pot and sear until brown and soft
- Add bouquet gami (thyme, bay leaves and cinnamon stick)
- Deglaze the pan with red wine and reduce it until nearly dry
- Add stock enough to submerge the meat
- Cover the pot and bring to simmer until the meat becomes tender ( 45 minutes up to 3 hours depending on the size of meat cubes )
- Serve garnished with fresh herbs (parsley)
N.B:
- Wine can be substituted with beer
- Some recipes use tomato sauce instead of stock
- You can add vegetables for the stew during cooking such as carrots, peas and celery
- Meat can be floured before browning in oil
