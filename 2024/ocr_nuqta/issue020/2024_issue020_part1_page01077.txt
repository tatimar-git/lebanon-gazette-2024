FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 5- Infusion and Steeping | Yes / No
Infuse beverages | Is mint de-stemmed?
Are leaves placed in the teapot infuser then submerged with hot water?
Is teapot covered with the lid?
Is mint left to infuse for 15 minutes before removing the infuser and serving?
Are coffee press and mugs preheated?
Are coffee beans ground to coarse or medium coarse?
Is water boiled and left to rest for 45 seconds?

Is ground coffee added to the coffee press after emptying it?
Are water and ground coffee combined together in right proportion as 8 grams of coffee per 118 ml of water?
Is the French press covered and mixture left to sit for 4 minutes?
Is the infuser pressed down slowly then coffee served?
Steep beverages and food Is the difference between infusion and steeping identified?
Are green tea leaves placed in the teapot infuser?
Is enough hot water added to submerge the green tea and teapot covered?
Is the mixture steeped for 15 minutes first before serving and the infuser is kept throughout the service?
Is lavender placed in jars and stems taken off?
Is honey added and the jar closed tightly?
Are honey and lavender left to steep for 2 weeks?
