ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Competence Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using cooking techniques
NQF level - 2

A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of cooking techniques such as baking, blind baking, boiling, blanching, braising, stewing, infusion, steeping, pressure cooking, simmering and steaming to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
