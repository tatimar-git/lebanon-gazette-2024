Boil food
Boiling is a moist-heat method that cooks food in boiling water or other water-based liquids at a temperature of 100°C. It is used for cooking pasta, rice, eggs, and vegetables.
- Bring water to the boil
- Add salt to the boiling water
- Add the food
- Boil as long as necessary
There are 2 basic types of boils:
a) Slow boil: It is reached when the water bubbles across the entire surface, but without the intensity of a full boil. Bubbles are generally large and slow-moving. The slow boil temperature is 96°C.
b) Full boil: aka rolling boil or real boil, it happens when all the water in the pot is fast-moving in waves of bubbles and giving off steam. A full boil temperature is 100°C.
