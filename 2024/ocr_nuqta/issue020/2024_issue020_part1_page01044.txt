١٠٤٤ الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٠ - ٢٠٢٤/٥/١٦
- Baking is the process of cooking by dry heat , typically in an oven
- It is probably the oldest cooking method
- Bakery products, which include bread, cookies, pies, rolls, pastries and muffins are cooked by baking
- Main ingredients are flour, leavening agent (yeast, baking soda or baking powder), shortening (butter, margarine, soybean oil), liquids (water, milk), eggs, and sweeteners
1- Bake Bread
- Scale your ingredients (flour, water, yeast and salt) on the basis of flour 100%, salt 2%, water 65%, and yeast 1.5%
- Mix ingredients until fully incorporated
- Check the dough temperature using a thermometer. It should be at room temperature for a good fermentation
- Proceed with bulk fermentation for 2 hours to give the dough volume, porous structure and add flavour by placing it in a bowl covered with plastic wrap
- Fold the dough over after 1 hour of bulk fermentation
- Rest the dough for 1 hour
- Divide the dough into pieces using a scale according to their pre-baked weight (340 grams for baguette; 450 grams for round bread)
- Pre-shape the dough by loosely moulding it into forms that roughly resemble the final shape
- Let the dough shapes rest on a bench for 15 minutes covered with a clean towel to protect the surface from drying-out
- Shape the dough into a smooth spherical lobe with light seams for round bread
- Place round shaped dough in baskets to preserve their shape during the next phase of fermentation
- Flatten the dough to degas a bit if you are preparing a baguette
- Take the bottom edge and tuck it in about 2/3 of the way up
- Fold over the top-edge and press it down to create a seam
- Seal the dough by folding the long of the piece using the thumb and heel of your hand
- Roll the dough into a cylinder shape
- Place the baguette in narrow channels formed in pieces of canvas cloth in order to preserve its shape
- Flatten the dough and fold it over itself in a rectangular shape for pan bread
- Place the dough in a metal loaf pan to rest
- Place round rolls and oblong ones on a clean baking tray seam down. Don't crowd them
- Proof the shaped dough by placing it on racks covered with plastic wraps in order to ferment for the last time at a temperature of 23°C and 75% humidity for 1.5 to 2 hours (automatic proofer can be also used). The dough will double its volume in about an hour and half
- Place the proofed pieces on a baking sheet or tray
- Score the dough with a bread lame. It allows the bread to expand to its maximum potential and to retain rigidity and proper shape during baking
- Bake the bread dough in a preheated oven at a temperature of 230°C for around 25 to 40 minutes depending on the size of the dough and the type of oven being used. Baking converts the dough into an edible product
- Vent the oven 3 minutes before the end of baking to release some of the humidity and prevent burning the crust by placing a wooden spoon. This is not applicable in professional bakeries
- Stop baking when the bread reaches a brown colour and crispness
- Tap on to hear a resonant noise
- Let the bread load cool down on cooling racks or in cooling baskets vertically placed
- Check the quality of the bread once it is cooled by looking at the following: shape, colour, crust, slice, aroma, crumb, taste and overall impression
