Braise meat
Braising is a technique that uses both dry and moist heat cooking. First, the food is usually seared at high temperature to brown and give it a nice crust, then a small amount of liquid is added and the temperature is turned down low, to cook for a longer amount of time. It is mostly used with less tender cuts of meals as it can also be used with vegetables.
Once the initial browning occurs, flavour is intensified.
When liquid is added, heat, moisture and cooking time breaks down connective tissues into gelatine and softens muscle fibre for a moist and very tender dish.
There are different braising methods:
a- Stove top method
b- Oven braising
c- Slow cooker
