During on the job training the following job activities will be carried out:
- Identify hazards and food contaminants, use of PPE and work clothing, apply personal hygiene habits (5 hours)
- Bake and blind bake food (10 hours)
- Boil and blanch different foods (10 hours)
- Braise and stew meat (10 hours)
- Infuse and steep different types of beverages and food (10 hours)
- Cook food in a pressure cooker (10 hours)
- Simmer food (10 hours)
- Steam food in different means(10 hours)
- Clean and housekeep the workplace (5 hours)
