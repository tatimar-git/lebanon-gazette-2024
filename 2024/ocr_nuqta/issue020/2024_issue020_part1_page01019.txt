ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Occupational Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using cooking techniques
NQF level - 2
The occupation covers the process of preparing food and meal ingredients using a variety of cooking techniques such as baking, blind baking, boiling, blanching, braising, stewing, infusing, steeping, pressure cooking, simmering and steaming to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
