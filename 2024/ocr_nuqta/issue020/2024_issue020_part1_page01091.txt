Steaming food in a steam oven
image here
A steam oven combines baking with steaming in only one appliance, creating a neatly balanced act of crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside.
With steam oven you can choose the exact temperature you need by using the integrated touch display. Moreover, the built-in water tank adds steam continuously, ensuring that your food remains moist and nutritious.
Steaming pasta (I won't at all recommend this dish to represent the requested technique)
- Place 400 grams of spaghetti on a baking tray
- Add sliced onions and minced garlic
- Add cherry tomatoes
- Sprinkle two teaspoons of salt and 1/4 teaspoon of black pepper
- Add 2.5 cups of water
- Turn on the steam oven and select steam cooking mode
- Cook for 10 minutes
- Remove from the oven and add some olive oil
- Loosen the pasta with a salad tong and combine all ingredients together
- Serve in a plate
- Add fresh basil
- Grate parmesan cheese
- Drizzle some olive oil
Grilled cauliflower:
- Place 1 whole cauliflower in a baking tray on a parchment paper
- Season with salt and pepper
- Drizzle with some olive oil
- Bake with steaming for around 20 min.
Steam food in an electric steamer
An electric steamer is a sealed pot with a set of tiers stacked one on top of each other. It is designed to cook food in the heat of the steam. It is a very convenient way to cook food for many reasons:
- Saves time. It cooks food in a quick period and is easy to use
- Requires minimal supervision. Comes with a timer
- A healthy way of cooking. Locks in most of the nutrients and retains the natural vitamins and minerals successfully
- Preserves the structure of the food. Veggies retain their structure and colour
- Requires no oil to cook as it uses steam to cook the food
- Considered by many to be the healthiest cooking method
• Does not require much clean-up and is less messy compared to other cooking methods
