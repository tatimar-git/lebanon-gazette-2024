The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 2 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 - Baking and Blind Baking
Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES

Bake food The candidate should scale and combine ingredients at room temperature, place the dough in a bowl covered with plastic wrap to ferment, fold the dough after 1 hour and let it ferment for another hour, divide the dough into scaled pieces, shape the pieces and let it bench rest for 15 minutes covered with a cloth, flatten the dough to degas it, proof the dough for 1.5 to 2 hours to ferment for the last time, score the dough, bake the dough in a preheated oven at 230°C for 25 to 40 minutes, vent the oven 3 minutes before the end of baking, remove the bread and place it on cooling racks.
Blind Bake food The candidate should remove the dough 30 minutes from the refrigerator before processing it, flatten it with a rolling pin and place it in a tart pan, remove excess pastry, pierce the dough with a fork, let it rest for 1 hour in the fridge, place parchment paper and top it with dry pasta or pulses, bake the dough in a preheated oven at 220°C for 15 minutes, remove parchment paper and past and bake for 5 minutes, let the pie crust cool enough time.
