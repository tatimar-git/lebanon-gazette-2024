Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 3- Boiling and Blanching | Yes / No
Are the steps to boil food identified?
Are the two types of boiling identified?
Are 2 litres of water and salt brought to boil in a pot?
Is pasta added and boiled for 8-10 minutes?
Is pasta stirred during boiling to prevent it from sticking?
Is boiled pasta drained?

Boil Food Are eggs removed from the refrigerator 30 minutes before boiling them?
Is enough water boiled and heat turned low before adding eggs?
Are eggs added to the boiling water using a skimmer?
Is timing set depending on the type of eggs desired?
Are eggs removed from boiling water and placed in ice water bath?
Is the difference between boiling roots vegetables and those that grow above the ground recognized?
Are vegetables washed, peeled and cut in same sizes?
Are root vegetables placed in cold salted water and brought to boil with pot covered?
Is heat turned low and vegetables checked with a pairing knife, then drained and cooled in ice water bath ?
Is water brought to boil before adding broccoli?
Blanch food Are broccolis added to the boiling water and covered? It shouldn't be covered at all
Is heat turned low and broccolis left to become tender, then drained?
Is the cooking time for different types of vegetables identified?
Are the advantages of blanching vegetables recognized?
Are tomatoes washed, cored and crossed in the bottom?
Is water brought to boil?
Are tomatoes added to the boiling water and left enough time for the skin to start peeling off?
Are blanched tomatoes cooled in ice water bath?
Are tomatoes peeled?
