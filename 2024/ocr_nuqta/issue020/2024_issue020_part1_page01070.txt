Infusion is the process of extracting flavours from food into a liquid. This is usually done by soaking the food into the liquid for a period of time. French press coffee, tea and herbs infusions are all examples of infusions. Infusion is also called brewing.
1- Mint tea infusion
- Remove the mint leaves from the stems
- Gather the leaves in one pile
- Slice the leaves finely (no need)
- Add the mint leaves to the teapot infuser
- Add hot water
- Cover the teapot
- Let it infuse for 15 minutes
- Serve the tea
