Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 8- Steaming | Yes / No
Steam food in a steamer basket | Are vegetables washed and cut in even sizes?
Is enough water poured in the steaming pot?
Is the basket placed in the pot and the steamer pre-heated?
Are vegetables placed in the basket and the pot closed?
Is heat lowered to a minimum level?
Are vegetables cooked for few minutes until they are fork tender?
Is gas turned off and vegetables removed from the steamer?
Are vegetables prepared and placed in a heat-resistant bowl?
Is the bowl sealed with a microwave-safe plastic wrap?

Steam food in the microwave Are vegetables cooked in the microwave for 3-5 minutes?
Is the microwave opened and plastic wrap punctured?
Is the bowl unwrapped?
Is the oven preheated to 200 °C?
Is lemon sliced thinly?
Is fish fillet seasoned and placed on a parchment paper?
Steam food in a paper bag Are 2 slices of lemon placed on the fish fillet along with 2 sprigs of thyme and a slice of butter?
Is the outside of the parchment paper brushed with egg white?
Is a quarter cup of white wine added?
Is the parchment paper folded in half then sealed starting from one corner and proceeding all the way around the fish?
Is the fish placed on a baking tray then baked in the oven for 10-12 minutes?
Is spaghetti placed on a baking tray then sliced onions, garlic and cherry tomatoes added?
Is spaghetti seasoned with salt and pepper?
Is water added?
Steam food in a steam oven Is the steam oven turned on and steam mode selected?
Are ingredients cooked for 10 minutes?
Is olive oil sprinkled and steamed pasta loosened and combined with all other ingredients?
Is pasta served in a plate and topped with fresh basil, grated parmesan cheese and some drizzles of olive oil?
Is rice rinsed well with cold water?
Is rice soaked for 30 minutes in water then drained?
Are 200 ml of water added to the rinsed rice?


Steam food in an electric steamer Is the steamer reservoir filled with 1 litre of water and all compartments assembled?
Is the rice bowl placed in the compartment and steamer closed?
Is rice steamed for 30 minutes?
Is the rice left to rest for 5 minutes after cooking then removed from the steamer and served?
