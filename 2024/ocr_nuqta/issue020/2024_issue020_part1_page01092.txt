١٠٩٢ الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٠ - ٢٠٢٤/٥/١٦
Steaming rice in an electric steamer
- Put 170 grams of rice in a bowl
- Rinse thoroughly with cold water
- Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes. It helps the grains cook evenly
- Drain the water after soaking
- Add 200 ml of water
- Add 1 litre of water to the reservoir
- Assemble the steamer compartments
- Place the rice bowl in the steamer compartment and close the steamer
- Cook for 30 minutes
- Let it rest for 5 minutes after cooking
- Remove the rice from the steamer and serve
