ASSESSMENT STANDARDS – UNIT 6
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 6- Pressure cooking | Yes / No
Cook food under high pressure steam | Is caramel prepared in a casserole on the stove by combining water and sugar in a heat resistant bowl?
Is caramel swirled to coat the bottom half of the bowl?
Is caramel left to cool on the counter?
Are whole eggs whisked with vanilla extract?
Are sugar, lemon, orange peel and whole milk added to the whisked eggs and whisked?
Is mixture strained into the caramel bowl?
Is 1.5 cup of water added to the pressure cooker?
Is the bowl covered with aluminium foil then transferred into the pressure cooker over the steaming tray?
Is the pressure cooker set at steam mode low pressure for 12 minutes then left to release for 10 minutes?

Is the bowl removed from the cooker along with the aluminium foil then left to cool on the counter for 1 hour?
Is the bowl transferred to the fridge to chill for 6 hours?
Is the flan loosen with a sharp then flipped into a serving plate and served?
