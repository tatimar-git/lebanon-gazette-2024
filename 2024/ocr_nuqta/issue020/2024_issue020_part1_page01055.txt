Blanch food
Blanching is a two-step technique that consists of plunging something into a big pot of boiling water for a very short period of time, seconds or few minutes, then quickly moving it to an ice water bath (a bowl filled with water and ice).
Advantages:
- Lessen bitterness in veggies especially for kale and cabbage
- Stop an enzyme action in plants that causes aging, discoloration and tastelessness. This is a very crucial step when freezing vegetables
- Enhance colour and lock in fresh flavour in green vegetables like green beans and broccoli
- Remove skins of fresh peaches, tomatoes or almonds
- Pre-cooks vegetables that are going to be sautéed or grilled
1- Blanching tomatoes
- Put water on boil
- Wash the tomatoes
- Cut out the core and cross the bottom
- Add the tomatoes and leave for 1 minute more or less
- Once you notice the tomatoes skin is starting to peel remove directly to an ice water bath
- Peel off the tomatoes skin
