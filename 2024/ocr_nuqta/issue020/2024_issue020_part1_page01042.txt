The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 8 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 1 - Setting up the work area

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures | The candidate should identify electrical, food contaminants and other types of hazards that may emerge in the work place, display the adequate warning sign, wear PPE, apply proper lifting technique, wear protective gear prevent their occurrence. | ☐ | ☐
Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures | The candidate should use different utensils and tools when preparing and storing raw and cooked foods to prevent cross-contamination. | ☐ | ☐

Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen The candidate should wear clean working clothes and shoes in the correct way by starting with covering the hair and face, then putting on the jacket, pants and apron, then wearing the clean working non-slippery shoes, then tying the neckerchief, and at the end wearing the clean gloves if needed. ☐ ☐
Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff The candidate should inform the supervisor if they feel sick, wash their hands with soap and warm water for 20 seconds and dry them well before starting any task in the production facility, wear clean uniform, remove jewelleries and nail polish, wash all fresh produce, separate raw meats from vegetables, keep foods safe from the Food Danger Zone, use different cutting boards for every type of ingredient, use colour label to track the shelf life of food, inspect deliveries for quality and temperature control. ☐ ☐
Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the workplace The candidate should refrain from coming to work if feeling sick, wearing jewellery and perfume, refrain from smoking, spitting, or coughing in the food processing area over uncovered food. The candidate should keep their body, hair, teeth, hands, and fingers clean, keep their hair and face hair covered, and wounds covered. ☐ ☐
Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures The candidate should identify cleaning chemical risks, wear protective gear when using chemicals, use the correct amount of chemical, store cleaning chemicals correctly well labelled, read the instructions written on the Material Safety Data Sheet before using the chemical. ☐ ☐
Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients The candidate should sanitize all tools, food contact surfaces and equipment with a sanitizing solution to minimize bacterial growth and prevent cross contamination. ☐ ☐
