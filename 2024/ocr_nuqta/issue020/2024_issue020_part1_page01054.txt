1- Boiling Pasta
- Pour 2 litres of water into a pot
- Add 1 tablespoon of salt (after water boils)
- Bring to boil
- Add 250 grams of pasta
- Cook for 8 to 10 minutes (cooking time will depend on the kind of pasta quality)
- Stir with a exoglass spoon during the first 2 minutes to prevent the pasta from sticking
- Drain the pasta
2- Boiling eggs
- Remove the eggs from the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking them
- Bring enough water to boil. Add a pinch of salt and vinegar
- Turn the heat to low while you add the eggs to prevent them from bouncing and cracking
- Put the eggs in the boiling water using a skimmer
- Start the timer depending on the type of eggs you want: 6 minutes for soft-boiled eggs, 9 minutes for hard-boiled eggs
- Remove the eggs and place in ice water or under cold running water to cool down quickly
3- Boiling vegetables
As a rule of thumb, vegetables that grow underground (potatoes, beetroots, carrots, sweet potato) goes into cold water, whereas vegetables that grow above the ground (asparagus, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, kale, peas, etc.) goes into boiling water.
a- Cold water start:
- Wash vegetables, peel if necessary and slice into pieces about the same size
- Place root vegetables in a pot or saucepan
- Add enough salted water to cover
- Cover the pot with the lid, bring to boil as quickly as possible
- Reduce the heat and simmer gently until tender when tested with a fork or point of knife
- Drain and cool down vegetables under cold running water
b- Boiling water start:
- Wash vegetables and slice into pieces about the same size
- Slice a cross in the base of Brussels sprouts, and slice cauliflower and broccoli into florets
- Place 2-3 cm of lightly salted water in a saucepan and bring to the boil
- Add vegetables. If vegetables are not just covered with water, add more boiling water (boil enough water to cover the vegetables)
- Simmer gently until vegetables are tender
- Drain and serve
c- Cooking Time
- Leafy vegetables: 3-5 minutes (kale, spinach, cabbage)
- Firmer vegetables: 8-10 minutes (broccoli, peas, cauliflower)
- Harder vegetables: 12-15 minutes (carrots, pumpkin, turnips)
- Starchy vegetables: 18-20 minutes (potato, sweet potato)
