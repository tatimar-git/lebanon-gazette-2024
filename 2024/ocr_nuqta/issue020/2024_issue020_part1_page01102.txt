The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in the 5 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 8 - Steaming
Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Steam food in a steamer basket | The candidate should prepare the vegetables in even sizes, pour 2 cm of water in the steaming pot, place the basket, close the pot and pre-heat it, add the vegetables to the basket, airtight cover the steamer, lower the gas to a minimum level, steam the vegetables until fork tender, turn off the gas and remove the vegetables. | ☐ | ☐
Steam food in the microwave | The candidate should prepare the vegetables in even sizes, place the vegetables in a heat-resistant bowl, seal the bowl with a microwave-safe plastic wrap, cook in the microwave for 3-5 minutes, puncture the plastic wrap and carefully unwrap the bowl.

Steam food in a paper bag The candidate should preheat the oven to 200 °C, slice lemon thinly, season fish fillets with salt and pepper, place the fillets on parchment paper, top with lemon slices, thyme and butter, add some white wine, brush the paper with egg whites, fold the paper in half, start folding from one corner and proceed all around the fish, place the pocket on a baking tray, and bake for 10-12 minutes.
Steam food in a steam oven The candidate should place spaghetti on a baking tray, add sliced onions, garlic and cherry tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, add 2.5 cups of water, set the steam mode in the oven, place the baking tray in the steam oven, bake for 10 minutes, remove the tray from the oven, drizzle some olive oil, loosen the pasta, combine it with all ingredients, serve in a dish, add fresh basil, grated parmesan cheese, and sprinkle olive oil.
Steam food in an electric steamer The candidate should rinse 170 grams of rice several times, drain the water, soak for 30 minutes, drain the water, add 200 ml of water, fill the steamer reservoir with 1 litre of water, assemble all compartments, place the rice bowl in a compartment, cook for 30 minutes, let the rice rest for 5 minutes after cooking, remove and serve.
