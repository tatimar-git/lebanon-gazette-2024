Steep beverages and food
Steeping is the process of soaking an organic solid, such as leaves, in a liquid to extract flavours.
Infusions are prepared through the process of steeping, the main difference is that in steeping the leaves are kept in water for the entire service of a tea for example, whereas in infusion the amount of time tea leaves staying in water is very short. Only non-fermented or slightly fermented teas can be steeped (green and white tea).
1- Steep loose leaf green tea
- Add green tea leaves to the teapot infuser
- Add hot water (80° C) enough to submerge the tea leaves. You can use a digital thermometer to check the water temperature
- Cover the teapot
- Wait for 15 minutes
- Serve while leaving the infuser in the teapot throughout the service
2- Herb honey
- Place lavender in the jar
- Trim off the stems
- Fill the jar with honey
- Close tight the jar with the lid
- Steep the honey for at least 2 weeks
N.B: Other herbs and aromatics can be used such as rosemary, vanilla, or cinnamon
