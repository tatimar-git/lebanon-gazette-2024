ASSESSMENT STANDARDS - UNIT 4
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 4- Braising and Stewing | Yes / No
Braise meat | Are different means of braising identified?
Is meat seasoned and added to the pot?
Is meat seared well in oil until browning?
Is meat removed from the pot and set aside?
Are cut vegetables added to the pot and seared until they become softer?
Is the pot deglazed with red wine and scrapped ?

Is meat placed again in the pot?
Is stock or water added just enough to cover the sides of the meat?
Is pot brought to boil then simmered?
Are aromatics ingredients added and the pot covered?
Is the pot covered and kept on simmering until the meat becomes tender for 1.5 to 3 hours?
Stew meat Is the difference between braising and stewing identified?
Is meat cut in uniform small pieces then added to the heated vegetable oil in a pot?
Is meat seasoned with salt and pepper and seared until browning?
Are cut vegetables added to the pot and seared until they become softer?
Are aromatic herbs added?
Is the pot deglazed with red wine?
Is stock added enough to fully submerge the meat?
Is pot covered and left to simmer enough time for the meat to becomes tender?
