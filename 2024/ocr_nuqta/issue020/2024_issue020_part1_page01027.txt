EDUCATION STANDARD

ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers | Education Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using cooking techniques
ISCO-08-5120 Cooks
NQF level - 2

Overview At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of cooking techniques such as baking, blind baking, boiling, blanching, braising, stewing, infusing, steeping, pressure cooking, simmering and steaming, to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competences CU1. Setting up the workplace CU2. Baking and Blind Baking CU3. Boiling and Blanching CU4. Braising and Stewing CU5. Infusing and Steeping CU6. Pressure cooking CU7. Simmering CU8. Steaming CU9. Housekeeping the workplace
Abilities / Learning Outcomes • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the working area • Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures • Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients • Bake food • Blind Bake food • Boil food • Blanch food • Braise meat
