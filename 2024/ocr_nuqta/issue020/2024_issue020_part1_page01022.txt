ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Assessment Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using cooking techniques
NQF level - 2
A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of cooking techniques such as baking, blind baking, boiling, blanching, braising, stewing, infusing, steeping, pressure cooking, simmering and steaming to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competence unit 1 Assessment criteria | Setting up the workplace The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to a) Identifying general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen, b) Identifying cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and taking preventive measures, c) Wearing appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen, d) Applying food safety procedures before preparing food stuff, e) Applying personal hygiene practices before entering the workplace, f) Identifying risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures, g) Cleaning and sanitizing kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients in less than 8 hours.
Assessment procedure and indicators | Setting for the assessment commercial kitchen or food production area.
