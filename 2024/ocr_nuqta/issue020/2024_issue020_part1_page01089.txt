Steam food in a steamer basket
Steaming is a moist-heat cooking method that works by boiling water which vaporizes into steam. the steam brings heat to the food and cooks it. Unlike boiling food submerged in water, with steaming the food is kept separate from the boiling water but comes into direct contact with the hot steam. Water boils at 100°C, so the highest temperature the food cooks at is 100°C. Steaming is ideal for cooking vegetables because the food remains separate from the hot water, allowing it to retain more nutrients in the cooking process.
Steaming is a delicate process with little if any agitation, so it's ideal for baby carrots, green beans, cubed zucchini, and delicate greens. Boiling is suitable for harder vegetables such as corn on the cob, potatoes, and beets.
