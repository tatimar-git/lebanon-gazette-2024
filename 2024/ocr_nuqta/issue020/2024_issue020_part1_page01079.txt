Cook food under high pressure steam
Pressure cooking is a method of cooking in which the boiling temperature of water is increased using a special vessel (pressure cooker), causing the food to cook faster.
The pressure cooker is a sealed vessel that traps the steam generated as its contents are heated. As steam builds, pressure increases, driving the boiling point of water past 100°C. This higher temperature shortens cooking times and, due to a lack of evaporation, extracts flavour more efficiently from foods. A safety valve releases steam when the pressure exceeds the safety limit for the cooker; usually the steam pressure lifts a weighted stopper allowing excess pressure to escape.
There are two types of pressure cooker, stovetop and electric. Stovetop pressure cookers cook food faster and are more sturdy than electric cookers but require a bit more attention while cooking, as users will have to adjust the heat throughout cooking.

1- Caramel custard recipe
- Combine 200 g of sugar and 80 g of water in a casserole
- Stir together to moisten the sugar
- cook it on the stove until getting a golden caramel colour
- Transfer to a heat resistant bowl, swirl the caramel gently in order to coat the bottom half of the bowl
- Let the caramel cool and proceed preparing the custard
- Add 6 whole eggs in a large bowl
- Add 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
- Add 1 orange peel and 1 lemon peel
- Whisk gently until combined
- Add 200 g of sugar and 1 L of whole milk
- Whisk together until combined
- Strain the custard into the caramel bowl
- Cover the bowl with aluminium foil and secure with a cooking band
- Add 1.5 cup of boiling water into the pressure cooker pot
- Transfer the bowl into the cooker placing it on top of a steaming tray or trivet
- Set the electric pressure cooker to steam mode using low pressure for 12 minutes
- Allow for a natural pressure release for 10 minutes once the cooking time is completed
- Transfer the bowl from the pressure cooker and place it on the counter
- Remove the foil and let the flan cool for about an hour
- Transfer the flan into the fridge and chill for at least 6 hours
- Run a sharp knife around the bowl to loosen the flan in order to serve it
• Invert over a dish and serve
N.B: If using a stovetop pressure cooker, reduce cooking time to 5 minutes.
