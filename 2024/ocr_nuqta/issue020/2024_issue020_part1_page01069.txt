The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 2 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 4 - Braising and Stewing.
Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Braise meat | The candidate should season the meal, sear it in a pot until brown, remove the meat from the pot, add vegetables and sear until soft, deglaze the pot with red wine, add the meat, add water or stock enough to cover the sides of the meat, bring to boil followed by a low simmer, add aromatics, cover the pot and cook until the meat is tender, scoop and serve. | ☐ | ☐

Slew meat The candidate should identify the differences between braising and stewing, cut meat in even small pieces, add to a heated oil in a pot, season, sear it until brown, add onions and garlic, deglaze with red wine, add stock until submerging the meat, cover the pot, simmer enough time for the meat to become tender, scoop and serve. ☐ ☐
