2- French press coffee
- Preheat coffee press and mug
- Grind coffee beans to a coarse or medium-coarse grind
- Measure ground coffee: 1 tablespoon (6 grams) per 118 ml of water
- Boil water
- Allow the water to sit for a 30-45 seconds after it has come to a full boil
- Empty the warming water from the coffee press and mug
- Add measured coffee grounds to the coffee press
- Pour hot water and stir
- Place the lid on the French press
- Wait 4 minutes
- Press slowly
- Pour the coffee into mugs and serve
