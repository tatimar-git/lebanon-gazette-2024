Art. 3: Expression of interest to participate in the licensing round
1- Companies interested in participating
in the Third Licensing Round shall first notify the LPA of their interest by sending a letter by email to the following address:
olr3@lpa.gov.lb.
2- The LPA provides each company that
has notified it its interest to participate in the Second Licensing Round with a “Username” and “Password” that allow access to the Company Portal that contains the detailed procedures and the forms relevant to the Third Licensing Round.
