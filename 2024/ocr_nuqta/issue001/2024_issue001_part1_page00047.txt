Diploma: TS2

Graphic design Matter: Computer Animation
Course Description
This animation course will introduce students to the world of 2D animations and basic video editing. Students will also learn the technique of stop Motion by physically designing and manipulating real objects or drawings, then turning them into animations using a digital camera and a frame-by-frame animation software.
Course Learning Objectives
The objectives of this course is to teach students how to manipulate videos (basic video editing) to get introduced to the basic tools of Adobe After Effects (2D animation software). The course will also teach students how to translate their ideas and stories into a short stop motion movie.
Course Learning Out Comes
Students will learn and understand the different video and audio formats used in animation and video editing projects, frame rates, and basic shooting methods. Students will be able to produce a short stop motion animation, and to animate a 2D logo on Adobe After Effects.
