Article 45 - Visibility
1. The Programme authorities and Lebanon shall ensure:
(a) the visibility of support in all activities relating to operations supported by the Interreg Funds with particular attention to operations of strategic importance;
(b) communication to citizens of participating countries of the role and achievements of the Funds through a single website portal providing access to all programmes involving that Member State.
They support the managing authority in carrying out its responsibilities referred to in Article 49(2) to (6) of Regulation (EU) 2021/1060.
2. The Programme authorities, Lebanon and beneficiaries shall use appropriately and in accordance with Article 36 of Regulation (EU) 2021/1059 the term ‘Interreg’ next to the emblem of the Union when carrying out visibility, transparency and communication activities.
3. For small project funds, the beneficiary shall ensure by means of the contractual terms that final recipients comply with the requirements to communicate publicly on the Interreg operation.
Part Three - Mutual rights and obligations with regard to the implementation and financial management of the Programme
Article 46 - Agreement between the managing authority, on behalf of Italy, and the national authority on behalf of Lebanon
The managing authority and the the Presidency of the Council of Ministers agreed on the following elements of mutual rights and obligations with regard to the implementation and financial management of the Interreg NEXT MED Programme and to complement the provisions under this Agreement.
46.a Arrangements regarding the national contribution and technical assistance
a. In accordance with Article 4 of this Agreement, the national contribution of Lebanon will be paid entirely by the beneficiaries of the projects as their own contribution to the expenses for the implementation of the project.
b. There will be no co-financing to the technical assistance component from the national bodies that participate in the management of the programme.
