The managing authority and the national authority will cooperate to prevent the risk of unsuccessful recoveries. The Grant contracts, and where needed partnership agreements, will introduce provisions for the mitigation of this risk, such as adapted lower prefinancing payments at contract signature, more frequent reporting, bank or insurance guarantees, and retention of part of the grant until project closure by the managing authority or the lead partner.
The Grant contracts, and where needed partnership agreements, will also state the acceptance by the private partners of any recovery intervention by the national authority in duly justified cases (such as irregularity, fraud, insolvency, corruption, etc.,), with the aim of ensuring sound financial management of the projects and the Programme. In addition to the financial capacity and reliability checks during the project selection phase in line with Article 22 of the Interreg Regulation, the managing authority will ensure a reinforced monitoring of the financial
performance of Lebanese partners during project implementation.
46.e Reporting obligations
The national authority shall have on-going access to aggregated data on the beneficiaries in the Programme’s electronic monitoring system and shall report to the managing authority any data concerning Lebanese beneficiaries by using it.
46.f Record keeping
1. Both parties shall ensure that all documents and electronic records related to an operation are kept for a five-year period from 31 December of the year in which the last payment from the managing authority to the beneficiary is made.
2. The period referred to in the previous paragraph may be interrupted either in case of a legal or administrative procedure or by a request of any of the Parties.
