The managing authority will take care of the financial management of the Programme in relation to accounting, payments from and to the Commission, signing of grant contracts, payments to project lead beneficiaries, financial corrections, management of technical assistance funds, monitoring of the financial execution of the Programme and transmission of data to the Commission.
46.b Management verifications
1. In accordance with Article 27 of this Agreement, the managing authority will carry out management verifications in Lebanon. The managing authority will have the support of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers appointed as Control Contact Point (CCP), for these checks, in particular the CCP shall ensure its support to the managing authority in:
- Providing information, upon request, to carry out the risk assessment;
- Establishing a long list of chartered accountants (project auditors) or endorse that the
auditor contracted by an organisation of its nationality involved in the partnership of a financed project meets the criteria set by Article 46(9) of the Interreg Regulation.
- Providing resolution of doubts regarding national legislation in coordination with the national authority, upon request of the managing authority;
- Provide support in on-site verifications, if requested by the managing authority.
In the event that Lebanon is willing to include additional support functions to its CCP, such as supporting the managing authority in auditors' training, quality control of expenditure verification reports, in coordination with the managing authority, the national authority will have to communicate this to the managing authority.
The details of the activities related to the above-mentioned functions as well as any additional task to be performed by the CCP will be described in the document with the description of the management and control system.
46.c Irregularities
1. The national authority shall ensure its cooperation in preventing, detecting and correcting irregularities including any potential fraud and corruption cases.
2. The competent body identified as counterpart of OLAF is the national authority.
3. The managing authority shall inform the national authority of any suspected or actual irregularity detected by any programme or European-level body. The managing authority shall also inform about the outcome of the investigations performed, whatever the source of detection.
4. The specific tasks and communications that shall be performed regarding any suspicion of irregularities, fraud or corruption will be detailed in a specific Anti-fraud strategy to be approved by the monitoring committee.
