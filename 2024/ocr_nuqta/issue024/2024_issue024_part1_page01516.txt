Pan-frying breaded chicken breasts
- Put the chicken breast in a zip lock plastic bag
- Seal the bag
- Pound the breasts to have wide even surface for better breading
- Salt the chicken breast with kosher salt
- Prepare the breading ingredients
- Break the eggs (1 egg per chicken breast), season with salt and beat them with a fork or whisk
- Season the flour with salt
- Season the breadcrumbs with black pepper
- Grate parmesan cheese over the breadcrumbs
- Coat the chicken breast in flour
- Shake off excess flour
- Dip in eggs (letting excess drip off)
- Dredge in breadcrumbs, turning twice and patting to adhere
- Place a sauté pan over medium heat
- Add enough oil to cover half of the chicken breast in the pan
- Lay chicken breast away from you
- Flip the chicken breast to the other side once it reaches a golden colour (2-3 minutes for each side)
Remove the chicken breast and drain them on a cooling rack
Stir-frying is a basic Asian cooking method that generally uses a wok for cooking, but can also be done in a frying pan. It involves cooking small pieces of food in a small quantity of oil over high heat for a brief period of time, stirring constantly. A small quantity of liquid is added to form a sauce as well as various seasonings.
Vegetables stir-fry recipe
- Quarter the mushrooms and onion
- Chop the green bell pepper and red bell pepper in cubes
- Chop the broccoli
- Put 13 tablespoon of soya sauce and 1 teaspoon of rice vinegar in a bowl
- Mix ingredients together
- Place sauté pan over high heat
- Drizzle some peanut oil and a splash of sesame oil
Add the green and red bell pepper
Stir-fry for a couple of minutes
Add the mushrooms and broccoli
Stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes
Add the baby corn and sauce
Stir-fry for another couple of minutes
Plate and serve
