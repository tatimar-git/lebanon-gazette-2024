ASSESSMENT STANDARD

ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Assessment Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using preparation techniques in Frying, Microwaving and Roasting
NQF level - 2
A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of preparation techniques such as frying, microwaving, roasting, grilling, barbecuing, rotisserie, searing and broiling to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competence unit 1 | Setting up the work area

Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the abilities learning outcomes related to a) Identifying general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen, b) Identifying cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and taking preventive measures, c) Wearing appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen, d) Applying food safety procedures before preparing food stuff, e) Applying personal hygiene practices before entering the working area, f) Identifying risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures, g) Cleaning and sanitizing kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients in less than 7.5 hours.
Assessment procedure and indicators Setting for the assessment commercial kitchen or food production area.
Tools and materials
Soap dispenser

Sanitizer dispenser

Competence unit 2 Frying


Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the abilities/learning outcomes related to a) Identifying the frying technique and methods, b) Cooking food in deep-frying method, c) Cooking food in pan-frying method, d) Cooking food in stir-frying method, e) Cooking food in sautéing method, f) Cooking food in air-frying method in less than 20 hours.
Assessment procedure and indicators Setting for the assessment commercial kitchen or food production area.
