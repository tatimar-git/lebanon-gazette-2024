Broil food | Broiling is the cooking technique that uses radiant heat from above to cook your food, so it's like upside-down grilling. Broiling is a good method to use for gratinating, giving a caramelized colour to ingredients. Broiling is also a way to add colour, crispness and flavour to a baked or roasted food. In an electric oven, it is a coil that is attached to the top of the oven. In a gas oven, it is a burner on the top of the oven or in a drawer underneath that slides in and cut. It's a salamander as well Bro Broil a cheese sandwich: - Open a baguette bread in 2 halves - Spread butter on each half - add grated emmental and mozzarella cheese - Adjust the oven rack while the oven is cold. There should be 7.6 cm to 12.7 cm distance between the top of the food and the heat source - Preheat the oven for 5 to 7 minutes at 260°C

• Slide the broiling pan onto the oven rack
• Remove the broiling pan from the oven and put the sandwich in a plate and Serve
