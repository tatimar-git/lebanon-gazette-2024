The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 2 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 - Frying

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO(*)
Identify the frying technique and methods | The candidate should identify the different methods of frying and types of fats and oils to use and apply the tips for better food frying. | ☐ | ☐
Cook food in deep-frying method | The candidate should wash and peel the potatoes, cut them using the mandolin fries blade, pre-heat the oil to 150°C, dry the potatoes, blanch the potatoes in the deep-fryer for 3 minutes, place them on a paper towel, deep-fry the potatoes again at 180°C until crispy and gold, drain them and season immediately. | ☐ | ☐

Cook food in pan-frying method The candidate should pound the chicken breasts, salt them, prepare the breading ingredients, coat the culets with flour then eggs and finally bread crumbs, place a sauté pan over medium heat, add enough canola oil to cover half of the culets, place the culets and pan-fry them on each side for 2-3 minutes until crispy and gold, remove them from oil and drain them on a cooling rack.
Cook food in stir-frying method The candidate should quarter the onions and mushrooms, chop the broccoli and peppers roughly, prepare the sauce (soya sauce, sesame oil, black vinegar), place a sauté pan over high heat, add peanut oil, stir-fry the peppers, then mushrooms and broccoli, and finally baby corn and sauce.
Cook food in sautéing method The candidate should slice the onions, heat a sauté pan over high heat, add canola oil then the onions, sauté the onions and flipping them for 5-7 minutes or longer depending on the recipe instructions.
Cook food in air-frying method The candidate should wash, peel and slice the potatoes, soak them in ice water for 2-3 hours, dry them, heat the air-fryer for 3 minutes at 400°C, place the potatoes in the basket, spray them with some oil, place the basket in the machine, air fry them for 25 minutes at 400 °F, remove the basket several times during frying to toss the fries, season the fries and serve.
