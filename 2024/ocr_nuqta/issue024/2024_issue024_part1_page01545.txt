2- Grilled steak
- Pat dry the steak with paper towels on both sides
- Season with salt and grounded black pepper
- Add olive oil or any other fat of your choice
- Rub the steak with garlic clove
- Place the steak on a pre-heated grill
- Grill one side and do not turn it at all until well seared
- Flip the steak to the other uncooked side
- Grill the steak sides
- Let rest for 5-10 minutes before serving it
N.B: The cooking time for meats differs depending on the consumer's preferences:
- Rare: 2 minutes each side
Medium rare: 3-4 minutes each side
Medium: 4-6 minutes each side
Well done: 2-4 minutes each side then reduce heat and cook for further 4-6 minutes

Barbecuing refers to a dry-heat cooking process which is used to cook meal and other ingredients over burning charcoal, smoke and fire, electricity or even gas powered barbecue. Traditionally, barbecuing is done outdoors, as there is smoke and fire involved in the cooking process. The meat is either marinated, rubbed or basted with flavouring ingredients before barbecuing.
Barbecuing a whole chicken
- Spatchcock chicken by cutting out the backbone with shears in order to lay it flat (aka butter frying)
- Press down on the chicken breast to flatten it
- Slide a metal skewer through both thighs and breasts. This makes the chicken easier to handle on the barbecue
- Season both sides with salt and pepper
- Preheat the barbecue grill
- Clean and oil grill grate
image here
Place chicken, skin side-up on the cooler side of the grill
Position the legs closer to the fire. Legs can handle the extra heat without overcooking, unlike breast meat
Cook until the thickest part of breasts reaches 45°C (40 minutes)
Flip chicken and place it directly over hot coals, skin side down
Continue cooking until skin is crisp and breast reaches at least 75°C
Move to cooler side if chicken starts to burn before it's done
Keep the grill covered at all times
Move the chicken to a cutting board
Let rest for 5-10 minutes
Carve and serve
