EDUCATION STANDARD

ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Education Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using preparation techniques in Frying, Microwaving and Roasting
NQF level - 2

Overview At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of cooking techniques such as baking, blind baking, boiling, blanching, braising, stewing, infusion, steeping, pressure cooking, simmering and steaming, to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
Competences CU1. Setting up the work area CU2. Frying CU3. Microwaving CU4. Roasting CU5. Grilling and Barbecuing CU6. Rotisserie CU7. Searing and Broiling CU8. Housekeeping the work area
Abilities / Learning Outcomes • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the work area • Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures • Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients • Identify the frying technique and methods • Cook food in deep-frying method • Cook food in pan frying method • Cook food in stir-frying method • Cook food in Air frying method • Identify the microwave cooking advantages and disadvantages • Cook food in a microwave


• Identify the advantages and disadvantages of roasting
• Roast food
• Differentiate between grilling and barbecuing
• Grill food
• Barbecue food
• Cook roisserie food
• Cook food in searing method
• Broil food
• Identify hazards and risks associated with cleaning and take preventive measures
• Use appropriate detergents in cleaning
• Use cleaning detergents in correct quantity

• Dispose food waste appropriately

• Clean and sanitize machines used in preparing food
