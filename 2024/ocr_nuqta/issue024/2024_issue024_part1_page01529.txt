Identify the microwave cooking advantages and disadvantages
Microwave cooking is a convenient method of food preparation especially for creating quick meals as well as for defrosting and reheating. (method not recommended at all)
Microwaves cook by using a process called dielectric heating, the act of passing microwave radiation through a food. Water absorbs the microwaves very well, so food with a high water content cooks more rapidly than foods consisting of mixtures of fat and water.
Unlike a conventional oven, the microwave heats only the food, not the entire oven. That's because the food absorbs the energy of the radiation, producing a reaction that causes heat.
Advantages of Microwave oven
- Quick heating and reheating
- Precise cooking programs (not recommended)
- Easy to clean
- Can be used to defrost food (not recommended)
- Variable heat settings
- Low energy consumption
Disadvantages of Microwave oven
- Dry out food: since it reduces water content
- Soggy food: cooking for an extended time can cause food to get soggy
- Not versatile: inability to bake, roast or fry foods
- Needs special containers: Microwave-safe cookware is typically composed of glass, ceramic, or specific types of plastic. Otherwise, there’s a risk of unwanted, toxic compounds from the plastic cookware contaminating the food
