Sautéing is a dry-heat cooking method that uses a small amount of fat or oil in a shallow pan over relatively high heat. It is a great method to cook small even-sized pieces of food as well as to sear or brown food before some other method of cooking. Food is tossed in the pan and shaken over until cooked thoroughly. This cooking method is suitable for tender vegetables and portion size cuts of meat, fish and poultry.
Sautéed onions
- Slice the onions as required in the recipe
- Place a large skillet pan over high heat
- Check if it is hot enough by dropping some water. It should bounce around a little and sizzle off quickly
- Add canola oil or any other type of oil or fat with a high smoking point
- Add the sliced onions
- Start sautéing the onion by pushing the pan down forward and pull back making a round movement in order to flip the onions
- Proceed for 5-7 minutes or longer if you want to brown the onions
Air frying is done in an air-fryer machine. It is actually a mini convection oven that cooks by circulating hot air around with a fan. This way, food is cooked by convection, thus giving food crispiness with less oil.
Air fried French fries
- Prepare the potatoes as discussed earlier in the deep-frying method above
- Dry the potatoes on a kitchen towel
- Preheat the air fryer for 3 minutes at 205°C (400 °F)
- Remove the drawer basket
- Place the potatoes in the basket
- Spritz with oil to get flavour and colour
- Place the basket in its place in the air fryer
- Set time and temperature (400 °F for 25 minutes)
- Remove the basket every while and then and toss the fries for even cooking
- Season the fries at the end of cooking
