ASSESSMENT STANDARDS - UNIT 2
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome Identify the frying technique and methods | Criteria for competence unit 2- Frying Are the different frying methods identified? Are the various types of fats and oils to use for frying identified? Are the tips to follow for a good frying well identified? Are potatoes washed and peeled? Are potatoes sliced using the mandolin fries blade? | Yes / No

Cook food in deep-frying method Is canola oil heated to 150°C?
Are potatoes drained and well dried with a cotton cloth?
Are potatoes blanched in the oil for 3 minutes then removed and placed on paper towels?
Are blanched potatoes deep-fried again in oil at 180°C until crispy and gold?
Are fries drained on paper towels and salted immediately?

Cook food in pan-frying method Are chicken breasts pounded in a plastic bag then salted?
Are breaded ingredients prepared?
Are chicken breasts breaded with flour first, then eggs and finally bread crumbs?
Is oil heated in a sauté pan?
Are chicken cutlets placed in oil and fried on each side for 2-3 minutes until gold?
Are fried cutlets drained on a cooling rack?
Cook food in stir-frying method Are onions and mushrooms cut in quarter?
Are the green bell pepper, red bell pepper and broccoli sliced roughly?
Are soya sauce and black vinegar combined well together?
Is peanut oil with sesame oil added to a heated wok or sauté pan?
Are peppers stir-fried for a couple of minutes?
Are mushrooms and broccoli added and stir-fried for 3-4 minutes?
Are the baby corn and sauce added and all ingredients stir-fried for a couple of minutes then plated ?
Cook food in sautéing method Are onions sliced as desired?
Is the sauté pan and canola oil pre-heated before adding the onions ?
Are onions added then sautéed by pushing down forward the pan then pulling back in a round movement so that onions flip over?
Are potatoes prepared as earlier for French fries?


Cook food in air-frying method Is the air-fryer pre-heated for 3 minutes at 400°F?
Are potatoes placed in the air-fryer basket then drizzled with oil before placing the basket in the machine?
Are potatoes air fried for 25 minutes at 400°F?
Is the basket removed several timed during frying in order to toss the fries?
Are fries seasoned and served?
