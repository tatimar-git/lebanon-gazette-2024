Knives cut injuries
Using the wrong knife and lack of knife skills can cause serious injuries.
- Keep knives well maintained and sharp
- Use the correct knife for the jcb
- Cut away from your body when trimming or deboning
- Store knives in a proper knife bag or knife rack with knife guards
- Point the blade facing down when carrying knives
- Put down knives when interrupted
- Announce when moving with a knife
- Clean knives immediately after use or place them in labelled containers near the sink
Food contaminants in commercial kitchen
A food contaminant is a substance, object or organism that makes food unfit for human consumption.
There are three types of food contaminants:
1) Biological: when disease causing bacteria or pathogens contaminate food and are consumed. It is a common cause of food poisoning and food spoilage.
Preventive measure includes:
- Keep high-risks foods (meat, poultry, seafood, eggs, dairy) out of the Food Danger Zone
- Purchase, store, thaw, prepare, cook and serve high-risks food from known and safety-compliant suppliers
- Follow a regular cleaning and sanitising schedule for food contact surfaces and equipment
- Maintain high standards of personal hygiene for all staff
2) Chemical: It occurs when chemicals get into food such as insecticides, kitchen cleaning agents, pest control products, and non-safe plastic food containers.
Preventive measure includes:
- Store all cleaning chemicals away from food products
- Use designated containers for cleaning jobs instead of food containers
- Cover all food before using any chemicals
- Properly wash fruits and vegetables before preparing them
- Store food in containers designed for reuse
- Use food-safe oil when oiling moving parts of equipment
- Choose food safe cleaning chemicals
3) Physical: it occurs when physical objects enter food such as hair, glass or metal, dirt, pests, jewellery, or fingernails.
Preventive measure includes:
- Throw away broken or cracked utensils
- Pull back hair and wear a helmet
- Store food off the floor in rodent-proof lids
- Create cleaning schedules and guidelines
- Keep nails short
- Avoid wearing jewellery when handling food
Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures
