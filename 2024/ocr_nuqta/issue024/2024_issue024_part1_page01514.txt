Identify the frying technique and methods
• Frying is a method of cooking in which food is cooked in a bath of hot oil or fat, typically between 175°C and 190°C. Depending on the type of frying, food is either partially or fully submerged in the fat until the food has turned golden brown with a crisp outer layer and moist interior.
1- Frying methods
• Deep-frying: A full immersion form of frying in which food is completely submerged in hot oil. This method is frequently done with a deep-frying machine.
• Pan-frying: It is a healthier form of shallow frying known also as sautéing. Food is cooked in various amounts of oil in a frying pan. Generally, the foods are sliced thinly for this method.
• Stir-frying: Commonly used in Asian cooking, frying takes place in a unique pan with sloped slices. Food is cooked quickly over higher temperatures.
• Sautéing: It involves cooking in small amount of fat in a hot pan while tossing ingredients around.
• Air-frying: It is a healthy method of frying in which food is cooked by hot air instead of fat in a special air fryer machine.
