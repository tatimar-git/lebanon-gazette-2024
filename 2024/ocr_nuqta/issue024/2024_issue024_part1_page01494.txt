ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Competence Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using preparation techniques in Frying, Microwaving and Roasting
NQF level - 2

A competent person should be able to prepare food and meal ingredients using a variety of preparation techniques such as frying, microwaving, roasting, grilling, barbecuing, rotisserie, searing and broiling to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale

Competence | Ability / Learning Outcome
CU1 Setting up the work area | • Identify general hazards and food contaminants in commercial kitchen • Identify cross-contamination risks in commercial kitchen and take preventive measures • Wear appropriate PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen • Apply food safety procedures before preparing food stuff • Apply personal hygiene practices before entering the work area • Identify risks associated with cleaning chemicals and take preventive measures • Clean and sanitize kitchen tools and food contact surfaces before preparing meal ingredients
CU2 Frying | • Identify the frying technique and methods • Cook food in deep-frying method • Cook food in pan frying method • Cook food in stir-frying method • Cook food in sautéing method • Cook food in Air frying method
CU3 Microwaving | • Identify the microwave cooking advantages and disadvantages • Cook food in a microwave
CU4 Roasting | • Identify the roasting technique advantages and disadvantages • Roast food
CU5 Grilling and Barbecuing | • Grill food • Barbecue food
