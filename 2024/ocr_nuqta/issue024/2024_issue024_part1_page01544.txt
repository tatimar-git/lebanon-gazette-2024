Grill food
Grilling and barbecuing is a method of dry heat cooking method that involves cooking food on a rack over a heat source, usually a charcoal fire or ceramic briquettes heated by gas flames with the lid off. this method cooks food quickly with intense heat. It works best for thin cuts of meat that cook quickly (burgers and several kinds of steaks and chops for example) and most vegetables. It is not ideal for larger cuts of meat because the high heat will overcook them on the outside before they're done inside.
N.B: Grilling can also be done on an electric grill that can be used indoor and outdoor. Additionally, it comes with temperature control that help in heat regulation while cooking.
1- Difference between grilling, barbecuing and smoking
- Grilling and Barbecuing are often performed on the same equipment and they're typically small foods (steaks, burgers, chicken breasts), cooked hot and fast (5-15 minutes) with no lid, and over high heat (260°C)
- Barbecuing is done usually on a charcoal fire on the grills while grilling is done only on grills and grill pans.
- Smoking is typically larger cuts (ribs, brisket, shoulder), cooked low and slow (4-72 hours), with the lid on.
