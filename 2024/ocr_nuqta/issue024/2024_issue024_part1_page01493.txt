ISCO-08 -9412 Kitchen helpers ISCO-08-5120 Cooks | Occupational Standard Preparing food and meals ingredients using preparation techniques in Frying, Microwaving and Roasting
NQF level - 2
The occupation covers the process of preparing food and meal ingredients using a variety of preparation techniques such as frying, microwaving, roasting, grilling, barbecuing, rotisserie, searing and broiling to produce healthy and wholesome foodstuff in a micro scale commercial kitchen while ensuring sanitary and quality requirements.
