Cook food in searing method | The candidate should take the meat cut of the fridge 15 minutes before searing, dry it with paper towels, add cooking oil to the pan, preheat the pan, add the steak, season it and sear it 2-5 minutes on each side. | ☐ | ☐
Broil food | The candidate should cut the bread in 2, spread it with butter, fill it with grated cheese and line up the broiling pan with aluminium foil, spray it with cooking oil, poke the foil, place the sandwich on the foil, adjust the oven rack, preheat the oven, place the broiling pan in the oven, broil the sandwich until nicely brown, remove the pan from the oven and serve with accompaniments. | ☐ | ☐
