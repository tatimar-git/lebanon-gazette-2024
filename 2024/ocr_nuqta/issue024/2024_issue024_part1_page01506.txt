Wearing PPE and working clothes in commercial kitchen helps to protect kitchen staff from burns, cuts and other types of injuries as well as reduce the risks of contamination
Wash hands before wearing PPE
Pull hair back
Put on the chef toque tucking inside all your hair
Put on the hairmet if hair is long, covering all visible hair parts
Put on the chef's jacket then the pants making sure the buttons are done up all the way to the top
Roll the sleeves once on the uniform
Put on the apron. Fold it once, tie it in the front and fold it again
The square apron should come below the knees and should be removed before leaving the kitchen
Put on the side towel
Put on your shoes. Do not let the pants touch the ground
Tie the neckerchief
Apply food safety procedures before preparing foodstuff
Promote hand washing
- Wash hands regularly and thoroughly before touching any food at least 20 seconds under running water after soaping up well
Don't let sick employees prepare food
- Keep employees suffering from vomiting, diarrhoea or any other type of transmissible disease away from the food preparation area
Food handling safety
- Wear clean kitchen uniform and hair restraints at all times
- Avoid wearing artificial nails and jewellery
- Implement HACCP standards
Wash all produce
- Wash vegetables and fruits properly to remove any bacteria and dirt that may be on the surface
Store food correctly at the right temperature
- Keep raw meat and poultry separated from vegetables, prepared sauces, and anything that requires little preparation
- Store food cooled at 4°C in a shallow pan to permit ventilation
- Freeze foods at -18°C or below
