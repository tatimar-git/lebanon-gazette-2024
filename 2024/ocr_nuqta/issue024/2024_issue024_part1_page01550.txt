The candidate is considered competent upon demonstrating a best practice performance in all the 2 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 5 - Grilling and Barbecuing

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Grill food | The candidate should differentiate between grilling and smoking, identify the different cooking temperatures of meal, dry the steak, season it, rub it with oil and garlic, place it on a preheated grill, cook each side enough time as desired and serve it. | ☐ | ☐

Barbecue food The candidate should remove the chicken backbone, season it, preheat the barbecue, place the chicken skin side-up on the cooler side of the grill, cook the chicken until the thickest part reaches 45°C, flip the chicken to the other side, cover the grill again, cook the chicken until crispy and inner temperature reaches 68°C, place the barbecued chicken on a cutting board to rest, carve it and serve.
Criteria (*) Evidence of non-compliance if any ()
