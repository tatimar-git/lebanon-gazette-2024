Cook food in searing method
Searing is a cooking technique that exposes ingredients to a high temperature to create a crisp browning on the outside. This method called Maillard reaction, detain the juices of the ingredients inside, enhances the flavour of the dish and is often done in a small amount of oil, butter or fat.
Searing is ideal for adding texture and flavour to a tender cut of meat, and can also be used for seafood and some vegetables. This method is commonly used prior to sautéing, baking, braising, roasting, and briefly cooks food at a high temperature to bring out flavour.
Searing beef steak
- Remove steak from the fridge 15 minutes before searing
- Pat dry to absorb all moisture
- Add canola oil to a sauté pan
- Preheat the pan on high. Oil will wrinkle when it's ready
- Add the steak to the pan
- Season with salt and pepper
- Cook for 2-5 minutes on each side
- Proceed with the recipe
