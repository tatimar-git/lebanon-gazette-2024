ASSESSMENT STANDARDS – UNIT 5
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee Name: | Date:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 5- Grilling and Barbecuing | Yes / No
Grill food | Are the differences between grilling and smoking identified?
Are the temperature cooking times for meat identified?
Is the steak pat dried then seasoned?
Is the steak rubbed with olive oil and garlic ?
Is the steak placed on a pre-heated grill and cooked on each side according to desired temperature?
Are all steak sides grilled?
Is the steak topped with chopped parsley and served? Optional
Barbecue food | Is the whole chicken flattened by removing the backbone then seasoned?
Is the barbecue grill preheated, brushed clean and oiled?
Is the chicken placed on the cooler side of the grill skin side-up?
Is the chicken left to cook enough time to reach 45°C in its thickest part then flipped to the other side?
Is the barbecued chicken left to rest on a cutting board then carved and served?
