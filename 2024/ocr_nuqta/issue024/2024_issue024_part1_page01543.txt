Is the chicken placed on the rack of the roasting pan then roasted in the oven for 45-60 minutes?
Is the roasted chicken unrussed and transferred to a serving plate?
Is the fat spooned out of the pan?
Is the pan deglazed with white wine over high heat and chicken stock added to add flavour?
Is the sauce strained then poured in a gravy boat and served with the roasted chicken?

Roast food The candidate should preheat the oven to 220°C, remove the wishbone from the chicken cavity, season the inside with salt and pepper, truss the chicken well, season the outside of the chicken and sprinkle it with olive oil, put cuts of potato in the roast pan, transfer the chicken to the roast pan, roast the chicken enough time, untruss the chicken, deglaze the pan with white wine, add chicken stock, reduce the sauce, pour the sauce in a gravy boat and serve it with the roasted chicken.
