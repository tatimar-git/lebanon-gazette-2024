Cook food in a microwave
1- Microwave hard boiled eggs
- Fill a microwave safe bowl with enough water to cover the eggs
- Sprinkle some salt to prevent the egg from splashing as well as it helps in peeling
- Mix salt and water
- Place the egg in water
- Place the bowl in the microwave
- Cook for 10 minutes
- Put the boiled egg in ice water bath to cool it
- Peel the egg and serve
3- Microwave poached eggs
- Fill a microwave safe bowl with water
- Add salt and mix
- Crack the egg over the water
- Place the bowl in the microwave
- Cook for 1 or 2 minutes
- Remove from the microwave and serve

5- Microwave crispy potatoes
- Wash and peel the potatoes
- Slice as thinly as possible
- Place the potatoes in ice water bath for 2-3 minutes in order to reduce the starch content
- Dry the potatoes between 2 paper towels
- Place the potatoes slices on a parchment paper
- Apply enough oil to both sides of the potatoes
- Place the potatoes in the microwave
- Cook for 3 minutes
- Serve
6- Baked potatoes
- Wash the potatoes
- Place them on a microwave safe plate
- Prick the potatoes all over with a fork to let the steam out and prevent it from popping
- Cook the potatoes on full power for 5 minutes
- Turn it over using a paper towel
- Cook for another 5 minutes
- Remove the potatoes from the microwave and cut in half lengthwise
- Season with salt and pepper
- Mash up the inside a little with a fork
- Top the potatoes with 1 tablespoon of butter and 2 tablespoons of shredded cheddar cheese
- Cook in the microwave for 1 minute to melt the cheese
- Top the potatoes with sour cream and serve
