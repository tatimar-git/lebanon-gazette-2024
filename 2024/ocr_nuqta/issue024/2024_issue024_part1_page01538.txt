١٥٣٨ الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٤ - ٢٠٢٤/٦/١٣
Roast food
Roasted chicken
- Preheat the oven to 220°C
- Remove the wishbone
- Scrap a bit of the flesh to feel the wishbone
- Cut down on the outside of the wishbone around the joint
- Pull out the bone
- Slide the wings underneath the body to support it during roasting
- Season the inside with salt and pepper
- Truss the chicken by tying it with butchers twine
- Slide the twine underneath the tail
- Take the twine on top of the legs then underneath
- Take the legs into the breast
- Take a string underneath the neck and do a simple slipknot
- Season the chicken with salt and pepper and thyme
- Sprinkle with olive oil (or butter)
- Put cuts of potatoes
- Place the chicken in a roasting pan
- Roast in the oven for 45-60 minutes depending on the size and on the quality of the chicken
- Untruss the chicken and take the strings off
- Transfer the roasted chicken to a serving plate
- Spoon out the fat from the roast pan
- Put the roast pan on high heat and deglaze it with 1/2 cup of white wine
- Scrap the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon
- Add chicken stock to build up the sauce
- Let it boil until reduced
- Strain the sauce into a pot
- Season with salt if needed
- Pour the sauce in a gravy bowl and serve it with the roasted chicken
