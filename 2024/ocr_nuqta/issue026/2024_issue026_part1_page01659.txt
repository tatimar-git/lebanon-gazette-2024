Assemble the pieces
Assemble the plinth with glue and screws
- Read off the locations and dimensions of the floor units from the plan and mark them on the floor with a pencil
- Put the plinth components to length and lay them in position
- Level the top edge of the plinth with a spirit level or laser level and adjust as required.
- If only small adjustments are needed, you can use packing pieces, such as 4 mm plastic laminate
- For bigger gaps or variations in floor levels, you may need to scribe the kickboard to the contours or slope of the floor and reshape the underside of the kickboard with a plane

Seal the exposed edge of the kickboards that meet the floor
Assemble carcass pieces:
- Use a water-based glue to insert biscuits inside slots: the water in the glue is what swells the biscuit and makes the joint snug, well fitted and resistant
- Put glue in each slot but not on the biscuit itself: squeeze a little bit of glue into the slot
- Spread glue around and in the slot with a popsicle stick
- Clamp from the front to the back board while pressing against the sides
- Tweak the joint with a mallet so the pieces are perfectly aligned: this process should be performed quickly before the biscuits swell and while the joints still have some mobility
- Clamp form side to side to preserve the alignment you got
- Tighten the clamps, wipe out the excess glue, and leave to dry
Fix carcass to plinth:
- Install the carcass on top of plinth
- Make sure its levelled
- Drive the screws inside holes that are drilled on the inside of the bottom board to fix the carcass to the plinth
- Use a mallet to fit in the plugs
- Sand the access amount of the plugs if needed
- Paint the visible side of plugs with the same color of the carcass using a painting brush

Assemble the drawer:
- Use a water-based glue to insert biscuits inside slots: the water in the glue is what swells the biscuit and makes the joint snug, well fitted and resistant
- Put glue in each slot but not on the biscuit itself: squeeze a little bit of glue into the slot
- Spread glue around and in the slot with a popsicle stick
- Assemble one board at a time: start by fixing the sides, to the front and back
- Clamp from the front to the back board while pressing against the sides
- Tweak the joint with a mallet so the pieces are perfectly aligned: this process should be performed quickly before the biscuits swell and while the joints still have some mobility
- Clamp form side to side to preserve the alignment you got
- Tightlen the clamps, wipe out the excess glue, and leave to dry
- Mark holes to attach the front board on drawer
- Insert screws (3cm) in the holes
- Attach the front board to the drawer face with glue and nails.
- Attach drawer bottom to the drawer with screws
