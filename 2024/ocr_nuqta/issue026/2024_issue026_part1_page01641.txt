ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, wardrobe-makers, and related trades workers | Competence Standard Wardrobes Making
ISCO-08-7522 Wardrobe-makers and related workers
EQF level - 3

A competent person should be able to fabricate carcasses: cut, fit, join, assemble, and finish prepared wooden parts to make a wardrobe, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.
Competence unit 1 Setting up the working place
Assessment criteria The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the abilities / learning outcomes related to CU1 in less than 2 hours.
Assessment procedure Demonstrate ability to:


Use PPE to prevent risks of personal injuries
Organize working space and equipment, tools, consumables, and materials
Identify hazards and risks and take preventive measures
Provide first aid in case of personal injury
Interpret technical drawings and blueprints
