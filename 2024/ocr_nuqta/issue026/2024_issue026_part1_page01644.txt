Use PPE to prevent risks of personal injuries
- Prepare your PPE and wear it before you start doing any woodworking:
- Separate defected from non-defected PPE from your PPE box/locker
- Dispose defected PPE outside workplace to avoid contamination, unauthorized use, and mix-ups
- Wear eye protection googles
- Wear leg and foot protection typically including safety shoes
- Wear hand and arm protection typically including safety heavy duty gloves
- Wear body protection equipment including overalls and full-body garments
- Wear hearing protection typically including earmuffs
- Wear dust masks to avoid inhaling sawdust and other tiny particulate matter

Organize working space and equipment, tools, consumables, and materials
Organize your working space in a practical way to maximize efficiency and minimize hazards:
Install pegboards on walls above the workbench to hang tools and allow easy arms reach
Fix heavy power machines on wheeled bases to easily move out of the way
Label and compartmentalize cabinets and drawers to find tools easily
Keep distances around workbench not less than 100cm to guarantee smooth moving
Separate screws by size and type and store them in divided toolboxes
Store portable power machines on wall hung shelves for easy access
Store technical drawings, blueprints, and other documents inside cabinets to keep dust free
Move away everything from emergency exits to keep it clear
Store back all unwanted tools and materials to keep space clutter free and tripping free
Fix ceiling mounted hangers to hold electrical cords and extra work lights if needed
