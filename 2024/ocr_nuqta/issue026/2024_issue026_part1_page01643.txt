ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, wardrobe-makers, and related trades workers ISCO-08-7522 Wardrobe-makers and related workers | Competence Standard Wardrobes Making
EQF level - 3
Overview | At the completion of the Training Module the participant will be able to fabricate wooden carcasses, fit, join, assemble and finish prepared wooden parts to make a wardrobe, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.
Competences | CU1. Setting up the working place CU2. Building wall to wall closet CU3. Installing accessories

CU4. Housekeeping and reporting
Abilities / Learning Outcomes • Use different engineered wood types • Use several joining techniques • Use and chose several finishing techniques
Knowledge • Engineered wood types • Wood finishing techniques • Joining techniques
Learning program During the classroom lessons the following topics will be covered: 1. Best uses of different engineered wood types, advantages, disadvantages 2. Best wood finishing techniques according to wood type 3. Best joining techniques according to wood and furniture type During the workshop lessons the following practical activities will be execute: 1. Cutting wood using various machines and hand tools 2. Using several joining techniques according to wood and furniture type 3. Using fishing techniques according to wood and fumiture type


During on the job training the following job activities will be carried out
1. Cutting wood pieces
2. Joining wood pieces
3. Finishing wood pieces
4. Assembling wood pieces to obtain piece of furniture
