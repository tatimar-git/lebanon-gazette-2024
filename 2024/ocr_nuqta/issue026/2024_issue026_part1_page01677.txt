ASSESSMENT STANDARDS – UNIT 3
FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT

Trainee: | Date:
Assessor:
Ability / learning outcome | Criteria for competence unit 3 – Installing accessories | YES / NO

Install accessories to carcass: shelf pins, rods socket Are the pins fixed to carcass to receive the shelf after measuring and marking their spot?
Are the sockets fixed to carcass to receive the rod after measuring and marking their spot?
Is the rod sawn to length with a plumber's tubing cutter then filed to smooth edges?
Is the rod inserted between sockets?
Install accessories to drawers: slides, handles Are the steps of cutting the handle location performed?
Is the handle placed to check the fit after the drawer's face was painted?
Is epoxy glue applied to the back of the handle and the handle clamped it in place?
Is the epoxy cured?
Are the side slides fixed to drawer after marking, and drilling their location?
Are the side slides fixed to carcass after marking, and drilling their location?
Are the slides mounted to the carcass on both sides simultaneously?


Install accessories to doors: hinges soft close, push to open Are the location of the hinges marked and drilled?
Are the hinges fixed on the door?
Is a recessed magnetic push to open latch fixed to the carcass after drilling a hole to accommodate it?
Is a skinny self-adhesive plate attached to the magnetic latch then pressed into the door?
Is the touch latch adjusted in or out properly by turning with a flat blade screwdriver?
