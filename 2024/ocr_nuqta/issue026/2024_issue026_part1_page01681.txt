Keep table saw and circular saw blades sharp
Replace router bits and drill bits when they become very dull or damaged
Replace band saw blades when they become very dull or damaged
Keep the tools in alignment:
Add an accurate fence system and a precision miter gauge will greatly increase the accuracy and reliability of many tables saws
Add a set of ceramic guide blocks will significantly improve the tracking performance of most band saws
Add a carter band saw guide can transform an ordinary band saw into a professional class machine
Keep tools clean & smooth:
Apply polish and resin remover to saws to remove any build-ups and residues
Use fine steel wool or 400 Grit wet and dry sandpaper with mineral turpentine. Work in the mineral turps with the abrasive and wipe away sludge with a clean cloth. Make sure you do the mitre slot as well
Apply a coat of silver glide with a clean cloth and buff off the excess. Repeat the process when you deem it necessary or if you're going away for a while. If your machines will not be used for long periods, it's worth covering them with a machine cover to help protect them

Put tools must away and keep in the proper place: That's to be able to find what you're looking for with a minimum of time and frustration. Do not leave tools lying around the benches or
workshop; accidents may occur:
- Use a check list to assure that all the tools are collected and are in a proper condition, make sure that all the tools are stored in their proper place at the working bench and no tools are lying around on the countertop
- Hang on pegboard
- Store in chest toolboxes
- Store in cabinets
- Wheel heavy machines along the wall
- Remove combustible materials and store flammable materials away from sources of ignition in approved containers in designated areas

Dispose of the waste

- Replace any worn, ripped, or damaged flooring that poses a tripping hazard
- Keep wall paint clean to highlight railings, guards, and other safety equipment
- Keep walkways free from debris and any trip hazards
- Keep aisles, stairways, emergency exits, electrical panels and doors clear of clutter, and purge untidy areas
- Clean up spills such as oil on floors immediately
- Sweep floor areas on a regular basis to remove debris and dust using detergents and clearing tools
- Place scrap containers near where the waste is produced
- Label all waste receptacles (e.g., recyclable glass, plastic, metal, toxic and flammable etc.)
- Empty all waste containers regularly
Take care of personal hygiene
- Use hand wash paste to remove dirty grease
- Use brush and soap to clean the nails and brush also between the nails and the finger to remove dirt under the nail
- Keep shoes and gloves clean: Brush shoes sole and wash gloves regularly
- Keep working clothes clean: wash regularly working clothes, even if they have stains that could not be removed, make them washed regularly
