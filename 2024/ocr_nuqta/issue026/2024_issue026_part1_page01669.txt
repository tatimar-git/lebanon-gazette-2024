The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 6 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 2 – Building wall to wall closet.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
Cut the wood panels | The candidate should be able to read and understand woodworking drawings and cut list and read measurements. The candidate should be able mark lines to measure on the wood board to cut pieces to size and know how to operate a table saw.

Prepare and apply the joining technique for the plinth: simple butt joint The candidate should know how to mark butt jointing places on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to use a nail gun to assemble piece and make sure its square.
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the carcass: biscuit joint The candidate should know how to mark the location of biscuit on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil The candidate should know how to prepare a biscuit joint.
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the drawers: biscuit joint The candidate should know how to mark the location of the biscuit slots on wood pieces using measuring tape, square measure, and a pencil. The candidate should know how to use a biscuit joiner, prepare according to size, and thickness of the board and the biscuit. how to mill biscuit slots and how to dry fit biscuits inside slots to make sure it fits.
Prepare and send for finishing: spray painting The candidate should know how to prepare the wood pieces to get painted by sanding and cleaning the wood surfaces. The candidate should know what type of paint to choose according to wood type, how to choose paint color and communicate with the painter


to send the pieces to be spray painted.
Assemble the pieces The candidate should know how measure and mark drilling holes on the wall and the wood pieces using a pencil, a measure tape, a square and a spirit level before fixing the plinth and assemble carcass pieces together before fixing it to the plinth and the wall as well.
The candidate should know how to assemble drawer's pieces and make sure they are aligned and square.
