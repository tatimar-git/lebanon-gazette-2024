Prepare and apply the joining technique for the bed frame: connectors hardware and screws
Fix the European soft close hinges overlay and half overlay:
Mark the door:

- Mark 5 location for 5 hinges for each door using a pencil, a measure tape, and a spirit level
- Move 10 cm from the bottom up and 10 cm from the top down and 30 cm from the bottom and 30 from the top then center the mark to the center of the door
Drill the Cup Holes:
- To drill the flat-bottomed hole for the cup
- Machine Preparation
Clamp your board to the table.
- Always put a piece of sacrificial wood under your workpiece to avoid drilling into the table.
- Insert the drill bit.
- Remove the chuck key immediately.
- Adjust the table
- Start the drill
- Slowly pull the quill handle until the bit contacts the material
- Apply more pressure as needed
- Pull the bit out of hole periodically to help clear out chips
- Turn off the drill press
Install the Hinge Cups:
- Press the hinge cups into the holes
- Position a rafter square to keep the hinge aligned
- Using a self-centering drill bit, drill pilot holes through the screw holes in the flanges on both sides of the cup
- Drive wood screws into these holes to anchor each cup
Mark the Cabinet:
- Shim the door in place with an even gap all around and measure the gap
- Remove the door, and use a combination square to mark a vertical line on the cabinet's side panel
- Then make a horizontal line from the top and bottom of the opening
Install the Mounting Plate:
- Remove the mounting plate from the hinge
- Align its three screw holes with the intersecting layout lines on the side panel
- Using the self-centering bit, drill pilot holes through the plate's holes, then attach the plate with the screws
- Do the same for each hinge
Mount the Door
- Hold the door in the open position and hook the hinges onto their mounting plates
- Press on the bars to snap them in place against the plates
- Now close the door and check the reveal
