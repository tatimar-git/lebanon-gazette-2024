The candidate is considered competent if s/he demonstrates a best practice performance in all the 5 following criteria for the assessment related to the competence unit 1 – Setting up the working area.

Criteria for assessment | Qualifier | YES | NO (*)
The candidate should wear washed working clothes for personal hygiene and working shoes, googles, earmuffs, safety gloves, mask all the time to

Using PPE and washing clothes prevent personal injuries. The candidate should inspect the availability and the efficiency of the PPE that should be used during the work in less than 10 minutes. The candidate should dispose any defected PPE item. ☐ ☐
Organizing a working space for tools, consumables, and materials The candidate should know how to be prepared prior to start working, s/he should have the right tools and PPE on and should have the understanding to organize and handle tools and materials. ☐ ☐
Identifying hazards working in construction site and taking preventive measures The candidate should have general knowledge about all risks and hazards that could encounter while working, from risk activities to incident classifications and hazards such as injuries from power tools, respiratory issues, falling and tripping. ☐ ☐
Wearing PPE The candidate should deadlift move of heavy objects using the leverage of the legs and not bending the back on any occasion to prevent personal injuries. Heavy objects should be moved using a dolly or trolley. ☐ ☐
Providing first aid in case of personal injury The candidate should inspect the availability of the first aid kit and its content. The candidate should demonstrate how to treat injuries of cut, of eyes, sprains, finger dislocations or fractures. ☐ ☐
