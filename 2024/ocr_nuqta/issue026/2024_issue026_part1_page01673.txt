- Use a measure tape and a level to determine the location of the mounting box: move 5 cm up from the shelf and to the center of the carcass side
- Mark the mounting box location on the side boards with a pencil
- Place the mounting box that will hold the rod next to your pencil mark so that the top of the opening for the rod is centred with your guideline
- Take your pencil and draw in the screw holes on the adjacent side panels
- Drill a starter pilot hole for each screw hole on the left sideboard
- Put the mounting box in position and insert a screw for one of the top holes
- Use a screwdriver to attach the screw, three-quarters of the way
- Tie a string around the base of the screw: you will use this as your guide to position the bracket on the right sideboard
- Run a tape measure from the top screw on the left sideboard to the right sideboard
- Mark a temporary spot on the right sideboard
- Tap a nail into place on the right sideboard
- Run the string from the tcp screw on the left to the nail on the right wall and tighten the string
- Attach a string level to the string and check the level
- Adjust in the nail position until it is level
- Remove the nail and mark the spot with your pencil
- Use this spot as your guide for the top screw hole for the mounting box for the right sideboard
- Determine the screw holes on the wall as done for the left screw
- Drill starter pilot holes
- Attach all screws for the mounting box on the left side using your screwdriver
