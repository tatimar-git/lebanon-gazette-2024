• Mark 2 holes each 17 cm on the front of the face grain of the bottom, moving 2 cm back from the edge
Drill pilot holes inside all the marked holes:
• Place masking tape on the drill bit to avoid going too deep into the wood
• Drill pilot holes into the back frame pieces using a drill bit that is just smaller in diameter than your screws
Drive screws inside the pilot holes to join all the corners:
• Choose the right screwdriver bits according to the size of the screw head
• Fix the driver bit into the screwdriver chuck
• Place the drill bit on the head of the screw to test whether it is the right type
• Select the right torque setting and the right speed: medium speed in order not to split the wood
• Turn on the power source of the cordless screwdriver
- Place coarse thread wood screws into the predrilled pilot holes
- Place the drill bit into the screw head and apply pressure to the back of the tool to push the screw into the hole: keep it at a consistent angle
Prepare biscuit joiner machine:
- Set the depth of the slot cuts according to the size of the biscuit (here we are using #20 biscuit) using the mark knob on the biscuit joiner
- Line up the slot-centring marks on the biscuit joiner with your layout line
Dry assemble to check the fit:
- Insert biscuits inside slots without glue
- Clamp the pieces together lightly to check for any slots that were cut wrong
- Unclamp after checking the fit and adjust if needed
Prepare and apply the joining technique for the drawers: biscuit joint
Mark biscuit slots position:
- Align the two parts to be joined end to end
- Number the parts and draw pencil lines on the face grain where you want biscuits inserted, leaving 15 cm between the marks
- Leave the marks on the face grain of the front and back where they will meet the edge of the side boards: divide 2 biscuits that are 15 cm apart leaving 2.3 cm of margin at both ends
- Transfer the lines from the face grain to the edge grain of the sides where they will meet front and back boards: divide 2 biscuits that are 15 cm apart leaving 2.3 cm of margin at both ends
- Transfer the lines from the face grain to the edge grain of the sides of the drawer where they will meet the bottom board: divide 2 biscuits that are 15 cm apart leaving 8cm of margin at both ends
- Leave the marks on the face grain of the bottom where they will meet the edge of the side boards: divide 2 biscuits that are 15 cm apart leaving 8cm of margin at both ends
- Transfer the lines from the face grain to the edge grain of the front and back boards of the drawer, where they will meet the bottom board: divide 5 biscuits that are 15 cm apart leaving 7.85 cm at both ends
