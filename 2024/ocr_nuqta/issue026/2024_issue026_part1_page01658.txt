Prepare biscuit joiner machine:
- Set the depth of the slot cuts according to the size of the biscuit (here we are using #20 biscuit) using the mark knob on the biscuit joiner
- Line up the slot-centering marks on the biscuit joiner with your layout line
- Cut the slots on the edges:
- Clamp the wood piece you intend to work with to the workbench
- Fold back the fence of the joiner
- Rest the base of the joiner on the bench
- Align the cutting guide with the pencil mark
- Grip the joiner by the handle
- Start up the saw and once the motor is at full speed, smoothly plunge the blade into the stock until you can't push any farther
- Retreat the blade fully and repeat at the next mark
Cut the slots on the sides:
Hold the panel on the workbench with a 90°right angle support
- Fix right angle support against a table fence
- Clamp the wood piece you intend to work with to the support
- Fold back the fence of the joiner
- Rest the base of the joiner on the bench
- Align the cutting guide with the pencil mark
- Grip the joiner by the handle
- Start up the saw and once the motor is at full speed, smoothly plunge the blade into the stock until you can't push any farther
- Retreat the blade fully and repeat at the next mark
Dry assemble the drawer to check the fit:
- Insert biscuits inside slots without glue
- Clamp the pieces together lightly to check for any slots that were cut wrong
- Unclamp after checking the fit and adjust if needed
Prepare and send for finishing: spray painting
