COMPETENCE STANDARD

ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, wardrobe-makers, and related trades workers | Competence Standard
ISCO-08-7522 Wardrobe-makers and related workers | Wardrobes Making
EQF level - 3

A competent person should be able to fabricate wooden carcasses: cutting, fitting, joining, assembling, and finishing prepared wooden parts to make a wardrobe, within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.

Personal Health and Hygiene

CU1 Setting up the working place | • Use PPE to prevent risks of personal injuries • Organize working space and equipment, tools, consumables, and materials • Identify hazards and risks and take preventive measures • Provide first aid in case of personal injury • Interpret technical drawings and blueprints | • • • • •
CU2 Building wall to wall closet | • Cut the wood panels • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the plinth: simple butt joint • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the carcass • Prepare and apply the joining technique for the drawers: biscuit joint • Prepare and send for finishing: spray painting • Assemble the pieces | • • • • • •
CU3 Building a single bed | • Install accessories to carcass: shelf pins, rods socket • Install accessories to drawers: slides, handles • Install accessories to doors: hinges soft close and push to open • Install other accessories: pull downs, lighting | • • • •
CU4 Housekeeping and reporting | • Clean the tools and put back in place • Clean surfaces • Dispose of the waste • Take care of personal hygiene | • • • •
