Double check the length you need for the rods: mark 0.5 cm less than the width of the carcass on both sides of the rods to allow clearance
Cut the rod using a plumber's tubing cutter
Smooth out the rod's edges with a file to avoid injuries: hold file at 45° and go across it until smooth
Insert the rod in the sockets
Measure the drawer:
- Measure the vertical height and horizontal width of the drawer face. Divide each measurement by 2 to locate the center point. Mark the drawer's center with a vertical and horizontal line to form a cross, using a pencil.
Drill the hole:
- Drill through the drawer at the center point, using a drill bit that is the same thickness as the knob's bolt or screw
Push the bolt through the hole and tighten:
- Push the bolt through the drawer from the backside. Hold the bolt stable with your finger. Turn the pull clockwise to tighten it onto the bolt from the face of the drawer. When the knob stops turning, hold the bolt steady, using a screwdriver. Continue to tighten the knob with your fingers until it's snug
Fix side slides:
- First: Install two capacitors inside the cabinet on the two sides of the drawer
- Fix one side of the drawer rail
- Select side-mounted slide hardware
- Choose a slide that is 50 cm to fit the drawer's measurement
- Mark measurements of the first drawers slide using a spirit level and a pencil moving up 5.7 cm from the bottom edge of the side boards
- Mark the measurement of the slides of the second drawer moving up 22 cm from the last measurement
- Repeat the last task with the same measurement between the 2 drawers
- Mark lines and hole using the slide as a guide
- Drill pilot holes in the marked spots
- Drive screws in to fix rails to the side boards
- Transfer the same measurements to the sides of the drawers using a spirit level and pencil: move up 3.5 cm from the bottom edge of the drawers' side
- Dry fit after fixing each rail to make sure the measurements are level and compatible with the rails installed on the carcass
- Check slides for the mark left and right to place on the corresponding sides of the drawer
- Make sure the slides are parallel to the sides of the carcass
- Place drawer into the slide
- Take the drawer and line up the slider mounts on the carcass
- Hold the drawer parallel to the mounted slides on the carcass and level the two sides to insert it into the mount, then angle it up and slide it into position
- Make sure to have enough distance between all drawers (1.3 cm) and on each side of the drawer (1.3 cm)
- Adjust if needed
