Fix hidden LED light on shelf:
- Flip down the shelf and mark a location at the back end of it to determine the rabbet size
- Use a scrap piece of LED light channel to determine the size of the rabbet in the edge of the bottom panel
- Mark 3 spots on the shelf with a pencil and set the depth of the cut to 0.8 cm
- Use a pencil, a square, and a measure tape to trace a horizontal line across the shelf
- Make sure the table saw is unplugged to prepare table saw to perform rabbet
- Make sure the blade is set to a true 90-degree angle to the bed/plate of the tool

- Lower the blade (with the power off) and position it so there's the thickness of a hair between the teeth and the line
- Adjust the width of the fence that you need to cut
- Adjust the blade so it's a bit lower than the rabbit thickness
- Switch the power on
- Make the cut across the drawn line
- Use a push shoe, push stick, or splitter when cutting thin boards
- Let the blade come to a full stop before raising the blade
- Make sure pieces are square and cut at perfect 90-degree angles
- Measure the depth of the rabbit to make sure it's accurate
- Dry fit the LED light strip channel inside the rabbit to make sure it fits perfectly
- Make another pass to adjust the fit if necessary
- Pull back the board and turn off the saw
- Lay down a bead of CA glue inside the rabbit groove
- Install the LED light strip channel: before doing this, you can spray paint it with a color to match the shelf
- Drill a pilot hole in the carcass where the led strips meet the back of the carcass wide enough to just make a way for the LED wire to be connected to the suitable electrical point
- Feed the wire into the hole
- Remove LED strip back adhesive and press it down the surface as you go
- Now cut the acrylic sheet to length and snap into the LED strip channel
