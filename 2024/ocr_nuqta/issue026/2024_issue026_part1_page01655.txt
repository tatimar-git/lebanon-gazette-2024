الجريدة الرسمية - العدد ٢٦ - ٢٠٢٤/٦/٢٧
١٦٥٥
- Read off the locations and dimensions of the plinth from the plan, and mark them on the floor with masking tape to determine where the edges will fall
- Lay plinth components in position
- Dry assemble all boards to level and check fit of the plinth on the floor
Level the top edge of the plinth with a spirit level or laser level and adjust as required: If only small adjustments are needed, you can use packing pieces, such as 4 mm plastic laminate. For bigger gaps or variations in floor levels, you may need to scribe the plinth front board to the contours or slope of the floor, and reshape the underside of the front plinth board with a plane
- Adjust any length or width of the boards if needed and dry fit again: resaw big unfit pieces or add scrap boards extension with glue and nail gun to small pieces
- Remove plinth boards and put on working bench to start preparing for joining
- Sand the edges with fine sanding paper grit 320, to give a rough surface to accept glue later
Mark holes on the face grains of the boards where the screws will go in to fix the boards together:
- Mark 3 nail spots on the face grain, at the end of the board, where they will be joined with the sides: let there be 3 cm between each screw, and move 2 cm from the top and bottom edge of the board
- Mark 3 nail spots on the center face grain of the face board to fix the middle stretcher: let there be 3 cm between the nails starting from the center and moving 2 cm from the top and bottom edge of the board
- Mark centrally 8 nails spots along the depth of the horizontal board of the stretcher to form a T-shape on top of the vertical stretcher: let there be 5 cm between each nail, and move 5 cm from the top and bottom edge of the board
Apply glue to all the edges to be fixed together:
- Apply PVA (Polyvinyl acetate) glue with a silicon brush to the sides of the edge grain of the sides on both ends to receive front and back board
- Clamp the glued corners
- Apply PVA glue with a silicon brush to the sides of the edge grain of the vertical stretcher to be fixed to the front and back board
- Apply PVA glue with a silicon brush to the horizontal stretcher where it meets the vertical stretcher and be fixed together
- Clamp all pieces together and let dry
Insert screws using a 15-gauge finish nailer:
- Make sure the nail gun is unplugged
- Load the nails into the nail gun magazine with the tips pointed forward
- Put the pusher back into the magazine so it touches the bottom of the nail strip
- Place the tip of the gun perpendicular to the MDF surface on the previously marked holes and press it in
- Adjust the air pressure to its desired level, and have angled the gun against the surface; turn off the safety, press the tip into the surface, and squeeze the trigger to insert nails
- Insert screws into all the marked holes
- Turn safety back on once you are finished
- Make sure all comers and boards are fixed together
- Putt triangle wood to all corners to give stability to the plinth
