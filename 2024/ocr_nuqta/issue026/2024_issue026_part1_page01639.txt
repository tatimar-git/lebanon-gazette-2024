ISCO-08-752 Wood treaters, wardrobe-makers, and related trades workers | Occupational Standard Wardrobes Making
ISCO-08-7522 Wardrobe-makers and related workers
EQF level - 3

The occupation covers the process of fabricating wooden boxes and frames: cutting, fitting, joining, assembling, and finishing prepared wooden parts to make a wardrobe within the timeframe assigned while respecting health and safety regulations.

Process | Element | Description
P1 Process to make wardrobes | Output(s) | Wardrobes produced according to design and type of use.
Process | S1: Setting up the working place • S2: Building wall to wall closet • S3: Installing accessories • S4: Housekeeping and reporting
Sub-Process
Activity
Input(s) | Requirements and instructions for building a carcass cabinet to form a closet.
Resources | Technical documentation (execution drawings, material selection, and data sheets) of design. • Tools and machinery for wood cutting, assembling, joining, and finishing. • Materials and consumables used for building the cabinet. • Personal Protective Equipment (heavy-duty working shoes) and first aid kit. • Cleaning kit (garbage bags, broom, basket, etc.).
